How the ideas connect

Steiner’s definition names a path of knowledge. His autobiography explains how he understands its development, while spiritual science names the disciplined investigation he proposes. Learning to follow these accounts is different from claiming to have carried out their investigations.

Explain it yourself

Why is understanding Steiner’s definition different from possessing the spiritual knowledge he describes?

See an optional model answer

The definition tells us the path’s aim. Understanding his account requires following its ideas and distinctions; firsthand investigation, in his view, requires developed capacities. Neither memorizing the definition nor finding it satisfying supplies those capacities.
