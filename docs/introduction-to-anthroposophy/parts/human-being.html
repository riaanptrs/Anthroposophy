The Human Being

What distinctions make Steiner’s account of the human being intelligible?

Move from the broad body/soul/spirit distinction toward differentiated members, soul activities and the developing I.

How the ideas connect

Body, soul and spirit describe broad relationships to the world. The fourfold members distinguish material organization, living form, sentient experience and the I. Thinking, feeling and willing describe activities within that life. Soul development concerns how the I works with these capacities, including an orientation to truth and goodness beyond preference. The descriptions illuminate one human being at different levels.

Explain it yourself

How do the fourfold members differ from thinking, feeling and willing?

See an optional model answer

The members distinguish aspects of human constitution: physical body, etheric body, astral body and I. Thinking, feeling and willing distinguish activities, such as understanding a request, feeling concern and helping. They are not alternative names for the same four members. The I can gather and work with this life; its development does not eliminate feeling or sensation.
