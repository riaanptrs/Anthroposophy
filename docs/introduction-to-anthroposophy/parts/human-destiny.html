Human Destiny

How does Steiner understand an individuality’s development beyond one earthly life?

Ask what continues through earthly lives, how actions have consequences and what changes when physical embodiment ends.

How the ideas connect

Steiner describes spiritual individuality carrying fruits of experience into renewed embodiment. Karma concerns deeds that continue in the person and in the world; post-death purification concerns relinquishing attachment to the preceding bodily life. Retaining development is different from keeping an unchanged personality, body or memory of every episode. These are related claims in his account, not interchangeable names for one process.

Explain it yourself

How do reincarnation, karma and purification answer different questions?

See an optional model answer

Reincarnation concerns the spiritual individuality’s repeated embodiment. Karma concerns the continuing relation of deeds to soul-life and later circumstances. Purification concerns releasing longings tied to the preceding body. In the account, this release does not erase spiritually assimilable fruits of experience. Understanding these distinctions does not identify another person’s supposed previous life or explain a particular misfortune.
