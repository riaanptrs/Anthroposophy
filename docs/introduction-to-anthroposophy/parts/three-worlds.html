The Three Worlds

How do physical, soul and spiritual existence differ and relate?

Distinguish the three worlds and their relationships with one human being, without treating them as separate physical places.

How the ideas connect

Bodily senses encounter physical forms; soul life responds through feeling and affinity; spirit participates through understanding and formative meaning. Steiner connects these relationships with wider, interpenetrating realities, including a spiritual formative origin of sensible things. One person participates in all three during earthly life. The separate death account changes the bodily instrument and attachment while retaining spiritual fruits; it is not the geography of the parallel-world map.

Explain it yourself

Why does the diagram show three parallel relationships around one human being?

The relationships coexist during embodied life: sensory encounter, inward response and spiritual understanding. The fields distinguish kinds of reality in Steiner’s account, not three physical places or merely private viewpoints. He also describes formative realities underlying sensible forms. The separate death panel explains a change in embodiment rather than a route from one box to another.
