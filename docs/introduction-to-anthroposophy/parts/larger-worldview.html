How the ideas connect

Steiner places the development of the human constitution within a spiritual cosmic history. Differentiated beings participate through activities that overlap and develop. Luciferic influence opens independence together with the possibility of error; Ahrimanic influence makes sensory existence appear exhaustive. The Christ event is interpreted as changing humanity’s relationship to spiritual isolation. These connections explain the larger account; they do not turn its claims into conclusions of ordinary astronomy or establish the effects of practical disciplines.

Explain it yourself

How do the cosmic history, differentiated spiritual beings and the Christ event relate in this part?

See an optional model answer

The cosmic history provides a setting for the development of the human constitution. Spiritual beings have different formative activities within it. Luciferic and Ahrimanic influences affect independence and the view into spiritual reality in distinct ways. Steiner places the Christ event at the centre of a change in humanity’s relationship to that reality, including life after death.
