Course explanation

What Steiner is saying

Steiner connects the human constitution with the development of the Earth and cosmos. The physical, etheric and astral members have a history; the I works within them and transforms them. Understanding the human being therefore includes asking about the conditions that made this work possible.

His starting question is how the three bodily members became capable of receiving the I. He describes earlier cosmic states as preparing these members, with the Earth stage allowing the I to work within them and carry the fruits of that work through successive lives.

This history is presented as a result of spiritual investigation. It extends the earlier account of spiritual cognition; it is not a reconstruction derived from ordinary astronomical observations.

Names such as Saturn, Sun and Moon designate earlier evolutionary forms of the Earth in this account. They must be understood in that special sense, rather than simply identified with the astronomical bodies bearing those names today.

Key concept: Cosmic evolution

The linked development of cosmic conditions and the human constitution in Steiner’s spiritual-scientific account.

Course example

A person raises a hand to ask a question. Describing the muscles explains the bodily process; attending to the intention answers a different question. This illustrates the kind of distinction Steiner draws between physical processes and a search for their spiritual background.

Important distinction

Ancient Saturn, Sun and Moon are names for evolutionary states in this account. Reading them as present astronomical bodies would change the meaning of the source.
