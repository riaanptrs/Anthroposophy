Course explanation

What Steiner is saying

Steiner’s cosmological account includes beings with different capacities. The assigned passage describes some that do not reveal themselves in physical bodies, whose activity helps prepare later human development.

Names identify formative tasks. Spirits of Wisdom prepare the reflection of life; Spirits of Motion impart general expressions of sensation; Spirits of Form differentiate these expressions into individual reflected forms. These examples show why the account distinguishes groups.

The groups’ activities overlap. A group is especially evident in the period when its activity takes the leading role, while continuing to act in other periods. The beings themselves also develop through their activity.

Here “hierarchy” is a course term for this ordering of capacities and tasks. Its purpose is to make the different activities intelligible, without turning the lesson into a catalogue of names.

Key concept: Spiritual hierarchy

An ordering of spiritual beings by their capacities and formative activities in Steiner’s cosmological account.

Important distinction

These groups are distinguished by activities that overlap and develop. They do not form a ranking of human worth or a set of completely separate, fixed tasks.
