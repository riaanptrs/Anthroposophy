Sources and reading references

The course identifies the edition and section behind each explanation. Numbered thoughts, printed pages and digital PDF positions remain distinct.

Source passages are separate from course explanations and examples. A Brazilian Portuguese quotation is labelled as a course study translation of the supplied English edition. Editorial introductions and Steiner’s retrospective autobiography retain their own source context.

Anthroposophical Leading Thoughts · GA 26

George and Mary Adams · Rudolf Steiner Press · 1973 / 1985

The supplied EPUB credits George and Mary Adams, first English edition 1973, reprinted 1985. It has no fixed printed page list. References use the numbered Leading Thoughts; the opening lesson reads Thoughts 1–3. The unsigned edition introduction is separate from Steiner’s text.

The Story of My Life · GA 28

Authorized English translation, edited by H. Collison · 1928 · John Roland Penner eText

The supplied file is a digital reflow of the 1928 authorized English edition edited by H. Collison. References name its digital PDF positions, not the original printed pages. The main unfinished narrative reaches 1907, with later retrospective references; it does not supply a complete history of later practical movements.

An Outline of Esoteric Science · GA 13

Catherine E. Creeger · Anthroposophic Press · © 1997

The supplied Creeger translation carries copyright 1997. Its printed page numbers are preserved: add 13 to locate a body page in the PDF. Clopper Almon’s introductory discussion is editorial, while the selected chapters and author preface are Steiner’s.

How to Know Higher Worlds: A Modern Path of Initiation · GA 10

Christopher Bamford · Anthroposophic Press · 1994

The chosen supplied edition is Christopher Bamford’s 1994 translation. Add 9 to a printed body page to locate it in the PDF. A second upload labelled 2003 actually carries a 1944 Metaxa imprint and different digital pagination; it is a comparison witness, not the source of this course’s references.

Theosophy · GA 9

Elizabeth Douglas Shields · 1910 · Delhi Open Books reissue

The constitution, destiny and three-world lessons use Elizabeth Douglas Shields’s 1910 translation. The supplied PDF has 94 captures but no reliable original printed folios. These references are labelled PDF captures, separately from the 1971 Monges/Church edition.

The Philosophy of Freedom · GA 4

Michael Wilson · Rudolf Steiner Press · eighth English edition, 2012

The thinking and freedom lessons use Michael Wilson’s 2012 eighth English edition. The 166 PDF captures do not establish printed pagination. Its philosophical argument and moral intuition remain distinct from the later higher-cognition teaching.

The Four Temperaments · GA 57

B. Kelly, revised by Matthew Barton · Sophia Books / Rudolf Steiner Press · 2012

The 2012 Sophia Books edition credits B. Kelly’s translation, revised by Matthew Barton. The source is Steiner’s Berlin lecture of 4 March 1909, GA 57. Its educational suggestions are supporting historical context; later Waldorf-specific texts are needed for a full programme.

Agriculture Course · GA 327

Catherine Creeger and Malcolm Gardner · Bio-Dynamic Farming and Gardening Association · 1993 · Markdown witness

The working source is Creeger and Gardner’s 1993 Spiritual Foundations for the Renewal of Agriculture, supplied in Markdown. Lecture 2 is cited by its actual Markdown markers. A parallel Adams translation was checked separately. Full native PDF pages and drawings remain unverified.

What Is Biodynamics?

Marcia Merryman Means, editor · SteinerBooks · introduction © 2005; GA 327 selections translated by Creeger and Gardner

This mixed collection distinguishes Steiner’s lectures from Courtney’s introduction and the editor’s summaries. The inspected Agriculture I passage is GA 327 Lecture 4 in the Creeger/Gardner translation; it does not verify Lecture 2 or the full Agriculture PDF.

Colour

John Salter / Pauline Wehrle · Rudolf Steiner Press · translation © 1992

Colour gathers lectures from several cycles. The selected Lecture 4 is GA 286, Dornach, 26 July 1914, translated by Pauline Wehrle. PDF captures are reading-screen positions, not established printed pages; the entire volume is not GA 291.

Toward a Threefold Society · GA 23

Frank Thomas Smith · Rudolf Steiner Publications · 2019 · supplied 1920 preface in Markdown

Only the front matter, Smith’s introduction and Steiner’s 1920 preface are supplied. The lesson uses the preface and cites actual Markdown markers; it does not treat contents-page titles as available chapters or invent PDF/printed pages.

The practical parts will use only supported introductory material and will identify additional specialist sources needed for expansion.
