Watercolour website design

The eight-course site uses warm ivory paper with muted sage, blue-violet, ochre and terracotta. Cool green and violet washes balance warmer clay and golden accents. Saturation stays low over large surfaces; dark green type supplies contrast. The hero places stronger landscape detail away from its text, while lesson pages use pale paper panels. Course colours vary for visual interest; titles and numbers carry all meaning independently of colour.

Artwork: docs/assets/watercolour-path.png, generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Decorative CSS background; no instructional meaning depends on the image.

Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background painting for an anthroposophy study website. Original hand-painted wet-on-wet watercolour on warm ivory cotton paper. A quiet winding ochre path leads through translucent sage green hills and blue-violet distant mountains toward soft golden dawn light; delicate botanical silhouettes on the far right, abundant luminous empty ivory paper across the left half for dark readable website text. Subtle rose and terracotta pigment blooms balance cool sage and muted ultramarine, restrained saturation, visibly organic feathered edges and granulation. Sophisticated art education atmosphere, contemplative and welcoming. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. No text, lettering, people, symbols, logos, border or watermark. Keep left half very pale and uncluttered.
