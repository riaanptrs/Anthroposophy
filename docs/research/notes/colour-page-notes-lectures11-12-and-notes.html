Passage verification

  • IDs 11 and 13: retain “We must learn to paint out of the colour.” (9 words), capture 134, column 1. It follows the icon/Madonna comparison and calls for conscious renewal through weightless colour. A synthesis may apply the call only after reconstructing the source vocabulary and comparing supplementary arguments.
  • ID 12: retain “In the course of the hierarchies entering into evolution the Second Hierarchy appeared in the form of light and the Third Hierarchy in the form of colour.” (27 words), capture 142, columns 2–3. It begins at the bottom of column 2 and continues at the top of column 3; a single-column locator would be misleading. The following sentence gives Moon existence, and the preceding passage gives water as reflection of the hierarchies.

Curriculum consequences

Lecture 11 must teach the complete progression: waking/sleeping/dreaming; qualitative anti-gravity, colour-measure and dependent number; sleep/truth, dream/beauty, waking/goodness; formed chaos; quantitative science's achievement and asserted limits; icons/Cimabue, Giotto/Raphael/Madonna, and conscious renewal out of colour. A small-patch balancing exercise alone would replace its argument.

Lecture 12 must name all three orders of beings and humanity as the Fourth Hierarchy; distinguish manifestation from reflection; preserve Saturn→warmth, Sun→light/air, Moon→colour/water, Earth→life/solid contours; include the rainbow's emergence/fear and return/courage, and explain the clothes-stand criticism of abstraction. These are attributed spiritual claims. Calling them simply an imaginative rainbow or a chemical recipe loses the lecture's intended explanation.

Synthesis must first reconstruct image versus lustre and all seven formulas from the core text; next compare two supplementary lectures, with capture references and actual reasons/examples; only then produce the original portfolio. The draft offers Lecture 9 versus Lecture 11 as a worked comparison, while allowing other pairs from Lectures 4–12. Editorial clarifications are evidence; advertisements and purchase interface are not curriculum.

Source claims, later commentary and original course adaptations remain separately attributed.
