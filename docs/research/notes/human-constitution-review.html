Human constitution reference

Placement: a shared bilingual reference at reference/human-constitution.html, introduced from the home page and Theosophy lessons 0, 3 and 6. Lesson 3 is the primary curricular entry: life, sensation and formative organization. The reference supports all courses without adding a competing course or renumbering lessons.

The natural-worlds extension adds mineral, plant and animal world/kingdom explanations, with cumulative comparison, examples and three explained answers. Sources: GA 9 I §§ 10–12 and GA 13 II § 2 and §§ 9–11. The Adams edition marks § 9 untranslated and includes the plant comparison in § 10; the German has a separate § 9. The comparison is limited to this introductory account, avoiding universal claims about everything Steiner says about plants or animals elsewhere. Kingdom terms link to dedicated anchors, including mentions within the body reference itself.

Source basis

  • GA 13, Chapter II, English Adams edition and German: physical §§ 1–3; etheric §§ 5–10; astral §§ 10–11; I and transformation later in the chapter. The German original was consulted alongside the English reading references. Prose and examples are original rather than extended quotations from the translation.
  • GA 9, Chapter I: distinctions and regroupings; soul body plus sentient soul in the relevant astral grouping. Do not equate whole soul with astral body.
  • GA 13, Chapter III, English and German: the sleep account is attributed to Steiner and the diagram represents relationships, not measured spatial separation.
  • GA 108, §§ 14–18: mental images and the astral interpretation of the exercise.
  • GA 4 VI, §§ 4–6: percept, concept and mental picture. The connection to GA 108 is explicitly a teaching comparison.

Illustration research and decision

Image search for “Rudolf Steiner physical etheric astral body diagram” returned mixed traditions and nested-layer images. Examples examined in the search results include Fourfold Human Being and an energy-body illustration. These were not used as doctrinal sources or copied into the course. Nested shells could imply anatomical positions, and some results introduce a mental body in place of Steiner's I.

Two original bilingual SVG schematics are generated by the reference builder: one person with four related questions, and a waking/sleep relationship comparison. Neither claims to depict clairvoyant appearance. Colours are explanatory choices, not aura colours. Each SVG has an accessible title and description; the comparison is also available in a text table.

Links and preservation

It preserves wording, source quotations, attributes, existing links and input fields. Existing linked text retains its destination; code, SVG text and document metadata are not rewritten. The separate editorial learning-review page is excluded. The reference itself uses its comparison table and jump navigation rather than repeated self-links.

Validation: complete build, existing site/passage/practice checks, plus node scripts/check-constitution.mjs for language destinations, anchors, all eligible mentions, repeated mentions, input/source-link preservation and repeat-build idempotence. Visual review covers desktop and narrow layout.
