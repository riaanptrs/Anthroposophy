Colour: frontmatter and core lecture capture ledger

This ledger records a full reading of Markdown captures 001–044 and visual comparison with every corresponding native PDF image. Capture 045 was also read and inspected to complete Lecture 3; its ledger row belongs to the next section of the review. These are reflowed, three-column PDF captures, not printed book folios. Columns are numbered left to right. A short capture, or a column left empty after a section ends, is not missing book text.

The visible title page calls this Colour: three lectures given in Dornach on 6–8 May 1921, with nine supplementary lectures given on various occasions. Capture 003 explicitly assigns John Salter to lectures 1–3 and Pauline Wehrle to lectures 4–12, and records Translation © Rudolf Steiner Press 1992. That is a translation copyright, not evidence of this file's publication year. The same capture says the first three lectures appeared as Colour in 1982, identifies the German title Das Wesen der Farben, GA 291, and gives ISBN 978 1 85584 275 5. It supplies no separately witnessed year of publication for this supplied edition. No author signature for the Foreword is visible in captures 005–009.

The native boundaries are: Part One divider 014; Lecture 1, 015–023; Lecture 2, 024–033; Lecture 3, 034–045. The orientation reads the Foreword and synopses. Its short Steiner quotation previews Lecture 1, capture 015, column 3, and must not be attributed to the Foreword.
