Existing lesson and passage comparison

The existing lessons provide useful practical comparisons but compress the spiritual arguments and use two activity-based checks in every lesson. The proposed companions restore the historical-cultural arc of Lecture 4; the full moral colour sequence, tone windows and layered advocate account in Lecture 5; the head/cosmos inversion, temporal polarity and spectrum circle in Lecture 6; and gratitude, responsibility, love, sleep/weight, planetary development and the closing truthfulness dispute in Lecture 7. Three source-comprehension checks replace generic activity questions. Ordinary art studies remain explicitly identified as later applications.

All four existing English passages are accurate after normalization of line breaks and line-end hyphenation. Passage 4 is 33 words at capture 051, column 3, and is retained. Passage 5 is 58 words at capture 063, column 3; the draft proposes the opening 17-word clause about the tone as a spiritual window. Passage 6 is 32 words at capture 076, column 3, and is retained. Passage 7 is 42 words at capture 078, column 2; the draft proposes its final 15-word clause, with a note identifying what “this view” refers to. The proposals preserve actual source wording and punctuation; no printed folios are assigned. Brazilian Portuguese excerpts are new study translations, not text from the English source.

No actual shorthand gap is established in captures 045–088. Missing diagrams in OCR, damaged footnote numerals and irregular paragraph segmentation are conversion issues. Lecture 4's unavailable drawing colours are an explicit spoken limitation confirmed by editorial note 22. Editorial notes on gaps elsewhere in the volume must not be generalized to these four lectures.
