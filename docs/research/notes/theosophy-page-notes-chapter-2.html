Theosophy (1971): page-by-page notes for Chapter II

Reviewed 30 September 2026 from the supplied PDF and matching page-marked Markdown. This ledger covers printed pp. 40–69 / PDF pages 68–97. Each row records the page's role in the argument rather than reproducing its text. The full chapter was read; the source is retained privately. Printed page = PDF page − 28.

The chapter moves through three questions: what experience leaves within a person; how Steiner explains spiritual individuality; and how deeds shape destiny. Lessons 7–9 teach these in order. Keep the chapter's strong conclusions alongside its own qualifications on pp. 60 and 65 and in Addendum 7, pp. 186–188. The latter calls the reasoning preparation rather than a proof.

Chapter checkpoint

Explain an experience in three stages: the episode as remembered now; the ability retained from it; and a deed's continuing effects in the world. Then reconstruct the author's separate arguments for repeated embodiment and karma, making their additional premises explicit. Cite pp. 47, 60, 65 and 68 and Addendum 7. A satisfactory explanation distinguishes spiritual individuality from unchanged personality, and destiny from fatalism or a justification for blaming someone who suffers.
