65Part Three divider lists The Sturdy Sapling, The Half Moon-Nodes, The Reversal in the Change of Teeth, and Rudolf Steiner’s Indications for Form Drawing.Structural evidence for four appendices; no substantive passage or instructional diagram on this divider.
66Complete two-column sapling story: sunbeam → rose-red blossom → seed; wind carries it past chasm, lake and burning straw; sleep in the woods; rooted larch; blossoms and seeds renew the cycle.Rootedness changes remembered fear. The ending identifies earth/body, water/blood and wind/breath of God and explicitly submits hardship to God’s will. Teach that religious meaning accurately; a tree-of-supports exercise is a course adaptation, not the story’s whole conclusion.
67Half Moon-Nodes: reversal after nine and one-third years is compared with the ninth-year transition. Middle-column orbital schematic, arrows, node crossings, ecliptic and a stated 5° inclination enlarged for clarity.Author asserts cosmic forces and a pedagogical connection, not merely a poetic resemblance. Distinguish nodal reversal from monthly lunar phases and distinguish astronomy from the developmental inference. Source note at 76 names Joachim Schultz.
68Reversal in teeth formation and claimed bodily assistance from appropriately timed form drawing. Readable middle-column dental chart: ages 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, from 12th year, from 13th year; Arabic permanent teeth versus Roman milk teeth. First-grade vertical symmetry and second-grade cruciform form are visible.OCR tooth sequences are unreliable, but the PDF figure itself is legible. Do not repeat the prior claim of a damaged native chart. Teach grade principles from verified prose: lateral-to-middle in grade one; above/below and right/left harmony in grade two. The claimed strengthening of formative forces remains attributed.
69Third grade: earlier perfect symmetry breaks; forms relate freely to the middle, rather than strict symmetry; cursive may begin. One central three-lobed irregular form with tooth notation below.This is a development of the preceding argument, not a missing section. An original grade-principle table can explain the change without copying the chart or prescribing dental timing.
70Steiner selections: complete an unfinished figure, imagine its reflection in water, then relate changing inner and outer contours. Three native figures: vertical completion axis, object/water reflection, nested three-lobed contours.First selection is note 1, A Modern Art of Education; reflection and later exercises are note 2, Kingdom of Childhood. Flexible, inventive teaching and inward anticipation are essential. Credit Steiner as quoted in Koepke’s translated book; originals not independently checked.
71Continuation: outward projections answered by inward contours; three inner forms and corresponding outer forms; closed forms become open, streaming contours. Final claim: awake form work prepares ether/formative-force and physical bodies through movement during sleep.Native figures are readable. Exact mirror reflection differs from the source’s freer “asymmetrical symmetries.” Final native superscript is 3, whereas OCR gives 2; note 3 at 76 names Kingdom of Childhood, Lecture 4. Sleep/body effects are spiritual claims, not demonstrated by the course diagram.
72Notes: Introduction; Conversation with Peter’s Parents; Dear Parents; opening of Ninth Year in Biography. The parent-conversation note explicitly calls both Chapters 1 and 2 a free representation, with similarities to actual persons coincidental.Essential provenance for Peter and Monica. References include Child’s Changing Consciousness, Human Values in Education, Renewal of Education, Soul Economy, Kingdom of Childhood, Christmas Plays from Oberufer, GA 313 and Morgenstern. Strong teacher-response/whole-future-life assertion is visibly quoted from Kingdom of Childhood.
73Biography references continue: Carossa, Kokoschka, Bruno Walter, Steiner, Heinz Müller and Walter Johannes Stein; Second Seven Years cites Steiner and H. Matthiolius. Comparison notes quote Caroline von Heydebrand’s physics curriculum and Steiner’s breathing/circulation account.Notes identify mediation and historical educational context. Arts-to-physics route includes music/acoustics/larynx and painting/optics while withholding an eye explanation. These references were read here, not independently inspected in their cited editions.
74Breathing/circulation quotation ends. Child in the Ninth Year Transition notes name Balance in Teaching, Child’s Changing Consciousness, Rötges, GA 313, Practical Advice to Teachers, Soul Economy and Kingdom of Childhood.Extended authority passage calls for warmth, sincerity and truth, and makes strong lifelong-security claims. Preserve that strength when explaining the source; a course plan that allows adult correction is an adaptation, not identical historical wording.
75Authority quotation ends; von Heydebrand tone-eurythmy passage; Steiner’s music passage includes gradual preparation and explicitly rejects strictly separated categories. Notes also name Kingdom of Childhood and Walter Holtzapfel’s school-physician chapter. German lexical connections are translator explanations, not direct observation of every child.
