Understand the concept

The lecture moves from the riddle of the individual through a spiritual account of temperament to education and self-education. The closing passage, missing from the capture and checked in the parallel edition, connects understanding with responsive human love. Its fourfold map helps organize the reading, while its recognition of mixtures prevents the portraits from becoming complete descriptions of people. The practical principle is to work with an existing capacity. Your final case should distinguish what is observed, what the source proposes and what you choose to do. Explain one temperament pattern accurately, include a counterexample and design a response that can be revised. Agreement with reincarnation or a claim to diagnose a person is not required to demonstrate understanding.
