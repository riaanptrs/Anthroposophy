2 · Work through the explanation

A worked example

One teammate assumes that Lea’s changing interests mean she possesses only the astral member in Steiner’s account.

All four members belong to the person in Steiner’s account. The pairings concern predominance: I/choleric, astral/sanguine, etheric/phlegmatic and physical/melancholic. Lea’s varied actions do not establish which member predominates or verify the spiritual explanation.

Understand the concept

Steiner describes the human being through physical body, etheric or life body, astral body and the bearer of the I. All four belong to the person in his account. A temperament arises when one predominates in their relationship: I-bearer with choleric, astral with sanguine, etheric with phlegmatic and physical with melancholic. The physical member concerns bodily existence; the etheric concerns life and growth; the astral bears changing sensations, desires and representations; the I is associated with self-awareness. Predominance is not the absence of other members. The lecture also associates these members with blood circulation, nerves and glands. We study those correspondences as part of the text’s spiritual physiology, not as measurements that diagnose temperament.
