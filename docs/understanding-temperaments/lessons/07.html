Understand the concept

The educational principle is to begin with an existing capacity instead of trying to force a missing quality into someone. The source gives four routes: affection and personal interest for the sanguine child; respect for competence and meaningful resistance for the choleric; sympathy with another’s destiny for the melancholic; and companions’ interests for the phlegmatic. These routes make the book’s general principle concrete, but they are proposals within this lecture, not guaranteed outcomes for classified children. All children can need affection, capable guidance, companionship and understanding. Our adaptation treats each route as a possibility to consider in a particular situation, with attention to what the learner actually says and does.

Read the sources together

Rudolf Steiner · The Mystery of Temperaments: Guide the use of a capacity; do not make a label the educational aim.
