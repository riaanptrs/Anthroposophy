A worked example

Lea wants to decide immediately; a teammate wants time alone to consider the options. Each calls the other difficult.

Ask which decisions require discussion now and how much consideration time is possible. Agree on a deadline and a way to share reasons. Childs’s pairings can generate questions about differences, but they cannot decide whether these particular people can cooperate.

Understand the concept

Childs considers the ten unordered pairings of four temperaments, including pairs of the same type. He explicitly describes his sketches as deliberately skewed verbal caricatures and says secondary tendencies, personality, education, health and unexpected responses complicate prediction. A pairing is therefore not the complete relationship even within his own account. The recurring themes are pace, attention, control, privacy and reciprocity. Similarities may ease understanding or reinforce a difficulty; differences may complement or irritate. Our table turns each pairing into an original discussion question, not a success rating. Threats, coercion and violence are not treated as ordinary consequences to endure because of someone’s type.
