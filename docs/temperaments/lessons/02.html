2 · Work through the explanation

A worked example

A reader says, “The sanguine person has an astral body, so the others do not.” The classification has been turned into four different kinds of human being.

The correction is relational: all four members are present, but the source assigns a different emphasis within their combination.

The reading’s key idea

Steiner describes the human being through physical body, etheric or life body, astral body and the bearer of the I. All four belong to the person in his account. A temperament arises when one predominates in their relationship: I-bearer with choleric, astral with sanguine, etheric with phlegmatic and physical with melancholic.

The physical member concerns bodily existence; the etheric concerns life and growth; the astral bears changing sensations, desires and representations; the I is associated with self-awareness. Predominance is not the absence of other members. The lecture also associates these members with blood circulation, nerves and glands. We study those correspondences as part of the text’s spiritual physiology, not as measurements that diagnose temperament.

Words for the reading

How would you correct the fictional reader?

Show a suggested answer

All four members belong to every person in the account; the sanguine pairing names predominance, not sole possession.
