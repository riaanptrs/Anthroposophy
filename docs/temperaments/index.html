Sources and method

Source: The Four Temperaments, Berlin lecture, 4 March 1909 (GA 57). Capture of the 2012 English edition, B. Kelly translation revised by Matthew Barton. Pages and lines are missing; the parallel 1987 edition was consulted. Lessons and examples are original teaching divisions.

Assigned pages refer to the supplied capture, not the parallel edition’s pagination. Gaps are identified in the relevant lessons. Portuguese explanations are original course material.

We study Steiner’s spiritual classification without using it as a personality test, diagnosis or classification by appearance. Understanding the source does not require agreement with all its claims. Practical adaptations are identified as such.
