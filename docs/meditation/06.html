Session 7 / 9

The Foundation Stone: the whole meditation

Read the complete meditation and understand its three soul practices and final appeal.

Why this comes here

This is a later integrating study, after body/soul/spirit, thinking/feeling/willing and the Christian terminology have been introduced.

What the source is teaching

Steiner’s note in the supplied booklet places the spiritual foundation in the hearts and souls of the Society’s members, in connection with the meeting of 25 December 1923. It concerns a way of life proceeding from spiritual understanding, rather than a physical stone. Wilson’s introduction is a later interpretive voice.

The first three movements address the human soul through limbs and space, heart/lung rhythm and time, and the resting head and thought. Their practices are spirit-remembering, spirit-reflection and spirit-beholding in this new translation. The corresponding conditions are soul-depth, balance and quietness of thought. These terms invite more than recalling facts, having an opinion or seeing a vivid picture.

Each human address opens into a cosmic-spiritual response. Father-Spirit, Christ-Will and the Spirit’s world-thoughts belong to the meditation’s Christian and Anthroposophical account. The fourth movement turns toward light that warms hearts and illuminates heads so that what people will create and guide may become good. Its concluding concern is the quality of human deeds.

The booklet contains no verse text in the supplied eight-page capture; Start Now! has interleaved columns in its ending. To avoid publishing a scrambled meditation, the complete text below is a new study translation of the printed German version, with a link to that original. It is not presented as one of the booklet’s published translations.

Meditation text

The Foundation Stone Meditation · complete printed version

Rudolf Steiner, GA 260, printed German version. The English and Portuguese below are new study translations made for this course, not the Adams, Wehrle or Bamford translations. Original line structure retained where practicable.

Human soul! You live in the limbs That carry you through the world of space Into the being of the spirit-ocean: Practise spirit-remembering In the depths of the soul, Where, in the ruling World-Creator-being, Your own I In the I of God Comes into being; And you will truly live In human-world-being. For the Father-Spirit of the heights rules In the depths of the world, bringing being forth: You Spirits of Strength, Let sound from the heights What finds its echo in the depths; This speaks: From the Divine humanity comes into being. The spirits hear this in East, West, North, South: May human beings hear it. Human soul! You live in the beating of heart and lung, Which leads you through the rhythm of time Into the feeling of your own soul-being: Practise spirit-reflection In balance of soul, Where the surging Deeds of world-becoming Unite your own I With the World-I; And you will truly feel In human-soul-working. For the Christ-Will rules in the surrounding world, Bestowing grace on souls in the rhythms of worlds; You Spirits of Light, Let be kindled from the East What takes form through the West; This speaks: In Christ death becomes life. The spirits hear this in East, West, North, South: May human beings hear it. Human soul! You live in the resting head, Which opens to you, from eternal grounds, The thoughts of the world: Practise spirit-beholding In quietness of thought, Where the eternal aims of the gods Give light of world-being To your own I For free willing; And you will truly think In the grounds of the human spirit. For the Spirit's world-thoughts rule In world-being, imploring light: You Spirits of Soul, Let be asked from the depths What is heard in the heights: This speaks: In the Spirit's world-thoughts the soul awakens. The spirits hear this in East, West, North, South: May human beings hear it. At the turning of time The World-Spirit-Light entered The stream of earthly being; The darkness of night Had completed its reign; Light bright as day Shone into human souls; Light That warms The poor shepherds' hearts; Light That enlightens The wise kings' heads. Divine Light, Christ-Sun, Warm Our hearts; Enlighten Our heads; So that good may become What we From our hearts would found, What we From our heads would guide In willing.

How to work with it

Historical directions are identified above. The guided steps below are our teaching arrangement of the selected practice.

  1. Read the full text once, attending to the repeated address “Human soul!” and the transition to the final movement.
  2. Make a three-row map: bodily reference, soul practice, and the living/feeling/thinking relationship. Use the verse itself to check it.
  3. Then read the ending and ask why warmth of heart and clarity of head belong together in willing a good deed.
  4. Use the next session for a manageable way of working with the text over time. Do not turn references to heart and lung into instructions to alter breathing.

Check the connection

Is the final movement an unrelated prayer added to three descriptions?

Read an explanation

It brings the preceding relationships into an appeal for warmed hearts, illuminated heads and good creation and guidance. This links the spiritual account with human willing and communal responsibility.
