Read the book with guidance

Every lesson starts with a selected source passage and explains its meaning and place in the chapter. Read the core ideas, then use the example and questions to check your understanding.

Short excerpts are starting points for close reading. The source credit identifies the edition; the lesson’s reading assignment gives the wider section.

Where this belongs

Begin after the human-being foundations in Theosophy and some Practical Thinking. Read the introductory parts of Higher Worlds alongside it. The Foundation Stone and Rose Cross are later options with their own spiritual context; you do not need to adopt every practice to study the course.

Books and source fidelity

Source distinctions: Rudolf Steiner supplies the meditations and historical instructions. Christopher Bamford edits and introduces Start Now! and translates the Calendar verses; Patsy Scala adds her own reflections in Weekly Meditations; Michael Wilson introduces The Foundation Stone. Explanations, questions and introductory schedules on this website are original teaching material.

  • Start Now! A Book of Soul and Spiritual Exercises — SteinerBooks, 2004. 177 capture pages. An anthology with instructions from several contexts.
  • Weekly Meditations: Rudolf Steiner’s Calendar of the Soul — SteinerBooks, 2008. 58 capture pages. Verses by Steiner; translation by Bamford; reflections by Scala.
  • The Foundation Stone Meditation — Rudolf Steiner Press, 2012. The eight-page supplied capture stops before the verses. The complete text here uses new study translations of the printed German original, not a reconstruction of damaged English lines.
