The reading’s key idea

Pages 4–8 describe a hereditary stream and a spiritual individuality carried through repeated lives. Steiner sees bodily capacities as instruments through which individuality can express itself. He disputes an explanation of genius drawn solely from ancestors. His mineral, plant, animal and historical-science comparisons support the presentation of this proposal; they do not independently demonstrate reincarnation. Reconstruct the argument with its premises visible. The capture’s embryological generalization and account of Redi should be read as historical claims within the lecture, not adopted as current scientific teaching.
