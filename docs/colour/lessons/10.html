2 · Work through the explanation

A worked example

Imagine an abstract study with a dense lower region, an active middle passage and an open luminous top. Without depicting a person, it can still suggest a transition from weight toward radiance.

This invented study isolates one compositional idea from the lecture. It is not a reproduction of Titian or evidence about the spiritual status of the figures.

The reading’s key idea

The lecture of 9 June 1923 uses Titian’s Assumption of Mary, called Ascension of Mary in this edition, to discuss how painting makes a spiritual subject appear through colour. Steiner describes the apostles below in earthly weight, Mary in a mediating upward movement, and a luminous upper region approaching the limits of pictorial form.

The point is not merely to identify the religious figures. It is to ask how the transitions between regions make the work a painting: light, colour and form carry the relation. Steiner places art between an upper limit where form dissolves into wisdom and a lower limit where earthly weight dominates. These are his interpretive categories, not a rule that every composition must have three horizontal bands.

Keep this idea

Ask what the colours and transitions do, not only what the painting depicts.

Words for the reading

Composition: relationships organizing a work. Mediation: an active relation between different regions. Radiance: an appearance of light within the painting.

Check your understanding

Why is the middle region important in Steiner’s reading?

Show a suggested answer

Mary’s region mediates between the earthly weight below and the luminous upper region. It lets the painting hold different qualities together rather than merely stack unrelated subjects.
