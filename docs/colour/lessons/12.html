Explore the chapter’s core ideas

The reading’s key idea

Steiner presents the language he attributes to earlier spiritual knowledge. The First Hierarchy—Seraphim, Cherubim and Thrones—manifests as warmth in Saturn existence. The Second—Kyriotetes, Dynamis and Exusiai—manifests as light; air is its shadow, giving Sun existence. The Third—Archai, Archangels and Angels—brings longing and mediates light and darkness, producing colour; water is colour's reflection or creation, giving Moon existence. Humanity before the Fall is the Fourth Hierarchy: life interpenetrates colour and forms contours and solid crystal, giving Earth existence. The stages name a spiritual evolution and the elements name manifestations and reflections of beings. A list of chemical substances would answer a different question from the one this lecture poses.

The rainbow makes the account concrete. Steiner describes elemental beings emerging in red/yellow and returning around green/blue, fear streaming outward and courage accompanying return. A rotated cross-section pictures this circulation and its densification into water. He relates the Fourth Hierarchy's life to the etheric body, distinguishing life from spiritual existence itself. His concluding clothes-stand analogy says modern abstraction has retained nature's appearances while removing the beings they express; speculation about unknowable things or whirling atoms then mistakes the empty clothing for the person. His proposed remedy is to restore the beings to the picture. The course reconstructs these attributed cosmological and historical claims, including their actual relations, rather than presenting them as independently established medieval history.

Read the elements as expressions of beings

The question behind the historical pictures

Steiner introduces a way of speaking he attributes to spiritual research before modern abstraction. The lecture is a reconstruction through pictures rather than a conventional historical survey; he explicitly says he will not proceed historically. Calling air oxygen and nitrogen tells him no more about its cosmic activity than naming silver and glass tells one about a clock. He wants to recover the beings expressed in elemental appearances. Distinguish that aim from the separate question of whether his account accurately represents particular medieval authors. His criticism of chemical naming prepares the hierarchy sequence; it is not the sequence itself.

Captures 136–137 and 139.

Warmth and light: the first two hierarchies

The First Hierarchy comprises Seraphim, Cherubim and Thrones. Steiner describes enthusiasm, luminous thought and sustaining grace, then pictures Thrones at the centre, Cherubim around them and Seraphim as an outer covering. Their activity manifests as warmth: Saturn existence. The Second Hierarchy comprises Kyriotetes, Dynamis and Exusiai. Its arrival produces light and the densification of warmth into air, named light's shadow: Sun existence. Here manifestation means beings expressing their activity, while shadow names a related elemental result. The Saturn and Sun drawings are monochrome in the capture; colour assignments come from the labels and spoken description, rather than visible coloured ink.

Captures 138–140 and their diagrams.

Colour, water and the rainbow's circulation

The Third Hierarchy—Archai, Archangels and Angels—brings longing. These beings carry light into darkness and darkness into light, calling colour into existence. Water is described as colour's reflection or creation: Moon existence. Steiner develops the idea through the rainbow. Elemental beings emerge around red/yellow and return around green/blue; fear accompanies emergence and courage return. A rotated cross-section pictures a spiritual cylinder acquiring density as water, inhabited by images of the Third Hierarchy. This is the lecture's spiritual explanation of colour's activity. The diagram's arrows and labels help reconstruct that explanation; a learner's ordinary rainbow observation does not independently establish its beings or emotions.

Captures 140–142; editorial notes 57–59, capture 152.

Life gives contours, and the person returns to the clothes

The Fourth Hierarchy is original humanity before the Fall. Life now interpenetrates colour and rounds it into contours, corners and solid crystal: Earth existence. Steiner distinguishes this technical etheric life from the existence of ego and astral body, which he says do not themselves need life. The conclusion compares modern explanations with clothes hung on a dummy after their wearer is removed. Unknowable things and atomistic mechanisms then speculate about appearances emptied of beings. Spiritual science must restore the wearer. His Basilius Valentinus example similarly warns that pictorial embryology becomes nonsense when read as a modern chemical procedure. Both examples concern how an interpretive framework determines what a text or phenomenon seems to say.

Captures 142–145; editorial notes 60–61, capture 152.

Connect the parts of the reading

Why are air, water and solid contours essential to the sequence, rather than incidental additions to warmth, light and colour?

An explained answer: They explain how manifestations of beings become elemental nature: light brings its shadow air, colour its reflection water, and humanity's life gives colour solid contours. The cosmic sequence therefore includes both spiritual activity and its elemental expressions. Omitting these relationships leaves only a list of sensations and loses the explanation that the clothes-stand analogy seeks to restore.

Keep this idea

The sequence warmth, light, colour and life is an account of spiritual beings expressing themselves through nature; its reflections and transformations are essential to its meaning.

Words for the reading

Hierarchy: an order of spiritual beings. Manifestation: a being's expression through warmth, light or colour. Reflection or shadow: a secondary elemental expression, such as air or water. Saturn, Sun, Moon, Earth: cosmic stages in this account. Fourth Hierarchy: original humanity before the Fall. Etheric life: the technical sense of life distinguished from ego and astral existence.

See the idea in an example

A reader summarizes the sequence as warmth, light, colour and living form. Another labels it a recipe for making water from coloured air.

The first preserves the structure of the spiritual narrative. The second changes its terms into a laboratory procedure and misrepresents the kind of account being offered.

How does the rainbow illustrate the lecture's colour cosmology?

Show a suggested answer

Elemental beings emerge in the red/yellow region and return around green/blue. Steiner associates emergence with fear and return with courage, and imagines the circulation as a spiritual cylinder. The rotated cross-section illustrates densification into water, whose beings are images of the Third Hierarchy.

What does the clothes-stand analogy criticize?

Show a suggested answer

It criticizes removing the spiritual beings while keeping the appearances they express, just as one might remove a person and call the clothes on a dummy the person. Steiner treats later speculation about unknowable realities or atomistic mechanisms as further abstraction and asks spiritual science to restore the beings to the account.
