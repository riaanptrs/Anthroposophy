Rudolf Steiner · Course 5

Colour

Observe, experiment and understand: twelve lectures on colour, painting and spiritual interpretation.

Read the book with guidance

Each lesson teaches its central ideas, shows an example and offers questions with explanatory feedback. A selected source passage is included for optional closer reading.

Short excerpts are starting points for close reading. The source credit identifies the edition; the lesson’s reading assignment gives the wider section.

Begin with the orientation →

Orientation, twelve lessons and a final portfolio. Begin with the three core lectures of May 1921, then nine supplementary talks from 1914–1924 in the collection’s order.

The collection we are reading

The supplied PDF and Markdown have 158 captures. The foreword (5–9), synopses (10–13), twelve lectures (15–145), references (146) and editorial notes (147–152) have been checked. The final advertising and interface pages are excluded from study. Numbers identify capture positions, rather than printed pages.

The imprint identifies John Salter for Lectures 1–3 and Pauline Wehrle for Lectures 4–12, with translation copyright 1992 and ISBN 9781855842755. This copy’s publication year is not stated. The notes clarify transcript and shorthand gaps; these remain identified in the lessons.

Sources and method

Source: Colour, Rudolf Steiner Press, translation copyright 1992: John Salter (Lectures 1–3) and Pauline Wehrle (4–12). Text and diagrams checked against the supplied PDF; references identify captures, rather than printed pages. Explanations, activities and Portuguese study translations are original.

The collection contains three GA 291 lectures and nine drawn from other volumes. Locate readings by date and the assigned pages. The public index below helps locate parallel editions; their text and pagination may differ.

We distinguish observation, personal response, artistic proposal and spiritual interpretation. Understanding does not require agreement. Digital studies are original teaching illustrations: screens and materials affect their appearance. Practical activities do not include the treatments proposed in Lecture 8.
