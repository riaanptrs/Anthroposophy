504: nettle — the full-year preparation

Nettle is the classical compost preparation without an animal sheath. Its distinguishing maturation period is a full year underground.

Harvest the suitable above-ground material

  • Identified stinging nettle, classically Urtica dioica, harvested around the beginning of flowering. Use above-ground material, removing roots and coarse unwanted matter. Regional nettle substitutions require a knowledgeable local maker.
  • Protective gloves and clean cutting tools. Dry material, if used, needs the making group’s specified moistening procedure.
  • A retrievable soil-contact arrangement that keeps the nettles identifiable and separates them from surrounding soil; the book describes a peat surround. A labelled marker and separate storage jar complete the record.

Allow the complete year

  1. Harvest and sort the plant, using gloves to avoid stings. Chop or arrange the material so it packs evenly and can later be recovered.
  2. Prepare the marked site and the group’s retrievable container or separation layer. Pack nettle material consistently. No bladder, intestine, skull or mesentery is added.
  3. Bury and record the exact date, location and quantity. Keep surrounding insulation or soil out of the plant material as far as the chosen method allows.
  4. Leave for a whole year, through both summer and winter. Make the next annual batch before relying on the current one being ready; this prevents a gap in supply.
  5. Recover after the annual cycle and assess. The remaining amount may be much smaller than the original plant bulk. Remove outside material and store the dark matured contents as 504; some stems may remain identifiable.

How and when to use the finished preparation

The source assigns nettle a regulating role in decomposition and nitrogen retention. “Intelligent soil” describes the proposed responsiveness to different plants, not human consciousness in the heap.

Use the finished material as one member of the complete 502–507 set in newly assembled manure or compost, following the shared compost-placement guide. Keep the five solids separate until each is placed in its own position. The quantity is a measured small addition, not a layer of raw flowers or bark. Do not give this solid an hour of stirring or spray it directly over vegetables.

Store a reserve separately, cool, dark and moist under the maker’s directions. Record the batch, retrieval date and pile receiving it. The month of manufacture is different from the time of compost application: a finished stored preparation can be used when a suitable new pile is assembled.

Common mistake: substituting liquid nettle feed

Nettle soaked in a barrel to make a liquid plant feed is a different product. It has not passed through 504’s year underground. Do not use the same name, dose or storage record for the two.
