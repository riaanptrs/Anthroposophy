Foodwise glossary

Anthroposophical terms describe Cook’s interpretive framework; botanical and kitchen terms describe structures and processes.

Physical
The material organization in Cook’s fourfold account.
Etheric
The life and formative organization in anthroposophical interpretation; not an ordinary nutrient.
Astral
The sentient dimension in this framework, associated with inner experience.
I
Individual agency in the fourfold account, distinguished from merely selfish behaviour.
Threefold plant
Root, leaf-and-stem, and flower-and-fruit regions used for observation and interpretation.
Humus
Relatively stable transformed organic matter in soil; not the same as mineral particles.
Tillering
Production of additional shoots from the base of a grass plant.
Fermentation
Transformation involving microorganisms; different foods require different controlled processes.
Whole grain
A grain retaining its principal bran, germ and endosperm components.
CSA
Community-supported agriculture: participants support a farm and share some production commitments and risks.
Formative gesture
An interpretive description of patterns of growth, not a measured medical action.
