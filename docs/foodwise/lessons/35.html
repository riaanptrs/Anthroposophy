Foodwise · Lesson 35 · Foods that transform a meal

What travels with coffee, tea and cocoa?

A familiar drink carries processing, trade and social ritual.

Cook’s account · attributed paraphrase

Cook approaches coffee through stories of discovery and the development of a drink culture. The familiar roasted bean is a seed from a fruit; roasting is part of its transformation. Tea begins with leaves, and cocoa with seeds, but the final drink or confection may contain many additional ingredients. This makes a useful distinction between a plant source and a finished product. Cook also gives these foods spiritual and cultural readings. A coffee legend is not a verified discovery date, and an interpretation of concentration is not a promise of benefit for every drinker. The lesson asks what preparation and social setting add to the plant material.
