Foodwise · Lesson 27 · Meet the plant on your plate

What should we notice about nightshades?

A potato is a stem tuber; a tomato is a fruit. Their family relationship does not make them the same food.

Cook’s account · attributed paraphrase

Cook brings potato, tomato, pepper and aubergine into a history of travel, resistance and adoption. The potato’s American origin and European dependence illustrate how a crop becomes entangled with social conditions; a crop failure is not only a botanical event. Her spiritual judgements about individual nightshades are interpretations and are not used here to recommend disease restrictions. Botanically, potatoes are enlarged underground stems rather than roots; tomatoes and peppers are fruits. A family also contains plants unsuitable for eating, so recognizing a family never authorizes foraging. The learner should distinguish cultivated culinary ingredients from the wider family and examine each ingredient’s preparation separately.
