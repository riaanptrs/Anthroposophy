Foodwise · Lesson 17 · Meet the grains

Why are there different forms of wheat?

Bread, pasta and cracked grain use wheat in different ways.

Cook’s account · attributed paraphrase

Cook presents wheat as versatile and gives it a central place in her symbolic grain picture. In the kitchen, however, variety and processing matter. Durum wheat is associated with pasta; grain can be cracked for cooking or milled into flour. Whole grain retains its principal components, while refining removes portions of the outer layers and germ. Flour therefore does not simply mean grain made smaller: its composition depends on the milling process. The practical question is which form suits the dish. Cook’s solar image is an interpretation of wheat’s place among grains, separate from these material differences. Spelt and durum remain forms of wheat and contain gluten.
