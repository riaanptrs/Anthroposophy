Foodwise · Lesson 23 · Meet the grains

How can a grain rhythm remain creative?

A weekly pattern should make variety easier, rather than become another household burden.

Cook’s account · attributed paraphrase

Cook describes a seven-day grain rhythm: rice on Monday, oats on Tuesday, millet on Wednesday, rye on Thursday, barley on Friday, maize on Saturday and wheat on Sunday. She also reports an alternative ordering of oats and barley. More importantly, she acknowledges that several associations are tenuous and warns against empty routine. The teaching is therefore not obedience to a timetable. A pattern can remind a cook to vary ingredients and can give children an anticipated rhythm; judgement still needs to consider availability, preference and dietary needs.
