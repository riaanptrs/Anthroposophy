Foodwise · Lesson 33 · Foods that transform a meal

Why discuss salt alongside soil minerals?

Salt crystals and living soil belong to very different kinds of relationship.

Cook’s account · attributed paraphrase

Cook connects salt with preservation, exchange and political power, including Gandhi’s salt march. She also uses its crystallization and dissolution as interpretive images. In the minerals chapter, attention shifts to relationships in soil: mineral particles, organic matter and organisms do not act as an isolated list of substances. Together the chapters challenge a view of food built only from detached ingredients. Yet a cultural or spiritual meaning does not tell us an appropriate salt intake or supplement dose. This course teaches the distinction between a substance’s material behaviour, its social history and the author’s interpretation. Supplement use and medical needs lie outside this lesson.
