Foodwise · Lesson 32 · Foods that transform a meal

What is the story inside an oil bottle?

An oil can come from a seed or from the fleshy part of a fruit.

Cook’s account · attributed paraphrase

Cook considers plant fats as reserves and gives olive oil a cultural setting in food and light. The kitchen distinction begins with the source: many culinary oils come from seeds, while olive oil comes mainly from fruit flesh. Extraction and refining are further steps with their own effects on flavour and use. Oil also changes a dish materially: it carries aromas, coats surfaces and can be dispersed in an emulsion. Cook’s language of warmth gives these observations an interpretive dimension; it should not replace an understanding of the particular oil or its cooking directions. Store oils according to the label and protect them from unnecessary light and heat.
