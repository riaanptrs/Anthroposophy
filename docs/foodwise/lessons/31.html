Foodwise · Lesson 31 · Foods that transform a meal

How can milk become different foods?

Yoghurt, cheese and butter show different transformations of one starting material.

Cook’s account · attributed paraphrase

Cook treats milk through nurturing, husbandry and culinary transformation. Fermentation changes milk through microbial activity; cheese making involves separating and working the curd; churning cream changes the organization of fat and liquid to produce butter. These are distinct processes, not simply different names for milk. The chapter’s spiritual picture of nourishment belongs to her interpretation, while practical preparation requires current food-safety knowledge. Lactose intolerance and milk-protein allergy are different conditions, and fermentation does not make all dairy suitable for everyone. This course uses pasteurised dairy for any kitchen suggestions and excludes the book’s infant-feeding advice. Understanding a process should lead to informed choices rather than a universal dietary rule.
