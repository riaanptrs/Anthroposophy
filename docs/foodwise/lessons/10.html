Foodwise · Lesson 10 · From living soil to food

How does a biodynamic farm become an organism?

Imagine crops, livestock, compost, water and hedgerows as parts of one working whole. That is the starting image Cook uses.

Cook’s account · attributed paraphrase

Cook places biodynamics alongside organic methods while giving it a distinctive spiritual framework. The farm is understood to have an individuality: its elements should support one another through cycles of nourishment and transformation. Compost from the farm, diversity and attention to rhythm express that ideal. Planetary influences and preparations belong to this anthroposophical explanation; they are not simply alternative names for measured soil nutrients. The organism image asks whether each decision strengthens relationships within the whole farm. Organic certification, whole-food processing and freshness describe other dimensions and should not be collapsed into one judgement of quality.
