The family kitchen

These three recipes are original course activities, not recipes transcribed from Cook. They have not been kitchen-tested. Quantities, yields and times are estimates; use the doneness cues and adjust for your ingredients and equipment. They are not infant-feeding recipes.

Before cooking, wash hands and utensils. Rinse produce under running water. Adults handle knives, hot pans and the oven. Participation is optional.
