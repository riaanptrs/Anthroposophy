Oat porridge with apple

About 2 portions · About 10 minutes preparation + 10–15 minutes cooking

All amounts, times and yields are estimates for an untested activity. This is not a recipe from the book. Wash hands and utensils; rinse produce under running water.

Equipment

Small saucepan, spoon, knife and board

Ingredients · metric estimates

  • 100 g rolled oats
  • 500 ml drinking water
  • 150 g apple, washed, cored and diced
  • 1 g ground cinnamon, optional

Method

  1. Put oats, water and apple in the saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer; stir frequently for about 10–15 minutes. Add a little water if thickening too quickly.
  3. Check that oats and apple are tender; add cinnamon if wanted and cool to a comfortable serving temperature.

How to recognize doneness

Oats should be soft, with no dry centre; apple pieces should yield to a fork. Packet instructions take priority for unusually thick flakes.

Substitutions

Use diced ripe pear instead of apple. Omit cinnamon if disliked.

Allergens and individual needs

Oats: check suitability and certified gluten-free labelling when needed. No milk or nuts are specified.

A child’s optional task

A willing child can wash the apple and measure cold ingredients with an adult; adult handles cutting and heat.

Storage and reheating

Serve freshly made where possible. Refrigerate within 2 hours (within 1 hour above 32°C), at 4°C or below. For this activity, use refrigerated leftovers within 24 hours. Reheat leftovers thoroughly to 74°C. These conservative activity instructions use current FDA handling guidance; see Sources.

Divide leftovers into shallow containers to cool promptly in the refrigerator. Do not wait for a large pot to cool at room temperature. Use a food thermometer when reheating.
