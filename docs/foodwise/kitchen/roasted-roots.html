Roasted carrots and potatoes

About 4 side portions · About 15 minutes preparation + 35–45 minutes cooking

All amounts, times and yields are estimates for an untested activity. This is not a recipe from the book. Wash hands and utensils; rinse produce under running water.

Equipment

Oven, baking tray, knife, board and oven gloves

Ingredients · metric estimates

  • 400 g carrots, washed and trimmed
  • 500 g sound potatoes, washed
  • 25 ml olive oil
  • 2 g rosemary leaves, chopped, optional
  • 2 g salt, optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C conventional or 180°C fan.
  2. Adult cuts vegetables into roughly 2 cm pieces. Toss with oil and optional rosemary and salt.
  3. Spread in a single layer on the tray. Roast 35–45 minutes, turning once halfway through.
  4. Check tenderness; extend cooking in short intervals if needed. Adult removes the hot tray.

How to recognize doneness

The centres yield easily to a fork; edges can brown without becoming burnt. Oven performance varies.

Substitutions

Replace carrots with parsnips, using similar-sized pieces; omit rosemary if disliked.

Allergens and individual needs

No common major allergen is specified; check individual needs and ingredient labels.

A child’s optional task

A child can wash vegetables and tear rosemary leaves away from stems; adult cuts and handles heat.

Storage and reheating

Serve freshly made where possible. Refrigerate within 2 hours (within 1 hour above 32°C), at 4°C or below. For this activity, use refrigerated leftovers within 24 hours. Reheat leftovers thoroughly to 74°C. These conservative activity instructions use current FDA handling guidance; see Sources.

Divide leftovers into shallow containers to cool promptly in the refrigerator. Do not wait for a large pot to cool at room temperature. Use a food thermometer when reheating.
