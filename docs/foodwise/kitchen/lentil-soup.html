Red lentil and carrot soup

About 4 portions · About 15 minutes preparation + 25–35 minutes cooking

All amounts, times and yields are estimates for an untested activity. This is not a recipe from the book. Wash hands and utensils; rinse produce under running water.

Equipment

Saucepan with lid, spoon, sieve, knife and board

Ingredients · metric estimates

  • 200 g split red lentils, rinsed
  • 250 g carrots, washed and diced
  • 120 g onion, peeled and diced
  • 15 ml olive oil
  • 1,000 ml drinking water, plus more if needed
  • 2 g salt, optional; adjust to household needs

Method

  1. Warm oil over medium-low heat. Add onion and stir about 5 minutes without burning.
  2. Add carrots, lentils and water. Bring to a simmer and partially cover.
  3. Simmer 25–35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until carrots are tender and lentils break down. Add water if needed.
  4. Taste and add optional salt; serve at a comfortable temperature.

How to recognize doneness

Carrots yield to a fork and lentils have no hard centre. Extend cooking if needed.

Substitutions

Use diced squash for carrots. Whole lentils require a different cooking time; do not substitute without adjusting.

Allergens and individual needs

No common major allergen is specified; check packaging and individual legume allergies. Avoid adding stock without checking its label.

A child’s optional task

A child can rinse lentils and wash carrots; adult handles chopping and the hot pan.

Storage and reheating

Serve freshly made where possible. Refrigerate within 2 hours (within 1 hour above 32°C), at 4°C or below. For this activity, use refrigerated leftovers within 24 hours. Reheat leftovers thoroughly to 74°C. These conservative activity instructions use current FDA handling guidance; see Sources.

Divide leftovers into shallow containers to cool promptly in the refrigerator. Do not wait for a large pot to cool at room temperature. Use a food thermometer when reheating.
