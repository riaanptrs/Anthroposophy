How to read the teaching

The expanded first readings in lessons 1–8 are checked against the new partial PDF. The retained earlier “Cook’s account” sections remain attributed paraphrases of the previous readable OCR, not direct quotations. “Course interpretation and expansion” connects that account with an original explanation. Activities and recipe cards are original course material; every recipe is untested. Uncertain source material is identified here and is not silently completed. Cook’s views are attributed to her; descriptions she connects with Steiner are not presented as direct Steiner quotations. Spiritual correspondences are taught as interpretations, not demonstrated physiological mechanisms.

Current guidance for practical activities

The course does not adopt the book’s disease-treatment, supplement, fasting or infant-feeding claims as advice. The OCR’s statements about B12 from algae, miso or the colon are excluded as nutrition instructions. A vegan diet needs reliable B12 provision; use qualified current guidance. Botanical clarifications distinguish millet from sorghum, identify wild rice as another grass genus, and identify saffron’s culinary part as stigmas rather than stamens.

  • NHS · The vegan diet

    Reliable B12 provision includes appropriately fortified foods and, where needed, supplements; general guidance does not replace individual care.

  • FDA · Safe Food Handling

    Handwashing, produce washing, prompt chilling, shallow containers and reheating temperatures. The 24-hour leftover limit is a conservative course choice, not the FDA’s general shelf-life rule.

  • NIDDK · Celiac disease: eating, diet & nutrition

    Wheat, barley and rye contain gluten; cross-contact and oat suitability require attention.

  • CDC · Raw milk

    Kitchen suggestions use pasteurised dairy, rather than raw-milk recommendations.

  • CDC · Botulism prevention

    Do not give honey to children younger than 12 months. This course excludes infant-feeding recipes and the book’s infant advice.

  • FDA · Microwave ovens

    The course does not turn Cook’s appliance preferences into claims that correctly used microwaves cause cancer.

  • NIH ODS · Vitamin B12

    Checked 16 October 2026: fortified foods and supplements are identified sources of vitamin B12. This supports the course’s distinction between reliable provision and unverified claims in the book.

  • Soratto et al. · Proso millet / painço

    Terminology check, 16 October 2026: this research article identifies painço as Panicum miliaceum and gives proso millet as its English name. This citation supports terminology, not a fertilization recommendation.

  • Embrapa · Milheto (Pennisetum glaucum)

    The indexed publication record identifies milheto separately. Only the indexed metadata were accessible in this terminology check; the full publication was not reviewed.
