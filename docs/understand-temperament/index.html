Source: Gilbert Childs, Understand Your Temperament!, Rudolf Steiner Press, 2012 edition; copyright 1995. References identify the new complete 97-page PDF capture and its columns, not printed folios. Passages and guides are grounded in the book; examples and activities are original course adaptations.

The new capture contains the continuous text of all nine chapters, both appendices and references. Previously incomplete figures and tables are visible. Earlier gap notices and capture references have been replaced with verified positions in this edition. The external link leads to the publisher’s catalogue, not the full text.
