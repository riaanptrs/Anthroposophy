Course 2 · Rudolf Steiner · GA 10

How to Know Higher Worlds

Attention, self-knowledge and responsibility. A path through the book with explanations, examples and questions to think through.

Read the book with guidance

Every lesson starts with a selected source passage and explains its meaning and place in the chapter. Read the core ideas, then use the example and questions to check your understanding.

Short excerpts are starting points for close reading. The source credit identifies the edition; the lesson’s reading assignment gives the wider section.

Orientation + 18 lessons, including a final synthesis. Work at your own pace; split longer readings across sessions.

About the reading

Based on Rudolf Steiner, GA 10, in the 108-page PDF capture of How to Know Higher Worlds, Dead Authors Society, 2018. Every page was reviewed; the lessons follow the chapters and appendix. The capture does not identify its English translator and contains digitization defects. Thirteen English excerpts were checked against the images; six lessons retain passages from the German original with study translations and their own source references. References identify PDF pages, without an assumed mapping to printed pagination; use chapter titles in another edition. Portuguese quotations are study translations, and explanations are original course material.
