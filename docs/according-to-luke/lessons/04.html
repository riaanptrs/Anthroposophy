GA 114 · Lesson 04 · 1909-09-18

Keep the two infancy narratives distinct

Map a difficult interpretation before judging it.

Start with a situation

Two biographies describe different childhood episodes. One reader assumes they describe one person; another proposes two people. A neat chart can display either account. To decide between them, we need reasons beyond the neatness of the chart: documents, chronology and the assumptions used to join the episodes.

First ask what each text actually says. Then ask which additional explanation makes the differences intelligible, and at what cost in assumptions. An interpretation can remove one difficulty while creating questions elsewhere.

The reading’s key idea

Steiner proposes two Jesus children to reconcile Matthew and Luke: a Solomon-line child associated with Zarathustra, and a Nathan-line child bearing preserved, unfallen Adamic forces. This is his esoteric interpretation, not an explicit statement by the Gospel writers that there were two children.

A map should make an interpretation easier to inspect. It should not make the proposed relationships look like independently established history. His cosmic account belongs to the lecture’s spiritual framework; use the source to examine its premises.

Keep this idea

A clear map makes an interpretation examinable; it does not prove it.

Words for the reading

Genealogy: an account of ancestry. Hypothesis: a proposed explanation whose support must be examined.

Work with the idea

Draw two columns headed Matthew and Luke. Use the lecture to locate one infancy passage in each Gospel in your own Bible. Record the narrative detail separately from Steiner’s proposed explanation. Add a question that your diagram cannot answer.

Check your understanding

Whose explanation is the two-child account?

Show a suggested answer

Steiner’s. Do not attribute it directly to the Gospel writers.

What should the diagram’s caption say?

Show a suggested answer

That it maps a particular interpretation, identifying whose interpretation it is.

How can you improve a diagram without adding more boxes?

Show a suggested answer

Label the relationship precisely and state which reference supports it.

How to assess your response

Check four things: correct attribution, a clear distinction, a reason supported by the reading and recognition of the example’s limits. Revisit anything vague. Reasoned disagreement can demonstrate understanding; agreement and reports of spiritual experiences are not required.
