About the sources

Lessons use original paraphrases based on the book and the reviews of Brian’s lectures. They do not reproduce the transcripts. “Basis” identifies markers in the 147-page study transcription of the Wilson translation, not pagination shared by all editions. Use chapter numbers in your copy. The 1918 additions are identified where relevant.

Portuguese text is original teaching material, not a published translation of the book. Lessons 16–17 also use the supplied Chapter 12 recording review, whose speaker was not identified. That recording omits most of the Darwinism discussion; Lesson 17 directs you to the book.
