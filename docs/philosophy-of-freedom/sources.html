The two editions and their page maps

The two new files have different roles: Wilson (2012) supplies the full book in 173 captures; Amrine’s The Essential Philosophy of Freedom: Third Expanded Edition (2022) supplies an abridgment in 84 captures with six editorial appendices. The abridgment helps compare wording but cannot replace omitted arguments. Earlier course excerpts and notes retain locators from a different Wilson capture with 166 positions. Do not use those numbers in the new 173-capture file.

The Philosophy of Freedom first appeared in German in 1894; Steiner revised it with additions in 1918. Wilson’s 2012 volume is the eighth English edition, not an independently written new book. The 173-capture upload is structurally complete through the philosophical appendix, notes, bibliography and publisher material. Amrine ends with editorial notes at capture 84; its shortened chapters are intentional.

Conversion reports establish extraction coverage, not perfect OCR. The new explanations and cases are original teaching, not quotations or a reproduction of either upload.
