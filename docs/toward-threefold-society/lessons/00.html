Course explanation

What the author means

The title page names Rudolf Steiner as author and Frank Thomas Smith as translator. The imprint identifies Rudolf Steiner Publications and its first printing in October 2019. It also credits Smith with historical footnotes. That publication date belongs to this English edition; it is not the date of every text within it.

There are two voices to follow. Smith’s introduction, signed October 2019, asks why the book remains relevant. Steiner’s preface addresses the fourth German edition in 1920. We can compare their judgments while keeping the century between them visible. A translator’s historical evaluation is different from Steiner’s own explanation of his proposal.

The front matter juxtaposes thinking, feeling and willing with freedom, equality and fraternity. The epigraph stresses three systems cooperating and challenges thinking that chooses convenience over life’s requirements. The preface will develop cultural life, economic life and the sphere of rights as distinct but cooperating members. The title’s brief formulas do not themselves supply that argument.

The contents list later chapters about the social question, social solutions, capitalism and international relations. Their text is absent from this upload. We can study the complete provided introduction and preface, including their promises about the rest of the book, while leaving those promised arguments for a later reading. The 14 page markers are local references in the supplied Markdown.

Key concept

A proposal for three cooperating social spheres must be read with both its authorial voices and its available source boundaries clear.

Course example

In a reading group, label one notebook entry “Smith, 2019” and another “Steiner, 1920.” Put a question about the contents list in a third entry for future reading. This course activity helps prevent a title, a later commentator and an unread chapter from becoming one undifferentiated argument.

Keep this distinction

The epigraph is Steiner’s statement as reproduced in the front matter. Using it here does not mean that the main chapter from which it was selected has been supplied or reviewed.
