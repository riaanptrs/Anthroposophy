Preface to the Fourth German Edition — 1920

Why does Steiner reject the idea that a perfect theoretical solution is enough?

What you will learn

  • Explain cultural autonomy, the proposed role of practicing educators, and its connection with social sensitivity.
  • Explain the recurring social question and the role of communities that continually renew social activity.
  • Explain associations, contracts, price regulation and the preface’s criticism of externally imposed economic planning.
  • Distinguish equal political rights from cultural ability and economic output, and explain the preface’s account of social unity.
