Spirit-self

Spiritual content received and individualized through the I, and described in the transformation account as the spiritualized astral aspect.

Consciousness-soul bears truth independent of preference; spirit-self concerns that same truth incorporated into the person’s independent spiritual being through the I. The difference is the relationship to individuality, not a change from shared truth into subjective opinion.

Steiner describes the I receiving spirit-self and illuminating impulses, desires and passions. The transformed astral aspect may coexist with an untransformed aspect.

Keep this distinction

Spirit-self is not the entire ordinary personality or a new physical organ.
