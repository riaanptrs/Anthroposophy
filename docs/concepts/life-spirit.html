Life-spirit

Also called: Ether-spirit

The spiritual life of the independent spiritual being, described by Steiner as ether-spirit and as the transformed counterpart of the life body.

Steiner compares spiritual nourishment and spiritual life with the way a living physical organization is sustained. Life-spirit belongs to his account of spiritual existence, not merely to the body’s present vitality.

In the developmental presentation, the I receives life-spirit and transforms the life body. This correspondence is distinct from astral transformation into spirit-self and physical spiritualization into spirit-man.

Keep this distinction

Life-spirit and ordinary bodily energy or health are not interchangeable concepts.
