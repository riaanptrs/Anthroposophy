Etheric body

Also called: Life body, Ether-body

Steiner’s independent supersensible life-organizing member, said to shape and sustain a living physical body.

Living organisms maintain and develop a recognizable form while materials change. Steiner proposes the etheric body as the formative reality underlying this life, not merely as the organism’s visible outline.

He says its direct perception requires a spiritual organ. The supplied Note 1 places life-force within spiritual science’s mode of thought and distinguishes it from natural forces. Ordinary observation introduces the phenomenon; it has a different role from this claimed perception.

Keep this distinction

Etheric does not mean electromagnetic energy, the physicist’s ether or a force already measured by biology.
