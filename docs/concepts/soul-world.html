Soul world

Also called: astral world

In Steiner's account, the domain of soul formations whose relations are organized by sympathy and antipathy rather than by physical spatial separation.

Steiner describes attraction, union, separation and self-maintenance as activities intrinsic to soul formations. The seven regions distinguish relations among these activities. They interpenetrate and are not divided by physical walls or distances.

The human soul participates in this world while also receiving influences from body and spirit. Its everyday feelings are therefore an entry point for understanding some terms, but they do not disclose everything Steiner attributes to the soul world.

Keep this distinction

The soul world is not simply the collection of human moods, nor a second physical environment made of thinner matter.

Related concepts and relationships

  • Sympathy and antipathy

    These attracting and separating forces organize the relations of soul formations.

  • Purification

    Steiner places release from bodily-dependent attachment within the soul world.

  • Three worlds

    The soul world interpenetrates the physical world and Spirit-land without sharing their organizing principles.
