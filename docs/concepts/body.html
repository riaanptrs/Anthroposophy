Body

The organization through which a person belongs to and encounters the surrounding world; Steiner also uses body more broadly for what gives a being form.

The initial bodily relationship is sensory access and material belonging. Later, physical, life and sentient bodies distinguish material constitution, living organization and bodily sentience. These refer to related aspects of one embodied being.

When the word is used for a supersensible member, it does not automatically mean another visible organ. The meaning of form or organization must be read within the particular relationship being described.

Keep this distinction

Body is broader than physical body in this vocabulary; not every use names a material object.
