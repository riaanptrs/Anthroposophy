Theosophy: reading commentary

A guided reading of Steiner’s account of the human being, reincarnation, karma, the spiritual worlds and the path of knowledge.

Read the book progressively. Each reading identifies the book section to read, then helps you follow its argument and answer two brief comprehension questions.

Main source: Elizabeth Douglas Shields’s English translation, 1910; Delhi Open Books digital reissue. References are captures of the supplied PDF, rather than printed page numbers.

Source and edition notes

The PDF contains the first- and third-edition prefaces, the introduction, four chapters and nine Notes and Amplifications. The translator’s foreword is her own contribution.

The 1971 edition, translated by Henry B. Monges and revised by Gilbert Church, is a distinct later source. Its thirteen addenda and other revisions keep that attribution in comparison notes.

Teaching divisions do not replace the original headings. Portuguese study translations and examples labelled as course material were prepared for this study.
