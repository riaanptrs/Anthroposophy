Course explanation

What the author means

Steiner distinguishes the ability of a plant to reproduce from its ability to become food or fodder. In his account, the nearer group—Moon, Mercury and Venus—works through lime and related substances in reproduction and growth. Mars, Jupiter and Saturn work through silica in the qualities that make plants nourishing. These are interacting tendencies rather than two separate kinds of plant.

Water and warmth become mediators in this proposed relationship. He connects water with the distribution of lunar forces and makes the example of sowing near a full moon conditional on preceding rainfall. He connects warmth with the activity of distant planets through silica. His account therefore cannot be reduced to a calendar date that acts identically under every local condition.

He contrasts short-lived annual plants with perennials, whose bark and enduring structure he relates to longer planetary periods. Suggestions about planting oaks or conifers and the quality of firewood extend this interpretation into practice. They belong to Steiner’s spiritual explanation, not to an established astronomical mechanism presented by the course.

The lesson’s essential structure is the interplay: reproduction and nourishment; lime and silica; water and warmth; short and long rhythms. Chemical identity, soil measurement, weather and crop requirements remain questions in their own right. Neither elemental percentage figures nor the lecture’s unqualified horsetail percentage supply a reliable modern measurement.

Key concept

Lecture 1 presents a differentiated cosmic–earthly model in which substances and environmental conditions mediate plant tendencies.

Course example

For a hypothetical sowing comparison, name the variety, soil, dates, moisture and weather before naming the proposed timing influence. Distinguish germination, later growth and food quality as different outcomes. A single good harvest cannot tell you which of these relationships caused the result.

Keep this distinction

Steiner’s lunar and planetary correspondences are claims within the source’s spiritual model. The familiar importance of sunlight, water and warmth for plants does not alone validate every specific correspondence.
