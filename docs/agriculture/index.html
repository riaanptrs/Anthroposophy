The source and its digital locators

Primary study text: Rudolf Steiner, Spiritual Foundations for the Renewal of Agriculture, GA 327, translated by Catherine E. Creeger and Malcolm Gardner, edited by Malcolm Gardner; Bio-Dynamic Farming and Gardening Association, 1993. Compared with George Adams’s 1958 classic translation, reissued by Rudolf Steiner Press in 2012. Review uses the supplied OCR Markdown companions; PDF images were unavailable.

The course follows the eight dated lectures and includes their discussions. It distinguishes Steiner’s speech, later reports and editorial commentary. Teaching examples and Portuguese study translations were prepared for this course.
