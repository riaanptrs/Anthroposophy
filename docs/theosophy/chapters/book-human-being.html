The Essential Nature of the Human Being

What distinctions are needed to understand the human being?

What you will learn

  • Explain the flower example through sensory encounter, personal response, and knowledge of enduring relationships.
  • Trace the relationships among bodily organization, sensation, feeling, willing, and thinking in Steiner’s account.
  • Distinguish physical body, ether or life body, soul body, and sentient soul, explaining how the chapter connects them.
  • Explain how thinking serves desire and how orientation toward truth and goodness differentiates the three soul members.
  • Explain the I as the mediator of body, soul, and spirit, and distinguish spirit self, life spirit, and spirit man.
  • Reconstruct the ninefold, grouped sevenfold, fourfold, and transformation-based sevenfold accounts, explaining their connections.
