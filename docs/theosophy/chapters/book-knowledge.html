The Path of Knowledge

What development does Steiner ask of thinking, feeling and willing?

What you will learn

  • Explain why Steiner begins with living thought, and distinguish receptivity from blind faith.
  • Explain the transformation of sympathy, antipathy, pleasure, and pain without confusing it with emotional numbness.
  • Connect disciplined reasoning and action guided by the good within Steiner’s account of spiritual development.
  • Trace the closing progression from transformed feeling to freedom and initiation while preserving individuality and ordinary life.
