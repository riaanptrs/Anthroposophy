The Three Worlds

What relationships distinguish the sensory, soul and spiritual worlds?

What you will learn

  • Explain sympathy, antipathy, and the seven interpenetrating soul regions in the order of the text.
  • Trace purification through the seven soul regions and distinguish it from karma and spiritual assimilation.
  • Distinguish physical form, life, soul, coordination, and creative impulses in the sevenfold spiritual account.
  • Explain the architect analogy and the fruits of relationships, reverence, and service in the first three spiritual regions.
  • Trace the fourth through seventh spiritual regions and explain the qualified relationship between purpose and destiny.
  • Connect the three worlds with form, life, sensation, and thought, including the three elementary kingdoms.
  • Explain the different forms of embodiment the text assigns to human, collective, and elemental beings.
  • Explain the claimed forms and three aura aspects while distinguishing spiritual colour experience from physical seeing.
