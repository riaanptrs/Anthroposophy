One human being, related classifications

A map may show individual streets or group them into districts. A second map may show how the city changes over time. Grouping and development answer different questions, even when two maps have the same number of labels.

Steiner’s ninefold account differentiates three bodily, three soul and three spiritual members. The first sevenfold account joins soul-body with sentient-soul, and consciousness-soul with spirit-self. The first pairing joins bodily mediation with inward sensation; the second joins truth borne in soul life with spiritual content individualized through the I. Two pairs now share entries, giving seven entries while keeping nine distinguishable roles. Grouping does not mean one member has developed into the other.

The fourfold account names physical body, life-body, astral-body and I. The final sevenfold account adds three proposed transformed counterparts through the I: astral organization toward spirit-self, life organization toward life-spirit, and physical organization toward spirit-man. These are parallel relationships, not a chain in which astral body becomes life-body and then physical body. Here transformation, rather than grouping alone, is central.

Rudolf Steiner — Theosophy

4. BODY, SOUL, AND SPIRIT

Read PDF captures 29–31. Begin on capture 29 at the comprehensive reconstruction of the whole human being. Read the ninefold list, both sevenfold arrangements and the fourfold account through the end of Chapter 1 on capture 31.

English translation: Elizabeth Douglas Shields, 1910. Digital reissue: Delhi Open Books; publication year unstated.

What Steiner is saying

The ninefold account differentiates three bodily, three soul and three spiritual members. It names physical-body, ether-body and soul-body; sentient-soul, intellectual-soul and consciousness-soul; spirit-self, life-spirit and spirit-man. These names gather the relationships already developed, rather than introducing nine unrelated occupants of a person.

The first sevenfold account joins two pairs described as unities in earthly life: soul-body with sentient-soul, and consciousness-soul with spirit-self. Joining those pairs preserves their explanatory differences. The intellectual-soul remains separately named in this grouping.

The fourfold account makes the I’s role explicit. Physical body, life-body, astral-body and I form its main terms. Astral-body groups soul-body and sentient-soul; the intellectual and consciousness soul members are considered in their relationship to the I. Steiner’s roots, stalk and flowering image expresses the bodily, soul and spiritual directions of the same being.

The concluding sevenfold account then adds development to the fourfold presentation. Through the I’s reception of spirit, astral-body is transmuted as spirit-self, life-body as life-spirit and physical-body as spirit-man. Steiner says the spiritualized physical aspect requires spiritual perception, although it appears physical to the bodily senses. This final list concerns transformation; the earlier sevenfold list concerned grouping.

Key concept

Astral-body

In the chapter’s fourfold account, Steiner uses astral-body for soul-body and sentient-soul considered together.

How the idea works

  • The I receives spirit-self: astral-body is transmuted
  • The I receives life-spirit: life-body is transmuted
  • The I receives spirit-man: physical-body is transmuted

Course example

An orchestra can be described by individual instruments or by its sections. Changing the grouping does not require a different orchestra. This course example clarifies grouping only; the source’s claim about spiritual transformation requires its own explanation.

An important distinction

The first sevenfold list groups pairs from nine members; the final sevenfold list adds transformed aspects to a fourfold account. Their equal counts do not make their contents identical.

Check your understanding

Why does the first sevenfold arrangement contain two fewer entries than the ninefold one?

Reveal the explanation

Soul-body/sentient-soul and consciousness-soul/spirit-self are the two joined pairs.

Which relationship belongs to the concluding transformation account?

Reveal the explanation

The concluding account relates spirit-self, life-spirit and spirit-man to transformed astral, life and physical organization.

Why this matters

The chapter ends by connecting its terms through organization, inward experience and development. The classifications make sense only when those relationships can be explained.

Connection

The chapter synthesis offers one optional reconstruction of these relationships. Chapter II then asks about the continuity and destiny of the individuality introduced here.
