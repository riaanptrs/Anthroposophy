Thinking, truth and the three soul members

You want to arrive early and devise a clever shortcut. Then you discover that the path is closed. Thinking served your wish; now accurate knowledge asks you to revise the plan.

Steiner distinguishes three related soul activities. Sentient-soul lives sensation and desire. Intellectual-soul organizes experience through thought, including finding means to satisfy a desire. Cleverness alone does not establish the truth of its assumptions.

Consciousness-soul concerns truth and goodness whose significance does not depend on liking. A disappointing correction can still be true. Calling something duty does not remove the need to understand its reason.

These are activities within one person, not grades of human worth. Consciousness-soul does not mean all awareness: ordinary sensation and animal consciousness are acknowledged in the source.

Two questions — optional

Can thinking be organized yet serve a mistaken aim?

See an explanation

Yes. It can connect means efficiently while overlooking a false assumption. The closed path exposes that difference.

Does consciousness-soul require eliminating feeling?

See an explanation

No. Feeling can remain and become responsive to what is understood. Its presence does not determine whether a judgment is true.

English translation: Elizabeth Douglas Shields, 1910. Digital reissue: Delhi Open Books; publication year unstated.

What Steiner is saying

The sentient-soul can receive the help of thinking. A child learns from being burned; transport and communication involve organized thought directed toward needs. Steiner names the differentiated soul activity served by thought intellectual-soul, also using the expression emotional thought-soul. Clever planning can still take its purpose from a desire.

The next movement concerns truth. Delight in the stars is personal, but a relationship understood concerning their movements has a significance other thinkers can examine. Steiner distinguishes truth from the changing conceptions through which people approach it. Correcting an error changes the belief held; it does not make the truth depend on whether the correction is enjoyable.

Goodness supplies a parallel. The value of the good does not arise from an inclination approving it. Steiner describes development in which inclinations become ennobled and freely accord with recognized duty. The movement is toward refining inward life, rather than declaring feeling permanently opposed to moral activity.

Consciousness-soul names the soul’s participation in the enduring true and good. It is not simply another word for being conscious: the source explicitly attributes ordinary consciousness to animals as well. Sentient-soul, intellectual-soul and consciousness-soul differentiate related activities within the same person.

Key concept

Consciousness-soul

Within Steiner’s account, consciousness-soul is the part of soul life in which truth and goodness become alive with a significance independent of personal liking.

How the idea works

  • Sensation and desire become inward experience
  • Thinking can organize their satisfaction
  • Inquiry can examine a criterion beyond preference
  • Consciousness-soul participates in the true and good

Course example

A person can compose a clever reply to win an argument, or examine whether the criticism is justified even when correction is unwelcome. This course example distinguishes serving a wish from examining validity.

An important distinction

Consciousness-soul is more specific than ordinary consciousness. The source does not reserve all awareness for this one soul member.

Check your understanding

A plan successfully satisfying a desire shows which role of thinking described here?

Reveal the explanation

Practical thought can organize a desire without its success establishing the desire’s truth or goodness.

Why is consciousness-soul distinguished from ordinary consciousness?

Reveal the explanation

The source makes ordinary consciousness broader and uses consciousness-soul for a particular spiritual orientation of soul life.

Why this matters

The move toward truth and goodness provides the bridge from personally lived soul experience to the spiritual content the I receives in the following readings.
