Material organization and living form

A wooden model of a tree can keep its shape, but it does not grow new branches. A living tree develops while taking up and releasing materials. Shape alone does not describe that difference.

Steiner calls organized material existence the physical body. He introduces life-force and ether-body to explain the formative activity that, in his account, sustains living organization through change.

He intends a real supersensible member, not a second material organ. Ether here is neither an electromagnetic field nor the ether once proposed in physics. Body can mean what gives form rather than a separately located object.

Growth is observable; the ether-body is his further explanation of it. Understanding the distinction does not make ordinary observation into spiritual perception. Living formation also does not yet explain inward sensation, which the next reading considers.

Two questions — optional

What Steiner is saying

Steiner calls the material organization the physical body. Its substances and forces also occur in the mineral world, but their arrangement serves the human being’s capacities. The physical body is therefore an organized body, not merely a heap of ingredients.

Growth and propagation introduce another question. The materials of a living organism change, while its form develops in continuity with its kind and its predecessors. The quartz and oak comparison differentiates the forces located in a mineral’s composition from the transmitted organization of a living being. Steiner names the latter formative activity life-force.

Ether-body, or life-body, is the name he gives to the supersensible organization he says brings the materials into life and sustains them against dissolution. He presents it as an independent reality available to a developed faculty of perception. The observations of growth and maintained form are the starting point; his further account concerns what he believes those observations require.

Two terminological qualifications prevent confusion. Ether does not designate the hypothetical ether of physics, and body here can mean what gives a being form. These terms identify the organization Steiner is describing. They do not require imagining a second physical substance or a small invisible organ.

Key concept

Ether-body / life-body

Steiner uses these names for the supersensible life-organizing member that, in his account, sustains and shapes a living physical body.

How the idea works

  • Physical materials are organized
  • Living form develops through growth and continuity
  • Steiner attributes this life organization to ether-body

Course example

A growing plant replaces and incorporates materials while maintaining a recognizable developing form. This course example clarifies the phenomenon Steiner wants to explain; observing it does not by itself establish his proposed supersensible organization.

An important distinction

Life organization is distinct from inward sensation. At this stage the chapter has not explained what must be added for the experience of color or pleasure.

Reveal the explanation

Why this matters

The distinction allows Steiner to discuss living organization separately from material composition before introducing the basis of sentient experience.
