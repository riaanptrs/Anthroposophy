Lesson 03 · The essential nature of the human being

Life, sensation, and formative organization

Distinguish physical body, ether or life body, soul body, and sentient soul, explaining how the chapter connects them.

Which relationship distinguishes ether body, soul body and sentient soul?

1 · Read the passage

Begin with the book

Rudolf Steiner · Theosophy · GA 9 · Monges / Church, 1971 · Printed p. 19 · PDF p. 47

Since the ether body is the life within the physical body, the sentient soul is also directly dependent on the physical body.

Anthroposophic Press, 1971; Henry B. Monges, translator; Gilbert Church, reviser. English excerpt checked against the supplied PDF image; Brazilian Portuguese is a new course study translation. Line breaks and line-end hyphenation normalized.

Open the source or edition index →

What this passage means

The sentence gives a chain of dependence: formative life works in the physical organization, and sentient experience depends on that living bodily basis. The following example connects the experience of color with functioning eyes. Distinguish interdependence from identity: the chapter does not use physical body, ether body and sentient soul as three names for the same function.

The focus of this lesson

Distinguish physical body, ether or life body, soul body, and sentient soul, explaining how the chapter connects them.

Explore the chapter’s core ideas

Understand the idea

Steiner first defends thinking as a path that can deepen feeling: persevering thought about spiritual questions can awaken warmth, beauty, and enthusiasm. He then names the material organization the physical body. Its substances also occur in the mineral world, while a living being maintains and develops a form through growth and successive generations. The oak and the quartz crystal introduce this difference in organization.

The ether or life body names the supersensible formative organization Steiner attributes to living beings. It gives living form and sustains the physical organization during life. On pages 14–16 he presents it as a real object of spiritual perception, approached through an organ or faculty that must develop. The comparison with sight explains the kind of access he claims; observing growth supplies the starting phenomenon rather than a demonstration of that faculty.

Read the long footnote on pages 14–15 before treating life-force as an extra chemical force. Steiner says inorganic laws act the same way inside an organism as outside it. The formative-force body concerns the living organization in which those processes occur. Ether here is also different from the hypothetical ether of physics. Addendum 1 reinforces his proposed change of mode of knowledge; Addendum 2 qualifies touch as a general awareness of an impression in this context.

Life and sensation are then distinguished. Physical and living processes in an eye and brain do not describe the inward experience of blue in the same terms. The sentient soul is the activity through which such impressions become inner experiences. It also embraces desire, aversion, impulse, instinct, and passion. In Steiner’s scheme plants participate in formative life, while animals and humans also have a world of sensation.

The soul body or sentient body, introduced on page 20, mediates bodily organization and sentient soul. Steiner describes a finer part of the etheric organization as united with the sentient soul and a coarser part as united with the physical body. Distinguish the bodily organization supporting sensation from the soul’s experience; later he will unite soul body and sentient soul under the name astral body.

The sentient soul depends on the living body and the functioning of the senses. Experiencing one’s own sensation is different from perceiving its expression in another being: the seer described on pages 18–19 does not simply feel the other’s feeling from the other’s standpoint. These qualifications explain relationships among the members before the next lesson differentiates soul life itself.

Keep this idea

Keep the observable growth separate from Steiner’s explanation of what organizes it.

See the idea in an example

Place a stone beside a growing bean plant. Both have material form. The plant also develops leaves and maintains a living organization.

What this helps explain

Steiner calls the supersensible formative organization of a living being its ether or life body. “Supersensible” means beyond ordinary sensory perception in his account. The growing plant illustrates what he wants to explain; it does not make the proposed life body visible.

Check your understanding

Which relationship distinguishes ether body, soul body and sentient soul?

Reveal the explanation

The ether body provides formative life in the bodily organization. The soul body is the sentient organization joining that bodily basis to soul life. The sentient soul inwardly experiences the blue and responds through feelings or desires. Life-process, support for sensation, and experienced sensation are distinguishable but connected. Steiner later groups soul body and sentient soul as astral body; this does not make either identical with the ether body.

Why does grouping soul body and sentient soul as astral body not make them ether body?

Pause and answer in one sentence, aloud or in your notes.

See a way to approach it

The grouping joins the bodily support of sensation with its inward soul side. Ether body names formative life, a different relationship in the explanation. Grouping two aspects does not erase that distinction.
