GA 327 · 1924

Biodynamic Agriculture

Soil, Farm, Cosmos and the Living Organism

A guided study of Rudolf Steiner’s 1924 Agriculture Course and the foundations of biodynamic farming.

Explore Steiner’s conception of the farm as a living individuality, the relationships among soil, plants and animals, the preparations, cosmic rhythms, and how these ideas relate to contemporary agriculture.
