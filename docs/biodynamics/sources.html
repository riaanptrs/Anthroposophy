Canonical course edition

Rudolf Steiner · Spiritual Foundations for the Renewal of Agriculture · GA 327 · translated by Catherine E. Creeger and Malcolm Gardner · edited by Malcolm Gardner · Bio-Dynamic Farming and Gardening Association, Inc. · 1993.

The supplied Markdown records SteinerBooks branding and 1993 title/copyright information. The course uses this captured Creeger/Gardner text as its canonical source.

Locators and verification

This course is based on the supplied Markdown editions. Quotations are verified within their assigned captures; capture numbers identify source positions, not printed book pages. All course figures are original teaching diagrams based on the text. The historical book PDFs and their drawings have not been inspected.

The canonical witness contains 192 supplied Markdown captures; Adams contains 178. These are separate digital capture sequences, not matching printed page numbers. All eight lectures and four discussions are present. The 1993 export ends during its bibliography, before the remaining bibliography, index and colour plates. Its diagrams and handwritten material cannot be authenticated through OCR.

Portuguese passages are course study translations of the canonical English excerpts. Original explanation diagrams are labelled separately from historical drawings. The six parts and 24 lessons are a course arrangement, not Steiner’s lecture divisions.
