Sources and method

Source: Hermann Koepke, Encountering the Self: Transformation & Destiny in the Ninth Year. Jesse Darrell is the credited volume translator; the imprint gives Anthroposophic Press English edition particulars/copyright 1989, ISBN 9780880102797. The date of this electronic edition is unstated. Walter Holtzapfel wrote the school-doctor contribution. Steiner selections are identified as quotations mediated by this book. References identify positions in the supplied 76-capture PDF, rather than printed folios. Portuguese passages are new study translations; explanations, examples and activities are original course material.

The new copy has the previously interrupted passages and readable figures. Lessons follow the contents order, check selections against the PDF and distinguish the author’s proposals from adapted activities. The external link leads to the publisher’s catalogue, not the full text.

The parent conversations are constructed examples, as the book’s notes explain. We distinguish observation, anthroposophical interpretation and educational adaptation. Age is not the same as school grade, and historical medical claims are not treatment instructions.
