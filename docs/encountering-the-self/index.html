Sources and method

Source: Hermann Koepke, Encountering the Self: Transformation & Destiny in the Ninth Year, translated by Jesse Darrell, 1989. Medical contribution by Walter Holtzapfel. Page assignments refer to the supplied incomplete capture. Explanations, examples and activities are original course material.

The capture has blank pages, interrupted passages and incomplete diagrams. Lessons follow the contents order using the available text; gaps have not been reconstructed. The external link leads to the publisher’s catalogue, not the full text.

The parent conversations are constructed examples, as the book’s notes explain. We distinguish observation, anthroposophical interpretation and educational adaptation. Age is not the same as school grade, and historical medical claims are not treatment instructions.
