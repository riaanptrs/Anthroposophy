Natural science: observation and relationship

Connect careful description with explanations of living systems.

Understand the idea

The supplied natural-science pathway moves through animals, plants, minerals and wider relationships. Observation should build accurate distinctions before an explanation is proposed.

Compare structure, function, growth and surroundings using clear criteria. Later models can explain relationships that are not directly visible. Pupils need to know which statements describe observations and which depend on an explanatory model.

Traditional anthroposophical interpretations and current biological explanations have different sources and evidential roles. Keep them explicit. Use actual observations, suitable references and opportunities to question a proposed relationship.

A worked example

Pupils compare leaves from two settings, record observable differences and discuss what further information would be needed to explain them.

Check and apply your understanding

Why should observation and explanation be recorded separately?

Pause and answer in one sentence, aloud or in your notes.

See a way to approach it

An observed feature can fit more than one explanation. Separating them makes assumptions visible and helps identify what evidence is needed to choose among explanations.
