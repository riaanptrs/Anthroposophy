Subject pathways

Each lesson explains a central relationship, works through an example and provides a question with an explained answer.

  1. Arts across the curriculum

    Trace the move from making and noticing to interpreting artistic choices.

  2. Chemistry: transformation and explanation

    Distinguish observation of a change from its chemical explanation.

  3. Eurythmy and movement across the years

    Connect expressive movement with increasingly conscious coordination.

  4. Foreign languages across the years

    Trace the growth from contextual speech to flexible, explicit understanding.

  5. Geography: relationships across scales

    Connect observation, mapping and explanation of places.

  6. Handwork: from sequence to design

    Explain how practical work develops planning and revision.

  7. History: story, context and evidence

    Distinguish an engaging narrative from a supported historical explanation.

  8. Language: from speech to interpretation

    Connect sound, literacy, structure and meaningful expression.

  9. Mathematics: from quantity to relationship

    Explain the continuity from concrete examples to symbolic reasoning.

  10. Music: listening, structure and expression

    Explain how musical experience becomes conscious interpretation.

  11. Natural science: observation and relationship

    Connect careful description with explanations of living systems.

  12. Physics: phenomenon, pattern and mechanism

    Develop an explanation from controlled comparison.

  13. Technology: tools, systems and judgment

    Understand how a tool works and assess the choices around its use.

Understand the idea. Explain the relationship. Apply what you learned.
