Mathematics: from quantity to relationship

Explain the continuity from concrete examples to symbolic reasoning.

Understand the idea

Mathematics begins with relationships among quantities, patterns and operations. Objects, movement and drawings can make them visible; symbols allow the relationship to be expressed efficiently.

The supplied progression extends through fractions, proportion, geometry and algebraic reasoning. Each step needs connections with earlier meanings. A rule is easier to use thoughtfully when pupils can explain why it applies.

Fluency and understanding support one another but are different checks. Include estimation, changed examples, explanation of errors and transfer to unfamiliar situations. An attractive diagram or fast answer alone can conceal a misconception.

A worked example

Pupils represent equal fractions, explain equivalence and use it to compare amounts without relying on the same original picture.

See a way to approach it

Pupils retain the relationship the symbols express and can connect it to an example, justify a step and use it in a changed situation. Symbol manipulation alone does not establish that understanding.
