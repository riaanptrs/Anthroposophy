1. Developmental picture

Grade 8 usually sits around ages thirteen to fourteen.

Contemporary Waldorf science describes this as a profound reorganization of bodily, psychological and spiritual life and the effective end of childhood. Waldorf Resources

This makes Grade 8 a year of integration.

The child has travelled through eight years of class-teacher curriculum and is approaching high school.
