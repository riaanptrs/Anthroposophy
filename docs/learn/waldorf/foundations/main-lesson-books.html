Main lesson books and textbooks

Distinguish constructing a learning record from copying one.

Understand the idea

A main lesson book records developing understanding through writing, diagrams, drawings and worked examples. Making the record can require pupils to select important ideas and explain their relationships.

Copying a teacher’s page may help practise a skill, but it does not automatically show comprehension. Include opportunities for pupils to summarize, solve a changed problem and correct their own explanation.

Textbooks and other references can provide independent explanations, practice and accurate information. A pupil-created book and a published reference have different roles. Accessible formats, accurate corrections and manageable presentation demands matter more than uniform decoration.

A worked example

After studying a plant, pupils label an observed drawing and write two explanatory sentences. They compare a reference picture, correct a mistaken label and add their own question.
