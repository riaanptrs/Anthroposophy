Development

Each lesson explains a central relationship, works through an example and provides a question with an explained answer.

  1. School readiness and the change of teeth

    Distinguish a developmental symbol from an individual readiness assessment.

  2. Ages seven to nine

    Explain how imaginative experience develops into explicit skills.

  3. The nine-year change

    Connect an interpreted transition with concrete reassurance and learning.

  4. Ages nine to twelve

    Explain the movement toward differentiation and organized observation.

  5. Around twelve: explaining causes

    Distinguish an observed pattern from a causal explanation.

  6. Puberty and changing authority

    Explain the shift toward reasons, dialogue and responsibility.

  7. Adolescence and independent judgment

    Build a reasoned conclusion that can be revised.

Understand the idea. Explain the relationship. Apply what you learned.
