Later Waldorf curriculum Provisional category; exact source verification pending. The lesson names physics, mineralogy, historical causality, formal geometry, and business mathematics.

The Grade 6 outline specifies:

Educational movement

Interpretation The established grade outline describes geometry as a movement from beautiful form → lawful construction. The broader course sequence places causality after pictorial learning and observation, and before independent judgment.

These are curriculum explanations from the course plan; the supplied outline does not establish an experimentally measured age at which every child becomes capable of causal thinking.
