Why one teacher for several years?

Understand continuity together with accountability and expertise.

Understand the idea

A continuing class teacher can know pupils over time and connect earlier learning with new tasks. The relationship may support trust, consistent routines and recognition of change.

Continuity also requires reassessment. A pupil should not remain defined by an old difficulty. Teachers need subject knowledge, colleagues, specialist support and communication with families.

Ask how concerns are handled, how teaching quality is reviewed and how pupils receive expertise beyond the class teacher. A lasting relationship has educational value when it continues to meet the learner’s needs.

A worked example

A previously hesitant pupil now explains a topic confidently. The teacher revises expectations and provides a more demanding role.
