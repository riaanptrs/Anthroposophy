ADHD, Dyslexia and Learning Differences

This section must be especially careful.

Do not imply that Waldorf pedagogy treats or cures medical or neurodevelopmental conditions.

Discuss:

  • movement
  • rhythm
  • multisensory instruction
  • individual observation
  • possible benefits

but state clearly that appropriate professional assessment and intervention may still be necessary.

ADHD, Dyslexia, and Learning Differences
