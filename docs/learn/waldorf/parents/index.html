Parent questions

Each lesson explains a central relationship, works through an example and provides a question with an explained answer.

  1. What if my child is academically advanced?

    Distinguish deeper challenge from simply accelerating every subject.

  2. Why so much art?

    Identify the learning relationship inside an artistic activity.

  3. What about tests and grades?

    Ask for specific evidence, clear criteria and a response plan.

  4. Why one teacher for several years?

    Understand continuity together with accountability and expertise.

  5. Preparation for high school and university

    Connect educational intentions with actual transition requirements.

  6. Home life: a practical parent guide

    Create supportive routines without reproducing the whole school at home.

  7. ADHD, dyslexia and learning differences

    Distinguish adapting teaching from explaining away a difficulty.

  8. Does Waldorf delay reading?

    Separate a typical teaching sequence from individual literacy needs.

  9. Is Waldorf religious?

    Distinguish philosophical background, cultural stories and religious practice.

  10. Is Waldorf anti-science?

    Ask how observation, explanation and evidence are taught.

  11. Why so many stories?

    Connect narrative engagement with language and conceptual learning.

  12. What if my child is struggling?

    Turn a general concern into a specific, reviewed support plan.

  13. Is Waldorf anti-technology?

    Evaluate a policy through learning purpose, access and preparation.

  14. Why fewer textbooks?

    Compare the roles of pupil-created records and independent references.

Understand the idea. Explain the relationship. Apply what you learned.
