How to learn in this course

Choose a topic or follow the main sequence. Read the explanation, work through the example and answer in your own words before revealing the model. Revisit any relationship you cannot yet explain.

Understanding the educational approach does not require accepting every philosophical claim. A supported disagreement can show understanding. Reading marks are optional, local to this browser and shared between the two language versions.

These lessons explain the Waldorf/Anthroposophical developmental framework. Ages are approximate; individual pupils, cultures and schools vary. Educational intentions are not evidence of demonstrated outcomes.
