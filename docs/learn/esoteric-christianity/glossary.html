People and concept glossary

Definitions describe this course’s use. They do not replace other traditions’ definitions.

David, Solomon and Nathan
David is the common genealogical landmark. Solomon names Matthew’s line and Nathan Luke’s; Nathan here is David’s son, not automatically the prophet of the same name.
Sarah, Hagar, Isaac and Ishmael
Figures in the Abraham narratives. Both Isaac and Ishmael receive promises; the preparation interpretation must not become an ethic of excluding people.
Rebekah, Esau and Jacob / Israel
The blessing narrative shows cleverness and deception; Jacob/Israel’s sons frame the tribes. Hair symbolism is source interpretation, never a measure of current intelligence.
Joseph in Genesis
Jacob’s son, dreamer and administrator in Egypt; distinct from either father named Joseph in the childhood account. Pharaoh, not Joseph, has the cow dream.
Melchizedek
Bread-and-wine encounter in Genesis 14; the lecturer interprets a solar initiatory role. God’s renaming of Abram is a separate Genesis 17 event.
Zechariah, Elizabeth and Gabriel
John’s parents and the annunciation messenger in Luke 1; the visitation brings Elizabeth and Mary into relationship.
Anna
Prophetess who gives thanks and speaks of the infant in Luke 2; distinct from Simeon and retained in the teaching.
Magi and shepherds
Visitors in Matthew and Luke respectively. The Foundation Stone image connects insight and warmth without ranking the visitors.
Herod and Archelaus
Threat and succession in Matthew’s infancy narrative; the family’s return leads to Nazareth.
Yahweh / Jehovah
Elohim who remains related to Earth and later the Moon in the lecturer’s cosmology; outward observation in Abraham’s preparation is connected to this being.
Christ
Cosmic spiritual being who enters the prepared bearer at the Jordan in this account.
Jesus
The human bearer and preparation; specify Solomon or Nathan when discussing childhood.
I
Spiritual individuality and responsible initiative; not merely selfishness or the eye.
Physical body
Organization of bodily form and sense embodiment.
Etheric body
Life, growth and formative continuity in the spiritual vocabulary.
Astral body
Organization of conscious sensation, desire and feeling.
Nathan Jesus
Luke stream, named through Nathan’s line from David; preserved innocence in the explanation.
Solomon Jesus
Matthew stream, named through Solomon’s line; Zarathustra’s individuality in the account.
Zarathustra / Zoroaster
Individuality of acquired wisdom; Zarathas and Nazaratos are variant identities in the lecturer’s spiritual account.
Heavenly Adam / sister soul
Preserved aspect of humanity associated with the Nathan child; not biological siblinghood.
Fall
The lecturer interprets embodied separateness as a condition for the risks and possibilities of freedom.
John the Baptist
Forerunner and baptizer; the lecturer relates an earthly Adam–Elijah–John line to the preserved counterpart.
Buddha
Compassion and spiritual radiance related to the Nathan child in this anthroposophical reading.
Asita / Simeon
Steiner’s relationship across lives recorded in the supplied GA 114 passage check; Anna is a separate witness in Luke.
Bodhisattva
A spiritual teacher preparing a capacity in the lecturer’s usage; not a definition of every Buddhist tradition.
Krishna
Connected with the Nathan being in the closing synthesis; manifestation and ordinary incarnation need distinction.
Hermes / Thoth
Greek/Egyptian names associated with the recipient of Zarathustra’s astral gift.
Moses
Related to the etheric gift, Egyptian preparation and Exodus in the lecture.
Abraham
Bearer of sense observation and constructive judgment in the spiritual-history account; God renames Abram in Genesis 17.
The two Marys
Use the household key. The heavenly/earthly-Eve identities are lecturer interpretations, not established course facts.
Essene
Community discipline in the lecturer’s preparation narrative; the precise Nazareth founding tradition is esoteric.
Jeshu ben Pandira / Netzer
Teacher and pupil in the lecturer’s account of Nazareth; not asserted as independently established archaeology.
Elohim / Exusiai
Spirits of Form in the lecture’s hierarchy. Yahweh is distinguished from the solar mediation associated with Michael.
Michael
The lecturer’s solar mediator identification; he notes the name is not explicit in the Matthew passage discussed.
Lucifer / Ahriman
Spiritual poles of self-exalting independence and hardening control; their gifts can serve a human purpose.
Recapitulation
An earlier pattern taken up at a later scale under changed conditions.
Spirit-self / Manas
Higher member distinguished from ordinary earthly personality; life-spirit/Buddhi and spirit-human/Atman are further named members.
Grail / vessel
An image of prepared capacity offered to a spiritual task; this course does not supply a full Grail history.
Phantom
Further-study term for spiritual formative reality associated with the physical body and resurrection in GA 131.
Master Jesus
Closing spiritual-inheritance lead associated with Zarathustra; particular recipients need further primary-source checks.
