Esoteric Christianity I: Preparing for the Coming of Christ

The Gospel accounts, the two Jesus children, and the path to the Baptism in the Jordan.

How, in the anthroposophical account presented by these lectures, was a human being prepared to receive Christ? Follow the distinct Matthew and Luke preparations, their Temple convergence, and the Jordan Baptism.

For adults and older teenagers, including newcomers. No compulsory prerequisite, written submission or devotional practice.

Allow about 4 hours: reading at a reflective pace, two or three checks per lesson and an optional short exercise. Deeper source reading adds time.
