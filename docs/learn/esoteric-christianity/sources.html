Books and lecture sources

The course is based on a sequential reading of all three supplied Intro to Esoteric Christianity transcripts. P1, P2 and P3 are their source IDs. Capture dates do not establish original lecture dates. The speaker is referred to as “the lecturer”; original recording credits remain to be confirmed.

All works below are named or alluded to in the lectures. The supplied knowledge base records bibliographic checks and three targeted primary-passage checks. Those are narrower than reading an entire work. Archive retrieval during this implementation returned HTTP 403, so the primary checks are attributed to that dossier, not claimed as newly completed. Links are reading destinations, not recording URLs.
