Books and lecture sources

The course is based on a sequential reading of all three supplied Intro to Esoteric Christianity transcripts. P1, P2 and P3 are their source IDs. Capture dates do not establish original lecture dates. The speaker is referred to as “the lecturer”; original recording credits remain to be confirmed.

The first list records works named or alluded to in Brian’s three talks. The reading guide below distinguishes the newly supplied books and the exact passages checked for the course revision. These targeted readings do not imply full-book study. The Asita/Simeon check in GA 114 still comes from the supplied research dossier; the Essene and Mary passages in GA 148 have now been checked directly in the supplied 1950 edition. Links are reading destinations, not recording URLs.

Reading guide: passages checked in the supplied books

These additions clarify 17 lessons. Read the listed passage to explore a particular question; no full-book prerequisite is added. PDF and Markdown versions of the same work are counted as one source. Added background titles are distinguished from titles named by Brian.

Christianity as Mystical Fact

GA 8 · Andrew Welburn translation · Anthroposophic Press, 1997

Ch. 1, pp. 1–4 (PDF 25–28); ch. 6, pp. 101–104 (PDF 125–128); ch. 10, pp. 144–146 (PDF 168–170)

Background on initiation, Gospel perspectives and the movement from the Mysteries to Christianity. Added study background; not identified as a title Brian names in these three talks.

Cosmic Memory

GA 11 · Karl E. Zimmer translation · first paperback edition, 1976; translation ©1959

“Our Atlantean Ancestors”, pp. 42–56 (PDF 36–50)

Memory, language and comparing experiences. The edition also contains racial hierarchies and dated claims about peoples; these are acknowledged as source limitations and are not taught as current anthropology.

An Outline of Occult Science

GA 13 · supplied text title page: Anthroposophic Press, New York, 1922

Ch. II, printed-text markers pp. 16–30 (PDF 27–34); ch. IV, pp. 104–109 (PDF 80–83)

Clarifies the four members, the special meaning of “body” and spiritual cosmology. The upload label says 2011; the embedded title page says 1922. The Markdown is a reading aid checked against the PDF.

The Gospel of St. John

GA 103 · Hamburg, May 1908 · Maud B. Monges translation · revised edition 1962, second printing 1973

Lecture I, pp. 19–20 and 24–25 (PDF 21–22, 26–27)

Explains why Logos concerns creative spiritual origin rather than merely a spoken word. Added background from an identified John cycle, not a claim that Brian specified this edition.

The Gospel of St. Matthew

GA 123 · Dorothy Osmond and Mildred Kirkcaldy translation · fourth edition 1965, reprinted 1985

Lecture III, pp. 67–70 (PDF 69–72); lecture VI, pp. 110–116 (PDF 112–118); lecture VII, pp. 125–128 (PDF 127–130)

Checks genealogy, the two children, Temple convergence and the separate Jordan entry. The supplied file label says January 2000; its imprint says reprinted 1985. GA 123 VI–VII speaks of the Solomon child at twelve at transfer, while Brian later entertains larger age gaps; no exact harmonized gap is asserted.

From Jesus to Christ

GA 131 · Karlsruhe, October 1911 · Rudolf Steiner Press, revised translation 1973

Lecture VI (PDF 79–83); lecture VII (PDF 89–95); opening of lecture VIII (PDF 96–98); no printed pagination in supplied PDF

Checks physical form versus material substances, the “phantom”, and Buddha’s influence on the Nathan child. Resurrection remains a brief continuation question rather than a new course module.

The Fifth Gospel

GA 148 selection · five Christiania lectures, 1–6 October 1913 · D. S. Osmond with M. Cotterell · London, 1950

Lecture IV, pp. 64–84 (PDF 68–88); lecture V, pp. 85–98 (PDF 89–102)

Directly checks the pre-Jordan encounters, the Essene-gate question and the conversation with Mary. This supplied edition is five lectures, not the entire GA 148 collection.

The Book of the Bee

Solomon of Akhlat · English edition by E. A. Wallis Budge · internal title page 1886

Preface, PDF 6; introduction, PDF 10; ch. 37, p. 73 (PDF 78)

Confirms the prophecy text and its medieval Christian transmission. Budge edits/translates the work; he is not its medieval author. The filename’s 2021 label does not establish a new translation.

A title-label mismatch in the supplied resources

The upload named “Esoteric Christianity and the mission of Christian…” contains Art as Seen in the Light of Mystery Wisdom (GA 275), eight Dornach lectures, 28 December 1914–4 January 1915, first complete English edition 1984. Its PDF title/imprint pages 5–6 and matching Markdown were checked. It is not used as evidence for Christian Rosenkreutz or the two-child account.
