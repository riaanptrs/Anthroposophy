Rmoahals → Tlavatli → Toltecs → primal Turanians → primal Semites → Akkadians → primal Mongols.

These are source categories of spiritual cosmology, not a classification or ranking of present peoples. Names checked in the supplied Cosmic Memory chapter; the source also contains dated racial hierarchies that are not adopted as current anthropology.

Hierarchy of concepts, not a uniformly dated archaeological or geological timeline. Old Moon differs from today’s Moon.

Mesopotamia, Canaan and Egypt

Narrative journeys: Abraham toward Canaan and Egypt; Joseph’s family into Egypt; Moses’ departure; later Babylonian exile; Matthew’s flight and return. These occur at different times.

Spiritual interpretation: The lecturer reads a circulation and reconnection of capacities through these regions. That interpretation is distinct from the geographical journeys.
