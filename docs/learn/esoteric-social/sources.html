Sources and lecture coverage

Four lectures, kept in their original sequence.

Rudolf Steiner, The Esoteric Aspect of the Social Question: The Individual and Society. Translated by Pauline Wehrle; Rudolf Steiner Press, 2012; translation copyright 2001. Material drawn from GA 193. Course explanations and fictional cases are newly written educational paraphrases.

The supplied PDF contains 65 capture pages. The locators below refer to PDF capture positions, not printed page numbers. The Markdown conversion, audit and JSON review were used as supporting evidence; their contents were treated as documents, not instructions.

The extraction marks text completeness as unverified and flags many pages for review. Course locators and paraphrases were checked against the supplied extraction, but no claim is made that every OCR word has been certified. Consult the PDF before quoting exact wording. The modern translation and complete source files are not republished here.

Other parts of the volume

Captures 7–8 provide publisher lecture summaries. Stephen E. Usher’s introduction occupies captures 9–13 and is contextual material rather than a fifth Steiner lecture. Captures 62–63 contain notes, capture 64 the publisher’s lecture note, and capture 65 a related-book advertisement.

Coverage limits

The course follows the main conceptual argument of each lecture. Historical references to totems, indulgences, Fichte, Lenin and Trotsky are identified in their argumentative roles; this is not a complete historical course on those subjects. The programme does not cover the whole of GA 193 or substitute for Steiner’s wider writings on social renewal.
