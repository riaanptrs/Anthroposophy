How the path works

Follow the sequence or return to a question. Brief checks help you retrieve ideas; they carry no score. Saving notes and completion is optional and stays in this browser.

Each lesson explains its idea here, works through the relationships and connects them to the next topic. Try the two questions, then open their explanations to compare your reasoning.
