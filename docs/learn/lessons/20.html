Cosmic Evolution

Lesson 20 / 36 · Builds on: Spiritual Beings and Hierarchies

What do Old Saturn, Old Sun, Old Moon and Earth name in Steiner’s account?

What a world condition means

Lesson 19 connected spiritual beings with earlier conditions of existence. Here, a world condition means a phase in how the world is constituted: the forms of existence and activity that characterize it. Steiner describes the world itself as developing. Old Saturn, Old Sun, Old Moon and Earth name successive conditions in that proposed spiritual history, not places someone travels to. “Old Moon” therefore does not simply mean the Moon visible in the sky today.

A developmental sequence

A seed, a flowering plant and fruit are different phases of a developing organism. This comparison helps explain what a developmental sequence means; the phases are not separate destinations. It does not establish Steiner’s cosmic history or assign each plant phase to a cosmic condition. Read his names in order: Old Saturn, Old Sun, Old Moon, Earth. The table below introduces their associations with warmth, light, colour and life in the Colour account. These associations are starting points, not complete definitions of the conditions.

World development and individual lives

Keep world development distinct from an individual’s repeated lives. Cosmic evolution concerns changes in world-existence; reincarnation concerns a spiritual individuality living through different earthly bodies and biographies. Steiner places human development within the larger history, but the two sequences are not interchangeable. A person’s successive incarnations are not a journey from today’s Saturn to the Sun and Moon. Further study: light and darkness in the Colour passage

Light, darkness, past and future

The Colour passage also approaches past and future through light and darkness. Steiner relates light to earlier spiritual thinking becoming manifest and darkness to will still germinating toward a future. The distinction concerns what has become manifest and what is still developing. Reading darkness automatically as evil would replace that specific relationship with a different moral opposition. This is a further spiritual interpretation in the lecture, rather than something needed to identify the four world conditions or a claim about physical light alone.

Course explanation

Understand the idea

The locally available Colour lecture names Old Saturn, Old Sun, Old Moon and Earth. In its account, warmth, light, colour and life express changing activity of spiritual beings. These are successive conditions of development. “Old Sun” is not simply today’s astronomical Sun, and “Old Moon” is not simply our present satellite.

The selected passage approaches past and future differently: light carries thought and a dying past, while darkness holds germinating will and a future becoming. Steiner connects the cosmos with earlier spiritual activity and presents present human willing as contributing to future existence. Darkness therefore cannot be read here as an automatic synonym for evil.

The broader sequence commonly studied in Anthroposophy adds future Jupiter, Venus and Vulcan. This is a labelled orientation to Steiner’s account in An Outline of Esoteric Science, GA 13, Chapter IV; that complete source is not locally supplied. Colour’s separate discussion of planetary passage between lives must not be mistaken for evidence of those future cosmic stages.

Cosmic stages name changing conditions of world-existence in Steiner’s account. They are distinct from destinations in space and an individual’s successive incarnations.

Everyday example
