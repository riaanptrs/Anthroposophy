Bees in the View of Rudolf Steiner

Study the hive as a living whole: love and cooperation, wax and honey, flowers and insects, and the human responsibility to care.

Follow the prelude and all eight main lectures in the supplied 1998 edition. The introduction, appendix, drawings and artistic afterword have their own place. Each lesson explains an idea before asking you to work with it.

Begin the course →

How to study

Allow about 20–30 minutes per lesson, plus optional source reading. Explain the idea, work through the example, answer the unscored question and return to your notes. Keep observations, Steiner’s explanations and your own conclusions distinguishable.

Observe flowers from a comfortable distance without disturbing nests or handling insects. Exercises ask for observation, drawing and reflection; medical and chemical passages are historical study.
