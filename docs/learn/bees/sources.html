Sources and complete page map

Rudolf Steiner, Bees, translated by Thomas Braatz, Anthroposophic Press, 1998, ISBN 9780880104579

The prelude is from GA 348; the eight numbered lectures are from GA 351. Hauk wrote the introduction and Adams the afterword. Hahn’s reported exchange appears twice. The English explanations and Brazilian Portuguese study text are original course writing, not a published Portuguese translation of the book.

The supplied PDF has 119 captures, usually containing two facing printed pages. A capture can contain the end of one section and the beginning of another. All locators below count PDF captures from one; printed-page numbers in the book’s index are a different system.

Sequential reading used the supplied Markdown and embedded PDF text, with separate left and right spread extraction to resolve reading order. Covers and drawing plates were visually checked. This does not certify every printed line or German inscription. Full uploaded books are not reproduced on the website.

Historical interpretation and present knowledge

The lessons attribute cosmic correspondences, group souls and spiritual physiology to Steiner. Current research establishes bee color vision, including ultraviolet sensitivity. His historical health advice and chemical prescriptions are studied as claims, with corrections beside the relevant lesson. Short-term success or later bee losses cannot alone establish the cause predicted by a historical warning.

  • CDC Botulism Prevention — Supports the no-honey guidance for infants younger than one year.
  • AAAAI Stinging Insect Allergy — Explains sensitization and potentially fatal anaphylaxis; repeated exposure is not a self-immunization exercise.
  • Hempel de Ibarra Vorobyev and Menzel 2014 Honeybee colour vision — Research review of honeybee colour vision and ultraviolet sensitivity; abstract and indexed description consulted.
  • USDA ARS American Foulbrood Disease — Identifies the bacterial cause and transmission of American foulbrood.

These references support the named comparisons; they do not verify every historical or spiritual assertion in the book. Consulted 19 October 2026.
