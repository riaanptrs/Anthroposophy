Lesson 4 / 23

Queen workers and drones in cosmic time

Follow Steiner’s proposed connection between development time and Sun and Earth.

Understand the idea

Steiner compares the queen’s approximately sixteen days of development, the worker’s twenty-one and the drone’s longer development. In his interpretation, a queen remains closest to solar forces; a worker completes a solar formative cycle; a drone comes more under earthly influence. Time is treated as a quality of becoming, not simply a duration.

He connects these differences with reproduction and the mating flight. Fertilized eggs can become females and unfertilized eggs males, but he explains that relationship through the polarity of Sun and Earth. The same polarity is then used to describe the bees’ affinity with the queen and their response to a new queen.

Work through an example

A timeline of 16, 21 and about 24 days summarizes developmental intervals. Adding “solar” and “earthly” creates a second, interpretive layer.

Keep this distinction

These are egg-to-adult intervals, not ages of sexual maturity. Solar rotation varies with latitude; Steiner’s twenty-one-day correspondence is not an established astronomical or developmental explanation.
