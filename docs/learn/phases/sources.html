Interpretation, historical claims and educational adaptation

The course attributes the phase theory, spiritual individuality, reincarnation and physiology to the author or the school discussed. It also explains the book’s problematic claims about gender, sexual orientation, illness and social groups without adopting them as present facts. Homosexuality is not a disorder or a failure to mature. Menopause analogies, age stereotypes and spiritual interpretations cannot diagnose an individual.

Accounts of Freud, Adler, Jung, Rogers, Maslow, Frankl, Assagioli, Schiller, Goethe and Steiner are mediated by Lievegoed. Their original works have not all been independently rechecked for this course. Explanations, examples and Portuguese study text are original course writing, not a published translation of the book. The course does not reproduce the full upload or train practitioners in therapy.
