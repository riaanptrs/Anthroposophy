Claims and adaptations

The four images of the human being, age rhythms, spiritual individuality and developmental interpretations are presented as Lievegoed’s framework. Accounts of Bühler, Guardini, Moers, Schiller and Steiner are mediated by this book; their original works have not been newly verified for this course. We avoid exact quotations where OCR uncertainty would require additional checking.

Exercises, fictional cases, diagrams, the workbook and synthesis are original educational adaptations. Historical physiological statements, diagnostic age labels, gender roles and political judgements are not adopted as current medical or social facts. Captures 27, 32, 35, 46 and 51–53 particularly need this distinction.
