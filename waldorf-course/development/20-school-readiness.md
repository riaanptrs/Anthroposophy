## Established lesson scope

[CONVERSATION] The course outline places this transition at approximately age six to seven. It proposes discussion of physical changes, Waldorf school readiness, the shift from imitation, and an emerging capacity for pictorial learning.

[STEINER] **Attribution recorded in the conversation; primary passage not yet checked.** The master specification identifies the change of teeth as a developmental threshold within Steiner's Anthroposophical understanding. It assigns relevant background to *The Education of the Child in the Light of Anthroposophy*, GA 303, and GA 293.

The outline explicitly cautions: tooth change must not become an absolute biological test of school readiness. The developmental timeline also cautions against making exact ages appear mechanically fixed.

## Educational movement

[INTERPRETATION] The established course organizes this threshold as:

**Imitation and example → emerging pictorial learning → an imaginative introduction to formal schoolwork.**

This is the bridge to Grade 1, where the conversation proposes imagination, rhythm, movement, and relationship as the way academic learning begins. The literacy sequence is picture → drawing → letter → writing → reading.

## Distinction to preserve

[CONVERSATION] The course's seven-year developmental picture is Anthroposophical. It must not be presented as a universally accepted scientific model of childhood. The available material supplies a proposed lesson scope; it does not supply a validated readiness assessment or a clinical decision rule.
