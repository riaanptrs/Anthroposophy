## Original established research dossier
## Grade 7 Waldorf Education
### Exploration, discovery and the expanding adolescent horizon

#### 1. Developmental picture

Around twelve to thirteen the young person is entering early adolescence.

The curriculum responds with themes of:

- discovery
- exploration
- experimentation
- human physiology
- new technologies
- scientific inquiry
- global encounter
- individual initiative

This is not accidental.

The adolescent's own inner world is expanding, and the curriculum gives corresponding pictures of humanity **breaking boundaries**.

---

## 2. Renaissance, Reformation and exploration

Grade 7 history traditionally covers the transition from medieval to modern consciousness.

Contemporary curriculum includes:

- Renaissance
- humanism
- voyages of exploration
- invention
- trade
- Reformation
- Counter-Reformation
- religious conflict
- rise of nation states
- science
- colonial expansion
- consequences for colonized peoples [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/history)


The curriculum importantly stresses that modern teaching should not romanticize exploration: colonization, oppression and dispossession must also be addressed. [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/history)

That is essential for your course.

---

## 3. Physiology and health

Grade 7 nature study turns strongly toward the human organism.

Typical themes:

- digestion
- nutrition
- respiration
- circulation
- health
- hygiene
- relation between body and environment

Contemporary Waldorf science explicitly states that Grade 7 returns to the human organism, especially nutrition and health. [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/natural-science)

This makes developmental sense:

The adolescent is increasingly conscious of **having a body**.

---

## 4. Chemistry begins

Chemistry commonly begins in Grade 7.

Typical themes include phenomena involving:

- combustion
- acids and bases
- lime
- salts
- metals
- transformation of substances

The method remains phenomenological:

**observe exactly → describe → compare → derive concept**

rather than immediately presenting atomic theory as the primary starting point.

Contemporary Waldorf curriculum places chemistry from Grade 7 onward. [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/chemistry?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## 5. Physics becomes practical

Grade 7 physics shifts from broad phenomena toward everyday applications.

Questions include:

- How does a tuning fork vibrate?
- How does a mirror form an image?
- How does a thermometer work?
- What is an electric circuit?
- How does a pulley work? [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/physics)


The young person begins to ask:

> **How does this actually work?**

---

## 6. Mathematics

Common Grade 7 mathematics:

- algebraic notation
- equations
- negative numbers
- powers and roots
- percentages
- proportion
- practical applications
- geometry and geometric proof foundations

The transition is from arithmetic manipulation toward **symbolic thinking**.

---

## 7. Geography

Grade 7 frequently emphasizes cultural geography and global exploration.

Contemporary guidance suggests connecting the history of exploration with study of Africa and Asia, while stressing that the actual arrangement should be adapted to local context. [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/geography)

This is an important principle for our course:

> Waldorf curriculum should be developmental without becoming Eurocentric.

---

## 8. Foreign languages

Language teaching becomes more systematic.

Grade 7 retains lively oral work but adds:

- stronger grammar
- written composition
- vocabulary
- cultural studies
- reading
- dictation
- group work
- short assessments

Current curriculum emphasizes increasing structure while retaining variety. [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/english-as-a-foreign-language?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

#### Grade 7 in one sentence

> **The young person looks beyond familiar boundaries, while history, science and geography all become stories of discovery, transformation and human initiative.**
