## 2. Physics begins

This is a major curriculum event.

Typical Grade 6 physics includes phenomena from:

- acoustics
- optics
- heat
- magnetism
- electricity
- mechanics

The method usually follows:

**experience phenomenon**

→ **careful observation**

→ **description**

→ **discussion**

→ **concept**

rather than beginning with a formula.

---

## 3. Mineralogy and geology

The curriculum now reaches the mineral world.

Contemporary Waldorf science explicitly ties Grade 6 mineralogy to the child's increasing logical capacity and the “threshold of causality.” [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/natural-science)

Children may study:

- granite
- limestone
- basalt
- quartz
- ores
- crystals
- mountain formation
- rocks and landscape
- relationship between geology and human economy

The movement through the natural kingdoms becomes:

**animal → plant → mineral**

or:

**living relationship → life processes → structured physical matter**

---

## 4. Roman and medieval history

Grade 6 history traditionally focuses on Rome and the Middle Ages.

Contemporary curriculum emphasizes:

- Roman Republic and Empire
- roads
- law
- engineering
- military organization
- Christianity
- migration
- Islam
- medieval society
- feudal structures
- monasteries
- towns
- knights and political power

and specifically says children can now increasingly follow **rational historical causality**. [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/history)

Steiner's curriculum interpretation likewise contrasts Grade 5 story/biography with Grade 6 historical connections and consequences. Practical Advice to Teachers\_ (…

That developmental shift is central.

Grade 5:

> “What happened?”

Grade 6 increasingly asks:

> “What led to it, and what happened because of it?”

---

## 5. Geometry becomes exact

Grade 6 generally introduces formal compass-and-straightedge geometry much more strongly.

Typical work:

- perpendiculars
- bisectors
- triangles
- polygons
- circles
- constructions
- geometric laws
- increasingly exact drawings

The developmental movement is:

**beautiful form**

→ **lawful form**

Again: Grade 6 is about **structure**.

---

## 6. Mathematics becomes practical and economic

Traditional Grade 6 arithmetic often includes:

- percentages
- interest
- discounts
- profit/loss
- ratios
- currency
- business mathematics

This prepares the way toward algebra.

The curriculum source describes Grades 6–8 as the stage where algebra begins from practical commercial arithmetic, including interest. [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/mathematics?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## 7. Geography

The geographic horizon can now expand toward the whole home continent and then toward a systematic survey of the continents. [Waldorf Resources](https://www.waldorf-resources.org/en/vertical-curriculum/geography)

This includes:

- river systems
- mountain ranges
- climates
- vegetation
- topography
- celestial orientation

#### Grade 6 in one sentence

> **The child reaches for lawfulness: cause and effect, exact geometry, physics, mineral structure, Roman law and historical consequence.**
