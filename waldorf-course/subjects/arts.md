## Lesson 9 — Why Waldorf Uses Art in Academic Teaching

Critical point:

Art is not merely an extracurricular subject.

Teaching itself should be artistic.

Examples:

letters from images

geometry through beautiful construction

history through vivid characterization

science through careful observation and drawing

movement in mathematics

### Why art belongs inside academic lessons

[BOOK]

Steiner tells teachers that teaching itself should become artistic.

Writing should emerge from drawing.

Mathematics can be rhythmic.

History should be vividly presented.

Science should begin from carefully experienced phenomena.

He says explicitly that teaching should be permeated by artistic activity rather than addressing only abstract intellect. [unresolved conversation citation 16]
