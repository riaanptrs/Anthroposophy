# Understand Your Temperament!: figures and teaching tables

Reviewed 30 September 2026 against the newly supplied 97-capture PDF. All positions below are PDF capture indices and columns; printed folios are not visible. The new copy supplies readable original figures and tables, superseding the previous damaged-figure notices. Original illustrations remain in the private book; the course uses accessible tables and explanations.

The four source-based course tables are original teaching summaries: attention/perseverance (lesson 1), the psychological dimensions (2), the fourfold constitution (9), and the final body-type proposal (11). Semantic column/row headers, labelled focusable table regions and matched Portuguese versions support reading on small screens and with assistive technology.

The existing ten-pair table in lesson 8 remains an original set of discussion questions for all ten pairings. It is explicitly distinct from the author's exaggerated compatibility sketches. The existing argument map in lesson 11 and separate genetics context in lesson 9 are preserved.

The imprint at capture 4 acknowledges permission for illustrations from H. J. Eysenck's *The Structure of Human Personality* (1970). It does not individually identify every figure's provenance, so no more specific permission attribution is invented.
