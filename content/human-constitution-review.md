# Human constitution reference

Placement: a shared bilingual reference at `reference/human-constitution.html`, introduced from the home page and Theosophy lessons 0, 3 and 6. Lesson 3 is the primary curricular entry: life, sensation and formative organization. The reference supports all courses without adding a competing course or renumbering lessons.

## Source basis

- GA 13, Chapter II, [English Adams edition](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c02.html) and [German](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/German/GA013_c02.html): physical §§ 1–3; etheric §§ 5–10; astral §§ 10–11; I and transformation later in the chapter. The German original was consulted alongside the English reading references. Prose and examples are original rather than extended quotations from the translation.
- GA 9, [Chapter I](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA009/English/AP1971/GA009_c01.html): distinctions and regroupings; soul body plus sentient soul in the relevant astral grouping. Do not equate whole soul with astral body.
- GA 13, Chapter III, [English](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/English/RSP1969/GA013_c03.html) and [German](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA013/German/GA013_c03.html): the sleep account is attributed to Steiner and the diagram represents relationships, not measured spatial separation.
- [GA 108](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA108/English/Singles/19090118p02.html), §§ 14–18: mental images and the astral interpretation of the exercise.
- [GA 4 VI](https://rsarchive.org/Books/GA004/English/RSP1964/GA004_c06.html), §§ 4–6: percept, concept and mental picture. The connection to GA 108 is explicitly a teaching comparison.

## Illustration research and decision

Image search for “Rudolf Steiner physical etheric astral body diagram” returned mixed traditions and nested-layer images. Examples examined in the search results include [Fourfold Human Being](https://www.newfoodculture.info/HOLISTIC/2-fourfold_man.html) and [an energy-body illustration](https://note.com/sane_avocet5218/n/nde30efcab0f8). These were not used as doctrinal sources or copied into the course. Nested shells could imply anatomical positions, and some results introduce a mental body in place of Steiner's I.

Two original bilingual SVG schematics are generated by the reference builder: one person with four related questions, and a waking/sleep relationship comparison. Neither claims to depict clairvoyant appearance. Colours are explanatory choices, not aura colours. Each SVG has an accessible title and description; the comparison is also available in a text table.

## Links and preservation

The final build step links rendered terminology to language-matched sections, including ether body/life body and Portuguese variants. It preserves wording, source quotations, attributes, existing links and input fields. Existing linked text retains its destination; code, SVG text and document metadata are not rewritten. The separate editorial learning-review page is excluded. The reference itself uses its comparison table and jump navigation rather than repeated self-links.

Validation: complete build, existing site/passage/practice checks, plus `node scripts/check-constitution.mjs` for language destinations, anchors, all eligible mentions, repeated mentions, input/source-link preservation and repeat-build idempotence. Visual review covers desktop and narrow layout.
