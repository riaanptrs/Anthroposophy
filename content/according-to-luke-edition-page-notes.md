## Edition evidence

The new title and copyright captures identify the English edition more precisely than the older upload:

- **Title:** *According to Luke: The Gospel of Compassion and Love Revealed*, a cycle of ten lectures by **Rudolf Steiner**, with an introduction and descriptive outline by **Robert A. McDermott** (capture 2).
- **Publisher and copyright:** **Anthroposophic Press, © 2001**, Great Barrington, Massachusetts (capture 3).
- **Translator:** **Catherine E. Creeger**; book design and typesetting credited to **White Cloud Press** (capture 3).
- **ISBN:** **0-88010-488-0**. The cataloging block also gives `BP595.S894 L8513 2001` and control number `2001018172` (capture 3).
- **German source:** the copyright page identifies *Das Lukas-Evangelium*, a cycle of ten lectures in Basel, and **Rudolf Steiner Verlag, Dornach, Switzerland, 1955**. Its date phrase visibly reads **“15. bis 24. September 1909.”** The final number is **24**, not an OCR misreading of 26. This differs from the September 15–26 range in the existing GA 114 lecture chronology, whose final lecture is [26 September 1909](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GospLuke/19090926p01.html). Preserve the printed date when transcribing this page; do not silently substitute 26. The supplied captures do not resolve the bibliographic discrepancy.

These are normalized bibliographic facts, not a full transcription of the copyright page. The PDF creation timestamp, 30 September 2026, records capture-file creation and does not replace the visible 2001 book evidence. The upload does not supply the body of “About This Edition,” so no further edition history is established here.
