# Childs: sanguine, phlegmatic and melancholic portraits

Every Markdown capture 031–046 was read in full and compared with its matching native JPEG. These sixteen captures contain complete chapters 4–6 of Gilbert Childs’s *Understand Your Temperament!*, not lectures by Rudolf Steiner. The PDF uses reflowed three-column views; capture numbers are not printed folios, which are not visible. The new 97-capture source supersedes the earlier 239-page extraction and its reported gaps. No assigned capture is blank, and no text gap is indicated in these chapters. Short chapter endings leave columns empty normally.

The author/title were also visually checked at capture 003. Capture 004 explicitly records “Published by Rudolf Steiner Press 2012,” “First published in 1995 and reprinted in 1998 and 2004,” and “© Gilbert Childs 1995.” Publication year and copyright year are distinct here. The study guides attribute recognition, conduct, illness and handling claims to Childs; the existing course examples remain original fictional adaptations, not evidence for those claims.
