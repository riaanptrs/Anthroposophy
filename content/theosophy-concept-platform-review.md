# Theosophy concept-led learning prototype

Prepared 3 October 2026. This records the completed Theosophy prototype and the shared platform implementation. Other courses retain their existing teaching.

## Learning structure

The main course teaches without requiring simultaneous book reading. Its 23 lessons form five modules: the human being (5), soul life (4), individuality and destiny (3), spiritual worlds (7), and the path of knowledge (4). A twenty-fourth study unit synthesizes the whole course, with five connected concept clusters, a 44-term glossary, ten explanation questions, a practice overview and further study.

Each teaching lesson opens with a question and ordinary experience before developing Steiner's concept. Terminology, conceptual comparisons, linked relationships, an appropriate optional practice, reflection with revealable course notes, three to six takeaways and optional deeper study lead to primary-source references at the end. The main lessons contain no graded quizzes, scoring or required citation tasks. Their concepts and practices also have standalone pages with teaching-context links.

The four main areas are Learn, Explore Concepts, Practice and Read Steiner. The homepage provides beginner, course and concept entry paths. Learn discovers all 19 existing collections. The 36-lesson Foundations sequence retains its teaching and lesson URLs at a separate contents page. Research and Themes remain available through supporting navigation.

## Primary source and content integrity

The primary text is Elizabeth Douglas Shields's 1910 English translation of the third German edition of *Theosophy*, in the supplied Delhi Open Books reissue. The digital publication year is unstated. Its 94-capture source ledger, nine notes and four source chapters remain unchanged. New authoring used the supplied Markdown and the existing source audits; this revision does not claim a fresh visual inspection of every PDF image. The 1971 Monges/Church translation and thirteen addenda remain a distinctly attributed parallel and historical source. Capture locators are not represented as established printed pagination.

The curriculum covers every original reading context and all substantive chapter captures, 13–92. Among the preserved distinctions are sentient body versus sentient soul, the astral grouping, both sevenfold constitution accounts, memory versus acquired capacity, reincarnation versus karma, purification versus karmic relationships, interpenetrating soul regions versus spiritual regions and kingdoms, independent living archetypes, independent Folk Spirit and Spirit of the Age, all three aura aspects, and understanding versus assent versus spiritual investigation. Ordinary observations, Steiner's spiritual account and course adaptations have distinct presentation. The thirteen optional exercises are identified as course adaptations rather than invented quotations or promises of spiritual attainment.
