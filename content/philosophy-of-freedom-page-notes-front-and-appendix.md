## Translation distinctions used in the course

- **Thinking / thought:** an activity and a content or product of that activity; a stored phrase is not the activity of grasping its connection.
- **Percept / perception:** the observed content and the process of perceiving. The book's percept includes inward contents as well as sense appearances.
- **Concept / idea / mental picture:** concept is grasped by thinking; ideas can be more comprehensive conceptual contents; mental picture or representation is individualized through an encounter. Wilson does not restrict the last term to visual imagery.
- **Intuition:** the inward grasp of conceptual content. For ethical action it becomes understanding of the content that can motivate a deed; it is not defined by emotional intensity or an unexplained hunch.
- **Motive / driving force:** the consciously apprehended reason or aim and the factor that impels the act. They coincide in the deed Steiner characterizes as free when an individually grasped ideal itself moves the will.
- **Moral imagination / technique:** forming a fitting concrete action and knowing how to realize it under actual conditions; neither alone proves freedom or ethical adequacy.
