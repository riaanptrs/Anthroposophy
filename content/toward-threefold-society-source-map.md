These are teaching divisions, not invented source chapters. The one preface landing page gathers readings 02–06. The first two readings orient the source and distinguish its voices.

## Use in the beginner course

Beginner lesson 35 draws on reading 06 and a short passage from the transcribed preface's final page. Its explanation connects cultural self-administration, economic associations and equal rights with the author's allocation of labor hours and conditions. It identifies the reviewed preface and the absent chapter bodies. Lesson 36 carries that coverage distinction into the final synthesis.

Seven short English selections and their original Portuguese study translations have Markdown page-marker and section locators. Their wording is checked against the supplied transcription; it has not been verified against PDF images.
