## What the source adds to teaching

Wilson distinguishes the physical foundation stone of 1913 from the spiritual foundation of 1923, and explains the dramatic relationship between the human addresses and the Cosmic Replies. Steiner's own note gives the social-spiritual purpose of the foundation in hearts and souls. The course distinguishes these voices.

Session 7 now teaches the three relationships, why their conditions of attention matter, the final appeal for warmed hearts and illuminated heads, and differences in translation. Its original responsive diagram maps the three practices to their common purpose. It is labelled a conceptual relationship diagram, not an anatomical chart, breathing sequence or a historical reproduction of the copper dodecahedron.

Session 8 now gives readiness questions, a stable first-address excerpt linked to its complete reply, four worked learning sessions, a concrete application of the ending, and criteria for continuing or returning to study. These are explicitly original course arrangements. The booklet does not supply a complete seven-day historical programme or prescribe a universal duration or breathing technique.

## Place in the courses

The primary home remains Meditation and Inner Life, sessions 7–8, after purpose and phrase meditation, with preparation through the I/soul/spirit concepts and Practical Thinking. The study links directly to Theosophy and the Esoteric Christianity course for conceptual and Christian context. The existing Theosophy-to-meditation connection remains. Choosing this practice stays optional, and the meditation overview and chooser direct readers to the study and practice sessions.

The source has no step-by-step meditation pictures to import. The original diagram teaches the actual relationships; decorative photographs or an invented historical foundation-stone image would not supply instructions.
