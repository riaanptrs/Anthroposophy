## What the labels explain

| Label | Teaching meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Concept | Intelligible content grasped through thinking. A word alone does not demonstrate understanding. |
| Percept | Content encountered through observation, including inward content when attended to. |
| Knowledge / knowing | In Steiner's account, joining a percept with its fitting conceptual connection. |
| Mental picture / representation | An individualized concept retaining reference to a particular perceptual encounter. It need not be a vivid visual image. When observed, a representation can itself become an inward percept. |
| Experience | The connected range of particular encounters and conceptual apprehensions, expressed through individualized concepts. Numerous encounters alone do not supply its richness. |
| Feeling | The encounter's personal significance, expressed through pleasure, displeasure and related responses. Its occurrence is distinguishable from the truth of an interpretation attached to it. |
| Axis of knowing / science | A continuation of the lecture's inquiry framework, relating encountered contents and conceptual explanations. It is not a complete definition of scientific method. |
