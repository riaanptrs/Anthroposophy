It audits the available curriculum and the evidence that can be used for each introductory question; it does not claim that the repository contains every Steiner book or that this audit independently reverified every registered quotation against a new scan.

## Source status and gaps

`verified-local-excerpt` means the plan selects an existing registered passage whose author and locator are already recorded. It does not mean that a passage proves a metaphysical proposition independently. `reviewed-local-section-no-selected-excerpt` uses a real reviewed section without manufacturing a quotation. `historical-context-with-local-book-evidence` distinguishes ordinary historical context and autobiographical fragments from a complete independently checked biography. `partial-local-coverage-with-named-follow-up` means local evidence supports only part of the proposed topic. `missing-local-source-labelled-overview` requires an explicitly labelled introductory overview and a named source to obtain, with no claim that the repository holds that source.

The major gaps are concrete:

- **Biodynamics:** no dedicated local GA 327 agriculture source was found. Name *Agriculture Course*, Lecture 2 on the farm individuality, as a source to obtain. Theosophy and natural-world reference links provide background only. Do not invent quotations, calendars, preparations, recipes or agricultural protocols.
- **Social threefolding:** no local GA 23 source was found. Name *Towards Social Renewal / The Threefold Social Order* and its discussion of cultural life, rights and economic life as follow-up reading. GA 4 can inform freedom but cannot stand in for the social text. Do not project physical/soul/spirit divisions directly onto institutions or present a policy programme as an immediate inference.
- **Cosmic evolution:** Colour locally names Old Saturn, Old Sun, Old Moon and Earth and discusses future moral world formation. It does not supply the complete future Jupiter–Venus–Vulcan exposition. Name GA 13, Chapter IV, for that further account. Colour’s separate planetary-return discussion is not evidence for the future cosmic sequence.
- **Lucifer/Ahriman and the Christ polarity:** Colour offers limited Ahriman/light/darkness context, and Luke supports Christ’s place in Steiner’s account. Neither selected local reading fully supports a systematic treatment of the complete polarity. Identify the broader GA 13 evolution discussion and later dedicated treatments as follow-up; keep a concise attributed orientation distinct from the available local passage.
- **Art, architecture and eurythmy:** Colour provides substantial primary discussion of painting, colour perspective, spiritual arts and the Goetheanum. Koepke provides a secondary educational mention of eurythmy. There is no dedicated local GA 279 eurythmy book or comprehensive architecture source. Name *Eurythmy as Visible Speech* for further study; do not turn the overview into a movement, therapy or architectural-design protocol.
- **Medicine:** the available school-doctor contribution is by Walter Holtzapfel. It supports an introduction to an anthroposophical physician’s historical account, not attribution of all its claims to Steiner, nor coverage of the whole medical movement. Name Steiner and Ita Wegman’s GA 27 for broader follow-up. Historical pulse, breathing, sugar and iron claims must not become diagnoses or self-treatment instructions.
- **Biography:** local prefaces and Koepke’s quoted autobiography support limited historical and personal context. They do not supply a complete independent timeline or establish the validity of spiritual claims.

## Terminology and authorship safeguards

The beginner order must explain the relationship among concepts without collapsing different classifications. Body, soul and spirit describe three relationships; thinking, feeling and willing are activities; physical, etheric, astral and I form another account. Etheric concerns life and formative activity in Steiner’s terminology; it is not a synonym for physical energy or the chemical ether. Astral concerns sentience and soul experience, not merely emotion or mental imagery. The I is not interchangeable with selfishness or a passing thought.

The ninefold human constitution, its grouped sevenfold account, the fourfold account and the seven members described through transformation are related but distinct presentations. The grouped sevenfold account combines the soul-body/sentient-soul and consciousness-soul/spirit-self boundaries. In the transformed-member account spirit self, life spirit and spirit man concern transformations of astral, etheric and physical respectively. A single unexplained list would obscure the book’s distinctions.

The afterlife sequence begins with what death removes and what Steiner says persists. Soul-world purification concerns the relation of desires to bodily instruments, not punishment assigned by a course. Karma describes consequences and development; it must not become blame for suffering, a diagnosis of another person’s past life, or proof that every event has an identifiable moral cause. Reincarnation and worlds between lives remain attributed spiritual claims, with the book’s reasoning and its limits visible. Soul and spirit regions are not geographical floors or levels assigned to learners.

Imagination, Inspiration and spiritual Intuition are distinguished using Luke’s first lecture and the development source. Images, their intelligible meaning and participation in spiritual beings do not denote ordinary daydreams, sudden artistic enthusiasm and unsupported hunches. GA 4’s cognitive intuition and moral intuition belong to its argument about thinking and freely formed ethical motives; moral imagination and moral technique also have distinct work to do. They must not silently become GA 10/GA 114’s spiritual Intuition. GA 4’s final addition explicitly rejects treating later spiritual writings as deductions from that philosophical book.

Steiner describes cosmic evolution, spiritual hierarchies and Lucifer/Ahriman as claims about spiritual beings and influences. Reducing them to psychological metaphors alone would misrepresent him; presenting them as demonstrated astronomical or clinical facts would also misrepresent the available support. Hierarchies do not establish a hierarchy of the worth of contemporary people. Lucifer/Ahriman language must not be used to classify living individuals or groups as evil. Historical racial, cultural and gender generalisations in the retained material need visible attribution and careful discussion rather than endorsement or silent deletion.

Passage cards must show the actual author and voice: Steiner; Koepke; Holtzapfel; Childs; McDermott; or a translator/editor when applicable. A represented child’s inner speech in Koepke is a literary interpretation, not a verbatim research interview. Brian Gray’s teaching can help explain GA 4 but must remain commentary. Bamford’s introduction and Steiner’s later GA 225 lectures likewise keep separate credits. Translation or volume credits do not convert an author’s contribution into Steiner’s text.

## Teaching and preservation implications

The beginner sequence can now lead the learning experience without hiding gaps or making source acquisition a prerequisite to every introductory lesson. Use a short faithful overview when a source is missing; show the named further reading, clarify that related local book links are background, and withhold invented quotation/page cards. Applications introduce each field’s guiding ideas and history rather than procedures.

Each lesson should move from its attributed passage or identified section to a short explanation, one distinction, a labelled everyday example, two gentle retrieval checks and an ungraded reflection. Checks assess what the reading says and whether its distinctions were understood; they do not grade belief in a spiritual proposition. Explain the connection to the next question explicitly. The questions about spiritual knowledge, death and evolution require the distinction between understanding an author’s account and independently validating its claims.

Retain all existing deep-book URLs, source guides, passage-study cards, constitution reference, diagrams and page-review records as the deeper study and research layers. Existing quotations retain exact wording and provenance. The new source plan changes their teaching order and prominence, not their identities or the source record. This subtask has written only this audit and the private 36-row plan; UI, routing and build integration remain separate work.
