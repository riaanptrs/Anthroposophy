# Foodwise OCR edition publication review

The user requested a separate Foodwise course and explicitly instructed publication after the PDF transfer limitation was disclosed. This edition uses selected readable OCR passages. It is a published course with a pending source-verification audit, not a visually verified transcription of the book.

## Delivered scope

- 39 English lessons and 39 Brazilian Portuguese counterparts in seven modules.
- Selected concepts from the opening and all twenty chapters, with capture locators and a chapter-to-lesson map.
- 23 bilingual ingredient profiles and eleven bilingual glossary entries.
- Fifteen multiple-choice questions with feedback and explanatory answers; optional explanatory questions in every lesson.
- Three original course recipes, clearly marked untested, with metric estimates, equipment, steps, doneness cues, substitutions, allergens, child participation and storage notes.
- Separate course catalogue entry and optional Nutrition/Biodynamics connections. The existing nutrition course files have no changes.

## Source limits

The PDF exceeded the transfer tool’s 32 MiB limit. No Foodwise PDF page was visually inspected. The 272-entry source audit records this consistently. Selected readable OCR informed the teaching; complete full-page or column reading is not claimed. Metadata, chapter boundaries and capture locators are provisional. Damaged menus, inconsistent quantities, uncertain temperatures and illustrations are excluded rather than reconstructed. No direct book quotations, full source text, original illustrations or book recipes are published. Current practical guidance is credited separately on the sources page.
