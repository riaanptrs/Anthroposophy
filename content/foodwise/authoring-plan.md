## Provisional modules

1. Food has a story / Os alimentos têm uma história: Cook’s journey, cultivation, civilizations, exchange and industrialization.
2. From living soil to food / Do solo vivo ao alimento: farming methods, food quality, community and access.
3. Understanding nourishment / Compreendendo a alimentação: fourfold humanity, transformation, vegetarianism and individual context.
4. Meet the grains / Conheça os cereais: plant form, grain groups, histories, processing and optional rhythms.
5. Meet the plant on your plate / Conheça a planta no prato: roots, leaves, flowers, fruit, seeds, legumes and nightshades.
6. Foods that transform a meal / Alimentos que transformam uma refeição: bread, sweeteners, dairy, oils, salt, minerals, herbs, stimulants and water.
7. The family kitchen / A cozinha da família: menus, practical techniques, atmosphere, budget, participation and responsibility.

These module names are planning choices from the guidance. Final lesson counts and assignments must follow the sequential source audit rather than be inferred from its provisional spans.
