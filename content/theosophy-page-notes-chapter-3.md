## Passage and interpretation checks

The eight existing Chapter III excerpts are retained and were independently rechecked against rendered PDF pages: lesson 10, printed 86 / PDF 114; lesson 11, 92 / 120; lesson 12, 104 / 132; lesson 13, 112 / 140; lesson 14, 122 / 150; lesson 15, 129 / 157; lesson 16, 139 / 167; lesson 17, 146 / 174. No longer extract replaces them. New bilingual close-reading notes connect each excerpt to the revised lesson and its immediate context.

Additional visual checks covered printed pp. 91, 113, 123–124, 128, 138, 143–144 and 153. The p. 143–144 sentence beginning “The essential thing is not” continues across the page break: it denies treating spiritual blue as physical blue, rather than affirming that equivalence. This is consequential for the aura lesson.

The companion addenda were also read where needed: No. 4 (animal group-ego), Nos. 9–11 (rest, purpose, Spiritual Word), No. 12 (the proposed pre-birth will-like orientation), and No. 13 (aura method and distinction from Benedikt’s physically investigated phenomena). Addendum 8 supplies an optional cross-reference for perceptive training, whose primary course home is Chapter IV.

Remaining interpretive limits: the initial accounts of higher regions 5–7 are partly collective, and do not supply three separate detailed mechanisms; this is made explicit rather than filled in. Spatial and time words are comparative in the author’s descriptions. The account’s supersensible claims are attributed to Steiner, with no classroom requirement to accept them or diagnose another person’s spiritual history.
