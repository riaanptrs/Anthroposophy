## Classification rules

**KEEP** preserves useful research, source distinctions, credited excerpts, terminology and learning ideas. **REWRITE** changes teaching and references for the applied course and its canonical edition. **MOVE TO BACKGROUND** removes secondary framing from the compulsory sequence. **MOVE TO OPTIONAL STUDY** retains deeper source reading after an agricultural concept has been introduced. **MERGE** carries useful material into a new lesson/library/synthesis without duplicating primary instruction. **REMOVE AS DUPLICATE** applies to repeated presentation, not destruction of unique source research or browser records.

For the new primary course, the governing sequence is GA 327 agricultural question → source → explanation → proposed mechanism → example → modern context where useful → comprehension → connection. GA 136, GA 230 and Courtney must not determine the order.
