# Encountering the Self: transition, curriculum and school-doctor page notes

Reviewed 2026-10-01. Every Markdown capture from 53 through 64 was read, and every corresponding native PDF image was visually inspected. These are PDF capture positions, not printed folios. The images have three text columns, read from left to right; OCR occasionally mistakes the italic I for other characters and damages diagrams and word endings. The native images govern quotation and reading order. Hermann Koepke is the main author; captures 63–64 form the signed contribution of Walter Holtzapfel. Translation credit follows the volume's title and copyright evidence, not a separately identified translator for this contribution.
