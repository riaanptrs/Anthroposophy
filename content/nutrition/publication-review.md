# Nutrition reading-course publication review

The main nutrition course now teaches Rudolf Steiner’s twelve-chapter anthology through sixteen chapter lessons, an orientation and a final synthesis. Four denser chapters (1, 2, 6 and 7) have two lessons each. The course is learner-facing and self-paced, with no assumed child age group. Each chapter lesson explains the argument through four sustained concept sections, a short source excerpt, three multiple-choice checks and two descriptive prompts with model responses. There are 54 checks and 37 written prompts across the full sequence.

All 118 supplied PDF captures were read and recorded in a page ledger. All substantive chapter captures are assigned. The ledger distinguishes front matter, the editor’s introduction, the blank capture 19, source records, the lecture note and the final advertisement. Locators mean capture order, not printed book pagination. Lecture dates and GA references follow captures 115–116. The edition is Rudolf Steiner Press, 2012, with selection and translation copyright 2008. The source PDF fingerprint is retained in course metadata; neither full upload is published.

The sixteen selected excerpt captures (9, 11, 21, 25, 35, 44, 52, 59, 65, 73, 77, 86, 90, 104, 105 and 114) were visually checked against the supplied PDF. Wording also matches the private OCR after normalizing whitespace and line-end hyphenation. The course explicitly separates editorial framing from the lecture text and preserves differences between chapter 4 and chapter 9 nutrient mappings. Historical medical, racial and national-character claims are attributed and contextualized. Present-day comparisons cite NIDDK, NIAAA, NHLBI and CDC resources reviewed during preparation.

Public resources include the chapter sequence, source and glossary page, all-capture map, printable full course and Markdown study guide. English and Portuguese Learn, Read and Books directories link it; the Portuguese landing page clearly identifies the full lessons as English material. The previous six general nutrition teacher plans remain an archived supplement at `teacher-plans.html`, rather than the main course.

Validation: all 30 repository checks pass, including chapter assignment coverage, excerpt-source comparison, answer positions and model-response availability. Chromium checked all 23 course/resource/Portuguese-entry routes at 390 and 1440 pixels: no horizontal page overflow, missing assets or JavaScript errors. Empty, incorrect, retry and correct quiz states, written-response boxes, model reveal and lesson navigation passed. Answers work without JavaScript. No responses are automatically saved or transmitted. The full printable course produced a 54-page A4 PDF with answer explanations and model responses present.
