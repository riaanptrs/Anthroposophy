## Exact seven soul-region names

1. Region of Burning Desire
2. Flowing Excitability
3. Wishes
4. Attraction and Repulsion
5. Soul Light
6. Active Soul Force
7. Soul Life

The first three contain both forces in differing proportions; the fourth is inward sympathy; the higher three are outward/enlivening sympathy. Regions interpenetrate. The first description of5–7 is shared; do not invent independent mechanics. Purification later differentiates their attachment roles.

Spirit-land has a separate seven-region account:1 physical-form archetypes apart from life,2 unified life,3 soul-archetypes,4 coordination,5–7 creative impulses. In the human sojourn these become relationships, reverence, service, culture, enduring Self, mission and Life kernel. Do not exchange the two sevenfold lists.
