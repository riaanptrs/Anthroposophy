# Concept-platform content audit

Phase 1, 2 October 2026. This is a source and curriculum audit, not rewritten teaching. Only Theosophy is proposed for the first concept-led course. Existing course content, source banks and reading addresses remain intact.

## Scope and principal finding

The catalogue contains 16 course routes. Most are substantial book companions: author sections, selected passages and source assignments organize the learning sequence. They already contain original explanations, examples, distinctions, checks and synthesis work. The verified gap is a consistent, self-contained concept-learning route, rather than an absence of teaching or practical material.

The four proposed destinations can give those assets distinct purposes: **Learn** teaches connected questions without requiring an external book; **Concepts** explains terminology and relationships; **Practice** offers selected meaningful activities with their source and purpose; **Read Steiner** retains detailed reading commentary and edition-specific assignments. A practice need not accompany every concept. A source reading remains valuable without being the required first step in every lesson.

This audit examined all 16 catalogue course structures and representative actual generated lessons, including opening, intermediate and synthesis units where available. It also considered the existing 36-lesson beginner path, 28-lesson introduction and 24-lesson biodynamic course as reusable assets outside that catalogue's 16-route scope. Catalogue counts are not a reliable statement of current core curriculum: Freedom, for example, has a 16-lesson consolidated core with earlier split readings retained. The technical audit owns inventory and route corrections.

### Beginner teaching priorities

- Begin with an encounter: sensory access, a personal response and an intelligible relationship are different aspects of one experience. Introduce body, soul and spirit through this question before displaying constitutions.
- Explain physical organization, maintained living form and inward sensation separately. Ether/life body is Steiner's proposed supersensible organization, not electromagnetic ether, measured energy or an established result of observing growth. Soul-body mediates bodily sentience; sentient-soul names inward experience.
- Give the I, the three soul members and spiritual members their relational meanings. Consciousness-soul is not simply being awake; intuition here is not an everyday hunch; I is not selfishness.
- Preserve the **two different sevenfold presentations**: one joins pairs from the ninefold account; the other concerns transformation through the I. Fourfold, ninefold and these sevenfold schemes are related views, not interchangeable lists or a seven-stage causal chain.
- Keep episode/capacity, heredity/spiritual individuality and inward/outward consequences distinct. Reincarnation, karma and heredity answer different questions in the source. Steiner's confident conclusion remains attributed; ordinary biography or talent does not prove a prior incarnation.
- Explain independent soul and spiritual realities as claims, not merely poetic metaphors. The seven soul regions, seven Spirit-land regions and seven kingdoms are different classifications. Sympathy/antipathy are technical relationships; archetypes are living creative beings in the account, not just mental categories.
- Retain after-death release, experience becoming faculty, enduring Self, higher purposes, nature and collective beings, and all three aura aspects. Neither aura colour nor a child's conduct becomes a diagnostic observation exercise. Capture 74 asserts independently existing beings: the current ID 16 phrase “does not originate solely” weakens that assertion and needs a precise replacement in new teaching.
- Separate understanding a communicated account, assent and the reported perception itself. Knowledge training joins receptivity, coherent thought, feeling, purposeful action and freedom. Ordinary practice can examine these distinctions without promising spiritual perception or surrender of judgment.
- Keep historical anatomy, racial/developmental vocabulary and exceptional-talent arguments attributed and contextualized, rather than turning them into contemporary biology or measures of human worth. Harmonize Portuguese conceptual labels or show explicit aliases: current material uses both *alma senciente* and *alma sensitiva*.

## Provisional conceptual scope

The agreed provisional scope is **23 teaching lessons plus one final synthesis: 24 in total**, with a short uncounted orientation landing. The source analysis initially suggested 21 by combining the soul functions; review identified a stronger beginner progression with the three distinct soul functions taught separately, followed by transformation. This expands conceptual clarity rather than matching another course's count. It is a source-led scope recommendation for the later curriculum phase, not a commitment to equal module sizes or rewritten chapter headings. The introductory question of how to understand a reported claim should also be visible throughout, rather than withheld until the final module.

## Meaningful Theosophy practices and synthesis

These are possible course adaptations, not newly discovered Steiner exercises or requirements for every lesson:

| Learning purpose | Suitable activity and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Encounter and interpretation | Observe one familiar object or event; distinguish sensory details, personal response and an understood relationship. Revise one sentence where inference was presented as observation. This clarifies distinctions, not supersensible bodies. |
| Wish, reason and truth | Compare two reasons for a small decision, one serving a desired result and one addressing whether a claim is sound. Identify what would justify revision. No required confession or scoring of belief. |
| Episode and capacity | Use an ordinary learned skill, or a fictional example, to distinguish remembered occasions from a retained ability. The distinction does not establish reincarnation. |
| Action and consequences | Trace intended action, inward learning and outward effects in a fictional case. Preserve limits of knowledge; do not infer karma to explain someone's illness, hardship or moral worth. |
| Receptivity and judgment | Listen to a short explanation before paraphrasing it; then state an agreement, a reasoned objection and an unresolved question. Label this arrangement as a course adaptation of the source's training discussion. |
| Thought and willing | Choose an ordinary subject or modest action, follow a connected line of thought, and review the aim, reasons and actual result. Repetition is useful when there is a review question, not as a compulsory streak. |
| Claimed worlds and constitution | Compare a source model with an everyday illustration and identify where the illustration stops. Reconstruct relations using words or a concept map. No aura seeing, past-life identification, hearing spiritual music or reporting elemental beings. |

A course synthesis should explain **one person across embodiment, inward life, enduring individuality and knowledge**, using several important distinctions and one concrete example. A revealable model response should demonstrate reasoning rather than enumerate lists. Existing chapter maps, misconception pairs, explanatory examples and final unresolved questions provide strong material. The current final book-architecture essay remains useful optional Read Steiner work. Reflections can be spoken, mentally considered or recorded; neither quotation copying nor externally supplied book access is required.

## Waldorf coverage: present and bounded

Waldorf-related material is already present. `content/learning-lessons-25-36.json` introduces child development, Waldorf education and artistic applications; `content/learning-system-sources.json` records the relevant source limits. Encountering the Self's curriculum lesson provides practical work, language, music, poetry and movement, with later reflective understanding. Temperament courses discuss children and education, and Ancient Myths ends with an adult educational project. These are genuine reusable applications, not a missing entire topic.

The verified scope gap is **a dedicated, primary-source Waldorf foundation or teacher-training course**. Koepke's conversations and mediated quotations, later temperament commentary, an adult mythology exercise and brief beginner applications do not supply a full curriculum, developmental framework or teacher-training sequence. Some Steiner quotations are known only through Koepke; their original lectures were not independently inspected. Eurythmy appears in curriculum context, while GA 279 is further reading rather than a locally reviewed movement-training source.

This gap does not require another book for the Theosophy prototype. If Waldorf is chosen after the prototype, the foundational teacher lectures—GA 293, then the complementary GA 294/295—are appropriate candidates for a separate source review. They are recommendations for future acquisition and examination, not claims that those books have been read or that their educational effects have been established. The first prototype should not force child-education exercises into concepts whose actual question concerns human constitution or knowledge.

## Phase 1 conclusion

Preserve existing source-aware teaching and authentic practice. Build a new concept route from the full Shields source, with the book optional, ordinary activities purposeful, and dense classifications available as references. The provisional 23 teaching lessons plus final synthesis establish sufficient breadth for architecture and component work; detailed lesson allocation and authoring belong to the later ordered curriculum phase.
