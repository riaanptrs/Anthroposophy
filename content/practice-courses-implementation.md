# Practice courses and temperament consolidation

13 September 2026

## Published architecture

Eight primary bilingual courses. Practical Training in Thought adds ten lessons; Understanding Temperaments provides one twelve-lesson path drawing on the three existing companions. The 39 earlier temperament lessons remain at their original URLs in an optional source library. Existing notebook identities remain intact and are not automatically converted into completion of new lessons.

The Philosophy of Freedom remains a sixteen-step core with six optional exercises. Its thinking-observation, representation, moral imagination and individuality lessons link to relevant practice lessons; both new indexes link back to the philosophical argument. Exercises are not presented as proof of that argument.

## Sources and editorial decisions

Practical Thinking uses original workshop situations, explanations, small experiments and feedback. Its source companion is Steiner's 18 January 1909 lecture, GA 108, in the [numbered George Adams parallel edition](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA108/English/Singles/19090118p02.html). Paragraph references identify nearby discussion for comparison, not a claim that our exercises reproduce Steiner's numbered exercises. The observed habits and practical outcomes are distinguished from the lecture's spiritual foundation. This is not a transcription or a complete critical edition of the lecture; historical scientific examples are not adopted as current scientific explanations.

Sophia's supplied outlines and two public samples informed the emphasis on repeated reading and application. Its assignments, course-credit claims and submission service are not reproduced. Only those samples were reviewed, not all twenty Sophia lessons. Brian-inspired first attempts, worked explanations, changed conditions and revision extend the site's existing teaching method; no new recording or transcript is claimed.

The temperament core reuses reviewed original course explanations, with newly authored continuing cases and matched feedback. Steiner's GA 57 account supplies the four tendencies and educational approach. The supplied Mystery text contributes attention to individuality and indirect self-education. Childs supplies explicit commitments, workplace cooperation and relationships. Attribution and optional companion links preserve differences rather than imply that all three authors make every claim. Longer heredity, physical-recognition and spiritual-constitution discussions remain accessible in the source companions. A short historical introduction links to the [National Library of Medicine exhibition](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/exhibition/shakespeare-and-the-four-humors/index.html).

## Learning sequence

Practical Thinking: habit/reason; attitude; observation/inference; successive change; prediction; reconstruction; sustained attention; mental picture; proportionate conclusions; review.

Temperaments: person before type/history; four-member framework; choleric; sanguine; phlegmatic; melancholic; mixtures/context; learning; work; relationships; self-education; synthesis. Four tendencies are taught once and then applied. One returning garden case and source comparison replace three separate introductions and final projects.

Both courses use first attempt → explanation/source → three dated practice sessions → revised answer. Suggested 15–25-minute readings and short separate practice sessions are planning estimates, not measured completion times. Fictional cases are sufficient and personal disclosure is optional. Completion is a learner-controlled study mark, not an assessed credit or claim of mastery.

## Notebook implementation

The shared opt-in browser notebook now includes session1/session2/session3 alongside first/source/after on new lessons. Course indexes aggregate saved notes for that course and language, offer links back to edit, and export the course journal as text. Lesson export includes all six fields. Local saving remains optional, lacks device sync, and uses the existing storage namespace; older notes need no migration. No inference or grading of free-text responses is performed.

## Validation

358 static HTML pages, 168 lesson identities in English and Portuguese, and eight primary course cards. Build with `node scripts/build-all.mjs`. Check with `check-site.mjs`, `check-guided-study.mjs`, `check-freedom-route.mjs` and `check-practice-courses.mjs` in scripts.

Browser checks covered note entry, navigation to aggregated journal, reload restoration, language separation, deletion in both languages, opening explanations and export control. Test notes were removed using the lesson UI. English desktop and Portuguese 390-pixel layouts were visually inspected; the four-member table and notebook fields fit on mobile. These checks establish functionality and structural coverage, not learner-tested educational effectiveness.
