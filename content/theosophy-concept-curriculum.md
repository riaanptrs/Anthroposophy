# Theosophy prototype: conceptual curriculum

Finalized 3 October 2026 after the repository/source audit, four-area architecture, reusable component implementation and bilingual landing pilot. This is the authoring allocation; the main course is independent of required source reading. Its 23 teaching lessons and final synthesis arise from the source's conceptual breadth rather than a uniform lesson count.

| Module | Teaching lessons | Source basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The human being | Body, soul and spirit; Physical body; Life and etheric body; Sentience and astral body; The human I | Shields 1910, Chapter I |
| The life of the soul | Sentient soul; Intellectual or mind soul; Consciousness soul; Spiritual transformation | Chapter I |
| Individuality and destiny | Memory and individuality; Reincarnation; Karma and action | Chapter II |
| The spiritual worlds | Three worlds; Soul affinities; Death and release; Living archetypes; Life between lives; Nature and spiritual beings; Thought-forms and aura | Chapter III |
| The path of knowledge | Understanding and knowledge; Receptivity and equanimity; Thinking and willing; Freedom and initiation | Introduction and Chapter IV |
| Whole-course synthesis | Bringing the whole picture together | All four chapters |

Every teaching lesson develops its question through ordinary experience, Steiner's concept, useful terminology, progressive explanation, a real distinction, explicit concept relationships, appropriate optional practice, reflection/course notes and concise takeaways. References end the lesson. Seven soul regions and seven spiritual regions receive separate comparisons with their actual organizing principles. The source's full argument remains available in the retained 28-reading commentary.

## Semantic identities and authoring ownership

The authoritative ordered lesson identities and localized module introductions are in `theosophy-concept-course.json`. Each data file follows `concept-platform-data-contract.md` and the loader validates all edges, source references, reciprocal practice contexts and language fields before publication.

Constitution file: `theosophy-concept-constitution.json`, nine lessons in human-being and soul-life. Concept ownership: body-soul-spirit, body, soul, spirit, physical-body, etheric-body, sentient-body, astral-body, human-i, sentient-soul, mind-soul, consciousness-soul, spirit-self, life-spirit, spirit-man, spiritual-transformation.

Destiny/knowledge file: `theosophy-concept-destiny-knowledge.json`, seven lessons in human-destiny and path-of-knowledge. Concept ownership: memory, fruits-of-experience, individuality, reincarnation, karma, heredity, spiritual-knowledge, receptive-attention, equanimity, thinking, feeling, willing, freedom, initiation, intuition.

Worlds file: `theosophy-concept-worlds.json`, seven lessons in spiritual-worlds. Concept ownership: three-worlds, soul-world, sympathy-antipathy, purification, kamaloca, spirit-land, archetypes, life-between-lives, elemental-beings, folk-spirit, spirit-of-age, thought-forms, aura.

This creates 44 concept entries. Relationships may cross authoring groups and must name the relationship, without implying that every connection is a causal sequence. No additional concept identity is introduced silently. Exercises are chosen by conceptual fit; the course does not force an exercise or a spiritual experience into every lesson.

## Integrity and preservation

The source is the supplied Shields 1910 translation/digital reissue. The primary capture ledger remains unchanged. All source wording, selected passages and chapter reading identities remain in the reading layer; the new lessons are original course explanations. The 1971 Monges/Church translation and thirteen addenda remain separately credited parallel material.

The prototype does not equate bodily life with measurable etheric energy, the I with egoism, soul functions with personality types, karma with punishment or diagnosis, spiritual regions with physical floors, archetypes with human abstractions, aura description with personality classification, or equanimity with emotional suppression. Understanding, agreement and firsthand spiritual knowledge remain distinct. Ordinary exercises illustrate and develop conceptual distinctions without certifying supersensible claims.

Old routes, source banks and saved reading notes remain. New semantic study records are independent and do not import old completion as mastery of rewritten lessons. Other courses' teaching is preserved until the prototype has passed final verification.
