# Waldorf teaching revision

All 62 supplied topics now have original English and Brazilian Portuguese course
prose: 19 foundations, seven developmental transitions, nine grades, 13 subject
pathways and 14 parent questions. Each lesson has a clear outcome, three developed
explanation paragraphs, a worked example, a concept-specific question/model and
an application prompt/model. Each grade also has a four-row curriculum/purpose
table. The nine grades have three focused views apiece. The complete course has
196 pages, with 98 routes in each language; Home Life is now substantive.

The explanations develop the ideas in the supplied plans and dossiers. They are
course explanations, rather than purported quotations. They connect developmental
interpretation, learning need, method, content and the evidence of understanding.
Individual support and actual observation remain part of that teaching rationale.

Three short English passages and their contexts were checked in the identified
Steiner Archive GA 294 web witnesses for Lectures 1, 4 and 9. Exact wording,
translation credits, course translations and source-witness fingerprints are
recorded separately. The check concerns these passages and contexts; it is not a
verification of every original draft quotation or every claim in GA 294.

The original 62 Markdown files and their fingerprints are unchanged. Their
previous public English rendering, original anchors, provenance and qualified
attributions remain in optional research sections. Portuguese lessons contain the
complete translated teaching and explicitly link to those English research
records. The English research records are not silently represented as translated.

Reading-mark keys and the existing preference key are unchanged. Marks transfer
between language versions on the same browser. Search, reading-control feedback,
source explanations and course navigation are localized. Answer models and
excerpts are usable without JavaScript. Private uploads and full Archive lecture
copies are not added to the published files.

Validation: the site/course check suite, dedicated bilingual Waldorf checker and
browser review cover every route, language counterparts, concept-example-check
order, curriculum tables, credits, fingerprints, local targets, mobile layouts,
search, saved marks, storage failures and static answer access.
