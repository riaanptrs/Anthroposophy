# Higher Worlds: initiation, conditions and the soul organs

Reviewed from every PDF page 35–67 of the supplied `How to know Higher Worlds2.pdf` (108 PDF pages; edition copyright 2018 Dead Authors Society). PDF pages are screenshot sequence positions, not the original book's printed page numbers. Each screenshot contains three consecutive reading columns, followed left to right. Pages 34 and 68 were also read for context; their ledger entries belong to the adjoining review files. The full source remains private.

This section moves from the three initiation trials through practical character development and all seven conditions, then explains the claimed effects on the soul organism. Lessons 6–10 retain that progression. Chapter boundaries occur within screenshots: *Initiation* begins on PDF 34, *Some practical aspects* on 42, *The conditions of esoteric training* on 48, and *Some results of initiation* on 55. The etheric-body discussion begins on 67 and continues in lesson 11.

## Teaching checkpoints

Explain the three trials by the quality developed and source of guidance, then distinguish the two symbolic draughts. Reconstruct the seven conditions in order, including condition 4's spiritual premise. Map the sixteen-, twelve-, ten- and six-petalled organs with their claimed perceptions. The eight functions and six attributes must be complete, separately identified and connected with the author's explanation of coordinated development.

## Source handling

The PDF has no text layer. Its embedded images were transcribed by OCR, read column by column, and checked directly for the five selected source excerpts on PDF 36, 43, 49, 51 and 55. Line-wrap hyphenation was normalized for those excerpts. Some quotation marks appear as literal `e` and `f` characters in the supplied image itself; clean passages were selected rather than silently reconstructing damaged quotations. The intrusive fragment on PDF 43 is also present in the image and was excluded from the excerpt. Both uploaded Markdown files and the smaller PDF concern *The Essential Philosophy of Freedom*, so they were excluded from this GA 10 revision.
