## What this branch delivers

Scoped primary-goal teaching passes cover **108 lessons/topics**: Theosophy’s 23 conceptual lessons and synthesis, all nine Meditation sessions, all 12 Practical Thinking core lessons plus its optional forecast, and all 62 Waldorf primary topics. Each has a supplied worked example and a changed-case check with explanatory feedback in English and Brazilian Portuguese. Teaching is visible; the transfer question and answer are optional native disclosures. Nothing requires JavaScript or personal disclosure. Existing substantial explanations, concepts, source references, learning records and routes remain intact.

This is a substantive extension of an already developed course, not a claim that all existing prose was replaced. The current Theosophy course already explains the constitution, destiny, three worlds and knowledge in depth. The recurring gap addressed here is **reasoning through an application, then showing exactly what that application does and does not establish**. The cases deliberately avoid turning spiritual descriptions into diagnoses, empirical demonstrations or badges of attainment. They introduce no quotations or source locators.

Start the review with [Body, soul and spirit](../../docs/theosophy/lessons/body-soul-spirit.html) and [Portuguese](../../docs/pt/theosophy/lessons/body-soul-spirit.html), then [Karma and action](../../docs/theosophy/lessons/karma-and-action.html), [Living archetypes](../../docs/theosophy/lessons/living-archetypes.html), and [the synthesis](../../docs/theosophy/lessons/whole-picture.html). The synthesis connects a shared meal, acquired capacity and responsible revision while keeping reincarnation and initiation as distinct spiritual claims. For the second course, inspect [backward review](../../docs/meditation/03.html) and [Portuguese](../../docs/pt/meditation/03.html).
