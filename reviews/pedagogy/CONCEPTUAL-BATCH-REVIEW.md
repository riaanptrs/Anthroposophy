Existing substantial teaching, source assignments, quotations, navigation and checks are preserved. New material explains conceptual relationships within the main lesson, before assessment; no generic case/check block was appended.

## What changed

- Foundations replaces the third development paragraph in each lesson with direct explanation: grouping versus transformation; cooperation among members and regions; memory, capacity and consequences; different modes of cognition; and how educational, artistic and social ideas enter activity.
- Introduction develops the missing inferential steps in its existing prose: why the path needs both knower and world, why clever reasoning can defend preference, why release and assimilation differ, and why a developmental framework still requires observation of the individual learner. Five lessons integrate the new reasoning into an existing paragraph to retain the course's five-paragraph limit.
- Threefold Society explains cultural freedom versus tuition, practical judgment, administrative feedback, recurring needs, economic association through the bread supply relationship, and equal rights versus professional expertise. The absent main chapters are still not represented as completed.

## Two representative before/after samples

**Foundations 11 — the different constitutional maps**

Before: “When encountering a list, ask whether it separates members, groups pairs or describes transformation.”

After: “In the grouped sevenfold list, soul body and sentient soul become one entry, as do consciousness soul and spirit self: nine distinctions become seven entries through two pairings. In the developmental list, astral, life and physical members are transformed toward spirit self, life spirit and spirit man through the I’s work. One operation groups; the other describes transformation. Equal totals conceal different relationships.”

The earlier core lesson already named the lists. This replacement teaches the comparison in the development section instead of leaving the learner to perform it unaided. The Portuguese uses the course's existing *si-mesmo espiritual* terminology.

**Introduction 07 — soul development**

Before: “Thinking can also ask whether something is true or good even when the answer displeases us.”

Added explanation: “In the mistaken-accusation example, clever reasoning could defend the original irritation rather than correct it. The consciousness-soul distinction concerns the standard guiding thought: is the person seeking a justification for a preference, or allowing truth to revise it? The I brings these activities into an individual life. Development changes their relationship; it does not remove feeling or practical intelligence.”

This supplies the missing distinction between reasoning effectively and letting truth correct preference; the prior definition and example remain.
