# How to Know Higher Worlds: teacher review

29 October 2026. All **19 current English main explanations and both primary checks** were read individually. The retained English study activities and their three comprehension question/answer pairs were also read for alignment; this is not a new close reading of every retained detailed paragraph or source-book page.

The sequence makes sense: orientation and preparation precede initiation, conditions, organs, transformed consciousness and the Guardians; the appendix and synthesis help qualify earlier claims. Activities use source maps, comparison, fictional cases and reasoned questions. Completing them assesses understanding, not spiritual attainment.

**Twelve second primary checks are revised in English and Portuguese.** They require application, reconstruction or explaining the limits of an inference. Seven existing pairs are retained in context. No published explanatory paragraph, first check, retained activity or source body changes.
