# Philosophy of Freedom: 36 detailed studies

29 October 2026. All **36 current English explanations, examples, questions, answers and exercises** were read individually in order. They cover the prefaces, fourteen chapters, conclusion and the separate 1918 appendix. The progression makes sense: percept and concept precede representation and knowledge limits; the ethical account then distinguishes motive, driving force, imagination and technique before synthesis and other minds.

**Twelve question/answer pairs are revised in English and Portuguese.** The new cases require classification, reconstruction, evidence or limits of inference where a yes/no question previously disclosed too much. The other 24 assessments are retained in the context of their examples and exercises. Study 2’s comparison exercise no longer presupposes an omission in every passage selected. The teaching steps, examples and source references are retained throughout.

The core/practice route validator also passes. Mapping assertions verify the recorded map’s consistency; they do not freshly verify PDF wording.
- [Browser results](browser.json) cover every standalone study in English and Portuguese at 320 and 1280 pixels, with JavaScript enabled and disabled (288 cases), plus embedded chapter, workbook and print samples for studies 2, 21, 32 and 35 (96 cases). Questions, exercises, full steps where present, keyboard-operated answers, anchors and overflow are checked. All four delivery contexts for every study also receive static content checks.

This is a teacher review of the current English authored teaching and tasks, with bilingual revisions and delivery verification. It is not a fresh full comparison against the original books or a line-by-line review of all retained Portuguese prose. Existing source attribution and historical caveats remain; understanding is assessed through reconstruction and reasoned criticism, without requiring agreement. No learner trial or proof of mastery is claimed.
