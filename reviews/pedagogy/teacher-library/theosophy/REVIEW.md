# Theosophy: current main-course teacher review

29 October 2026. Reviewed the English primary explanations and both explained questions of all **24 lessons**, in their authored order. Revised questions, feedback and reading aids are supplied in English and Brazilian Portuguese using the course's existing terminology. This covers the concept course, not the separate 28-reading companion or a fresh verification of every source passage in the retained detailed reading.

The sequence makes sense: distinguish relationships within one encounter, develop the member vocabulary, examine continuity and consequences, introduce the three worlds and their relationships, then return to knowledge, conduct and synthesis. Its strongest explanations already separate a useful ordinary comparison from the stronger spiritual reality Steiner proposes. Those distinctions and the sequence are retained.

## Revisions

- **Seventeen second questions** now ask for application, correction of a diagram or inference, or a connected explanation. They replace questions that could too easily be answered with a leading yes/no response. Each has worked feedback. The first check remains unchanged in all 24 lessons.
- **Seven existing second questions are deliberately retained** because their changed cases already test a useful distinction. These are Consciousness Soul, Three Worlds, Death and Release, Living Archetypes, Thought-forms and Aura, Understanding and Knowledge, and Receptivity and Equanimity.
- **Three new reading maps** make dense distinctions visible without opening the optional source reading: parallel spiritual transformations; three reading groups within the seven interpenetrating soul regions; and Elementary Kingdoms versus named elemental beings and soul regions. They reuse the existing semantic figure/definition-list renderer and responsive style.
- **The I lesson** now distinguishes noticing an experience, the motive for action and the ability to carry it through. Making a mistake or struggling to change a feeling does not mean that individuality disappears.

The maps explain the account; they are not instruments for diagnosing people, detecting a spiritual member or ranking attainment. Source quotations and assignments, the study graph, old detailed explanations, notebook identities and navigation remain unchanged.

## Lesson-by-lesson decisions

| Lesson | Finding and action |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Body, soul and spirit | Retain the timetable distinction between inward activity and a relation that can be understood correctly or incorrectly. The revised case requires correction of a misread departure without denying the earlier feeling. |
| 2. Physical body | Retain organized material existence rather than a mere ingredient list. The new model-hand case distinguishes composition, working organization and the further question of living maintenance. |
| 3. Life and etheric body | Retain the distinction between visible growth and a real supersensible member proposed as its explanation. Ask learners to correct a photograph labelled as proof rather than merely answer whether the member is metaphorical. |
| 4. Sentience and astral body | Retain the existing four-role map and distinction between sentient body and sentient soul. A two-observer case tests mediation, lived sensation and differing feelings without assuming direct access to another's experience. |
| 5. Human I | Clarify self-awareness versus immediate control. The new question follows one shared reason into two different responsible expressions and distinguishes individualization from a private truth. |
| 6. Sentient soul | Retain the smell/pleasure/desire example. Ask the learner to explain what changes when permission is sought, rather than treating a changed action as erasure of desire. |
| 7. Mind soul | Retain ordinary route planning as thinking within soul life. The revised case adds the friend's refusal and requires reconsidering the aim after solving the means-to-end problem. |
| 8. Consciousness soul | Retain both explanation and existing changed-case check. The urgent caring responsibility already requires weighing facts and reasons rather than mechanical rule-following or emotional suppression. |
| 9. Spiritual transformation | Retain the distinct roles of spirit-self, life-spirit and spirit-man. Add a parallel-relationship map and a question correcting a false serial chain. Grouping labels remains distinct from transformation. |
| 10. Memory and individuality | Retain episode versus acquired capacity. The new reading case requires naming what remains effective and the extra premise needed to extend continuity across lives. |
| 11. Reincarnation | Retain the explicit observation → added premise → conclusion structure. The new question makes the learner identify the unsupported move from different abilities to a named historical former life. |
| 12. Karma and action | Retain inward fruits and effects in the shared world. The revised damaged-tool case asks for repair as well as learning, avoiding the inference that present responses are useless. |
| 13. Three worlds | Retain both questions. The second already requires correcting an invisible-physical-object picture of feeling, rather than merely recalling a definition of interpenetration. |
| 14. Soul affinities | Retain the source-specific distinctions among regions. Add a three-group reading map to reduce memory load. The revised conversation case asks how attraction, self-enclosure and attention to another's needs differ without assigning a spiritual rank. |
| 15. Death and release | Retain both questions. The shared-meal case already tests selective release, retained capacity and the limit of ordinary comparison; the second protects against pretending to know a particular deceased person's condition. |
| 16. Living archetypes | Retain both questions. The plant/gardener diagram case already tests cooperating aspects instead of exclusive containers for whole beings; the potter comparison keeps the stronger independent-being claim visible. |
| 17. Life between lives | Retain assimilation as active development rather than waiting. The revised case distinguishes a lost position from a retained capacity and avoids using outward success as a scale of spiritual development. |
| 18. Nature and spiritual beings | Retain distinctions among beings, manifestations and animal group-ego explanation. Add a vocabulary map and a question correcting unsupported assignments between similar terms or equally numbered lists. |
| 19. Thought-forms and aura | Retain both questions. The same-coloured-halo case already requires distinctions among outward act, motive, passing mood, disposition and the claimed spiritual appearance. |
| 20. Understanding and knowledge | Retain both questions. The vivid melody case already separates inner experience from a causal interpretation and understanding from agreement. |
| 21. Receptivity and equanimity | Retain both questions. The corrected-date case already shows steadiness with irritation present; neither automatic agreement nor emotional numbness is the learning criterion. |
| 22. Thinking and willing | Retain coherence versus true premises. The revised check joins enjoyment versus praise as motive with a changed closing time that requires a new plan. |
| 23. Freedom and initiation | Retain the reasoned-disagreement case in the first question. The revised completion case asks what study can record and why neither agreement nor disagreement establishes initiation. |
| 24. Whole picture | Retain the original bench explanation and Ana's existing changed meal case. The new second question requires a connected synthesis of constitution, fruits/consequences and understanding/investigation, while identifying a further spiritual claim the repair cannot establish. |
