# Start Now!: teacher review of all 38 lessons

29 October 2026. Read all **38 current English lessons**, including purpose, teaching paragraphs, suitability, preparation, guided steps, example, question and answer. The anthology’s seventeen-section order works as a study path. Practice selection remains separate from studying later chapters; the learner is not required to stack routines or claim a spiritual rank.

**Twelve question/answer pairs are revised in English and Portuguese.** New cases require reconstructing an instruction, distinguishing schedules or interpretations, tracing evidence or documenting source uncertainty. Twenty-six checks are retained, including the four applied questions revised in the initial library pass. All teaching, examples, practices and source readers remain unchanged. Start Now therefore has sixteen units with revised assessments across the combined review, not sixteen new revisions in this continuation.
