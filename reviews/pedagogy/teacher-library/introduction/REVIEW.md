# Introduction to Anthroposophy: lesson-by-lesson teaching revision

29 October 2026. All **28 current primary explanations and both explained questions** were read in course order. The seven-part sequence makes sense: orientation → human constitution → destiny → worlds → knowing and freedom → cosmology → applications and synthesis. It is retained. The detailed source-reading sections remain available as optional depth; this is not a fresh verification of the original books or a line-by-line editorial review of all that retained material.

## What changed

- One question and explained answer in **every lesson**, in English and Brazilian Portuguese, now requires application, comparison, correction of an inference or a reasoned synthesis. The first question remains a short conceptual check.
- Lesson 7 no longer asks about “both responses” without supplying them: both responses to the receipt are now in the question.
- Lesson 14 now assesses simultaneous participation through the chair-repair example, rather than testing an optional death panel in a lesson about the three worlds during life.
- Five primary explanations were developed: bodily/soul/spiritual contributions (4), sympathy/antipathy and soul formations (12), category/image/archetype (13), successive cosmic conditions and developing members (19), and an actual drawing-to-understanding teaching sequence (24). Seven paragraph positions were revised in each language. Five lessons also gain topic-specific section headings.
- Two already-published Portuguese corrections were preserved in the authoring file (13 and 20). The corrected wording attributes a claim to Steiner rather than making “must cooperate/develop” sound like a command.

The new questions are not additional belief tests. A learner can explain the account accurately and disagree with its grounds. Source quotations, locators, complete optional explanations, existing progress identities and course navigation are preserved.

## Individual decisions

| Lesson | Teaching finding and revision |
| --- | --- |
| 1. What Is Anthroposophy? | Retain the definition and personal starting point. The new case distinguishes an inner need to ask about freedom from grounds for a preferred answer. |
| 2. Who Was Rudolf Steiner? | Retain biography as context. The new question prevents the Goethe connection from being treated as proof of every later spiritual report. |
| 3. Spiritual Science | Retain the reader/investigator distinction. The learner must now state what understanding a report permits them to claim and what it does not. |
| 4. Body, Soul and Spirit | Make the three contributions explicit in the bell example, including soul as lived sensation and memory, not just emotion. Assess two listeners with different feelings and shared understanding. |
| 5. Four Human Members | Retain the concrete meal example and distinction between the threefold and fourfold maps. The learner now explains all four roles rather than identifying one term. |
| 6. Thinking, Feeling and Willing | Retain the change in a friend's stated need. Assess how all three activities can cooperate when the proposed help changes. |
| 7. Soul Development and Individuality | Repair the missing alternatives. The learner compares two fully supplied responses to evidence and distinguishes articulate argument from truth-guided revision. |
| 8. Reincarnation | Retain capacity versus remembered episode. A fast-learning child becomes a changed case requiring both an accurate account of the claim and recognition of alternative explanations. |
| 9. Karma | Retain inward change versus effects on another person. The new check requires repair and cross-life extension to be distinguished. |
| 10. Death | Retain desire versus its bodily means and attachment versus assimilation. The learner now compares attachment and a developed capacity, without treating the comparison as evidence of an afterlife. |
| 11. Physical World | Retain observation as indispensable. The two-visit plant case requires separation of observations, proposed connections and the partly unknown interval. |
| 12. Soul World | Define a formation and give a specific book-possession example. Assess how attraction and exclusion can coexist without converting technical vocabulary into a verdict about someone's character. |
| 13. Spiritual World | Explain why a category or remembered picture is different from the proposed formative archetype. Assess correction of “my favourite picture is an archetype”; preserve the limits of the painter analogy. |
| 14. Three Worlds Together | Retain simultaneous participation. Replace the question about the death panel with an explanation of bodily encounter, concern, understanding and action in one present activity. |
| 15. Why Thinking Matters | Retain the already-developed percept/concept account. The new missed-collision case tests whether a proposed cause is distinguished from what the spectator actually observed. |
| 16. Freedom and Moral Intuition | Retain the distinction between motive and outward success. Assess ethical aim, possible arrangement and workable means through an inaccessible meeting entrance. |
| 17. Developing Spiritual Knowledge | Retain ethical preparation as part of the proposed method. The learner now explains a revised teaching attempt after recognizing an unclear instruction. |
| 18. Imagination, Inspiration, Intuition | Retain the technical distinctions and their difference from GA 10 stages and GA 4 ethical terminology. The check requires correcting a vivid-image/confident-guess conflation. |
| 19. Human and Cosmic Evolution | Supply the actual sequence of members and conditions rather than leaving “earlier preparation” abstract. Explain beginnings of organization and warmth, not a finished modern body travelling between planets. |
| 20. Hierarchies | Retain roles and overlapping activity. The learner corrects a turn-taking summary by explaining Wisdom, Motion and Form as cooperating contributions in the assigned account. |
| 21. Lucifer and Ahriman | Retain independence/error and sense-existence-as-exhaustive as the selected GA 13 distinctions. The new comparison separates careful observation from a claim about all reality. |
| 22. Christianity | Retain event, spiritual effect and continuing human realization. The check identifies what an “example of kindness only” summary omits, without turning the ordinary comparison into verification. |
| 23. Development and Education | Retain the distinction between a general developmental account and one tutoring episode. Assess what to observe, adjust and check for two actual learners of the same age. |
| 24. Waldorf Education | Replace a vague “familiar activity” with corresponding points on a grid. Explain how activity develops a precise relationship and how a new point distinguishes understanding from neat copying. |
| 25. Biodynamic Agriculture | Retain whole-farm relationships before procedures. The new case traces additional animals through feed, manure, crops, soil and imported resources. |
| 26. Medicine, Art and Eurythmy | Retain the existing substantial account of three distinct fields. The new case separates a reported artistic experience, a shared philosophical aim and a treatment claim requiring its own grounds. |
| 27. Social Threefolding | Retain distinct tasks with interdependence. The donor case requires cultural judgment, rights and resources to be explained rather than merely naming the spheres. |
| 28. Synthesis | Retain conceptual dependencies rather than a chain of claimed proofs. The new task asks for two explained ideas, their connection in a repair lesson and one claim that remains unestablished. Alternative well-reasoned pairs are accepted. |

## Validation

- `node scripts/check-introduction-anthroposophy.mjs` passes: 28 lessons, seven parts, 74 bilingual course pages and the retained 132 comprehension questions, with provenance, navigation, locators and isolated progress.
- The new browser review covers all 28 changed units in both languages at 320 and 1280 pixels, with JavaScript enabled and disabled: **224 lesson cases**. It checks visible application questions, initially closed feedback, opening explained answers, overflow, correct entry and first/last course positions. See [browser.json](browser.json).
- All 56 HTML pages compare byte-for-byte with their pre-revision version after replacing only the primary-reading section with a placeholder. All source evidence, earlier detailed explanations, IDs, navigation and study hooks outside that section are identical.
- Authoring changes are confined to the Introduction rows. Other Foundations/Meditation records in the same data file are unchanged. Existing source assignments and source quotations were not edited; search indexes were refreshed.
- The older combined `check-foundations-clarity.mjs` still fails on an unrelated Foundations 1 home link pinned to a historical version. No Foundations page was edited in this continuation. This is reported separately from the passing course-specific and browser checks.

Browser execution checks delivery and usability, not learner comprehension or the truth of the spiritual claims. A classroom teacher should still listen to learners' explanations and use their errors to select the next example.
