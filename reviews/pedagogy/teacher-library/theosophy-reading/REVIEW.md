# Theosophy reading companion: teacher revision

29 October 2026. Read all **28 current English main explanations and both checks** in their authored order. These readings follow the book's four chapters, with orientation, inserted explanatory readings and synthesis; they are separate from the 24-unit concept course. New questions, explanations and comparison aids are supplied in English and Brazilian Portuguese. The optional detailed source-reading bodies remain intact and are not claimed as newly verified original-source evidence.

The progression is coherent: encounter and human constitution → memory, capacity, individuality and deeds → the three worlds, release and assimilation → the path of knowledge. The companion's stable numeric URLs are not its reading order. For example, `23.html` belongs after `03.html`, and `22.html` remains the final synthesis. That order has been checked in both the source model and the rendered course workspace.

## Improvements

- All **28 second questions** now require a connected explanation, comparison, correction or application instead of a short leading answer. The first check in every reading remains unchanged.
- Six main explanations were developed: the I and self-control (05), spiritual content and three spiritual roles (24), grouping versus transformation (06), the definition of soul formation (25), selective release and retained fruits (11), and Causal Body/Life kernel terminology (14).
- Three comparison tables are visible in the main text: the three spiritual terms; the different questions behind ninefold, sevenfold and fourfold descriptions; and the relationships distinguished by the first three, fourth and higher three soul regions. They use captions and scoped column/row headers.
- The tables and expanded prose explain what the questions require before learners reach them. Detailed source sections remain available for closer study rather than supplying a missing premise behind a closed disclosure.

This revision explains Steiner's claims without turning an ordinary example into their verification. It does not assess belief, spiritual attainment or the condition of another person's inner or after-death life.
