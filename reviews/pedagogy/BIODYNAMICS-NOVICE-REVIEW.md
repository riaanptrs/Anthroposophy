# Biodynamic Agriculture: complete novice review

Reviewed 18 October 2026 on `improve/biodynamics-novice-clarity`, from main `98c7eccc61cc25415d4aef4e5f2e2100dd6e8ccf`. This is the 24-lesson beginner course at `docs/biodynamics/index.html`, not the separate 18-reading Agriculture companion or 14-reading anthology. Those courses remain unchanged.

All 24 lessons received targeted English and Brazilian Portuguese improvements. Existing farm examples, source excerpts, historical qualifications, safety guidance, detailed comparisons, six part syntheses, fourteen practice entries and the final worked portrait remain available. The changes teach prerequisites and applications directly; they do not add public grading rubrics.

## Review method and scope

The complete rendered English course was read in learner order, including optional depth, practices and explained answers, with individual findings recorded before editing. Both short readings and answers were then read in Portuguese and English after generation. The four changed deeper explanations were reviewed bilingually; exact preservation checks cover all other deeper text. Six part syntheses and fourteen practice entries were checked and retained. This is an editorial novice walkthrough, not a study involving recruited learners.

The [chapter audit](biodynamics-novice-audit.json) records objectives, essential concepts, prerequisites, misconceptions, missing explanations, interesting connections, teaching methods, before/after paragraphs and explained questions for every lesson. The [source check](biodynamics-novice-sources.json) distinguishes fresh modern checks from unavailable historical witnesses.
