# Local continuation: concept alignment, Waldorf and Practical Thinking

These changes remain local on `review/pedagogical-redevelopment`, after the previously pushed `b25ea2b`. No further push, PR publication, merge or deployment is part of this continuation. The earlier remote branch is preserved.

## What changed and why

**First-course QA.** The earlier cases were compared with the central concepts and retained explanations. Most supplied a useful application, but three Theosophy cases chiefly warned about inference rather than rehearsing the particular spiritual relationships. They now directly teach:

- astral body / spirit-self, life body / life-spirit and physical body / spirit-man, distinguished from grouping a list;
- attraction and self-enclosure in the soul-region account, rather than a general kindness example;
- the Elementary Kingdoms and embodied kingdoms, distinguished from the unrelated seven soul regions.

The Foundation Stone case now supplies its actual threefold structural map and connects the fourth movement with it. The map is no longer left as an unspecified task for the learner. No new source assertion or quotation was introduced; these relationships were already explained in the course.

**Practical Thinking: all 12 core lessons plus the optional forecast.** The source explanations are substantial and were retained. Every lesson now includes a fully supplied new-case check with feedback on the misconception behind the tempting alternatives. The scheduling application now completes the calculation: sequential tasks of 15 and 10 minutes require starting at 9:35 for a 10:00 opening. Forecasting distinguishes declining raw counts from increasing rates: 8/20 = 40%, while 4/5 = 80%. Other cases separately test observation/inference, imagined transitions, causal inquiry, chosen attention, remembered/invented details, alternatives, prepared pauses and revisable conclusions. The four part syntheses retain their existing teaching; they are not counted as newly rewritten chapters.

**Waldorf: all 62 primary topics.** Every primary example and check was examined against its stated goal. Activity proposals were replaced with supplied situations, explanations of the relevant relation and changed-case feedback. This includes 19 foundations, seven development topics, nine grade overviews, 13 subject pathways and 14 parent questions. The broad explanatory paragraphs remain; physics, chemistry and natural science also gained more explicit subject mechanisms. The repeated generic “invent a classroom example” prompt was removed instead of counted as substantive teaching.

The nine grade overviews still contain broader curriculum tables. This pass teaches and checks their primary lesson goals; it does **not** claim to turn every curriculum-table row into a complete subject syllabus. Similarly, a coordination example is explicitly not a newly invented traditional eurythmy gesture. Detailed specialist technique remains a separate teaching task.
