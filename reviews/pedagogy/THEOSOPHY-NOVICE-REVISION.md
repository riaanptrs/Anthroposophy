## What the fresh pass found

The revised first readings and their explained questions were read in sequence in both languages. A thought’s inward occurrence is now distinguished before the learner might confuse it with truth; physical, living and sentient roles are usable before the fourfold list; spiritual members are defined before their transformation relationships; formations and affinities are introduced before their after-death applications. The later lessons reuse those distinctions rather than requiring retrospective repair by the glossary.

The pass prompted three Portuguese refinements: a clearer compound subject in lesson 2, removal of repeated organization wording in lesson 3, and use of senciente rather than sensação in lesson 6’s question. It also replaced lesson 15’s recall question with the meal/care distinction and lesson 16’s editorial question about source omissions with a plant/gardener transfer case. Lesson 7’s optional group-ego mention now includes its own definition, before the fuller treatment in lesson 18.

The questions remain optional. They ask learners to distinguish roles, identify a premise, revise a plan or explain a changed case; answers show the reasoning. The practical exercises do not promise spiritual attainment, diagnose others, or require declarations of belief.

This is an editorial simulation of a novice pass, not a user study or independent empirical validation of the spiritual teachings. Actual learner testing and renewed checking against the supplied original files remain open.
