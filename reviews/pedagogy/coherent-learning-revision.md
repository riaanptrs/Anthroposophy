# Coherent learning revision — 30 October 2026

Foundations is the canonical beginner course. The Learn page now presents one
clear starting point and seven subject pathways. The shorter Introduction
overview has seven parts; its 28 detailed topics remain available as an optional
source companion.

## Teaching scope

- Foundations: all 36 lessons in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
- Introduction: all 28 detailed topics in both languages.
- Concept-led Theosophy: all 23 teaching lessons in both languages. Its final
  course synthesis retains its separate review format.
- Total: 174 lesson pages follow question, experience, idea, relationship,
  distinction, application, understanding check, optional depth, and next lesson.

The clarity explanations and explained answers remain intact. Six Foundations
openings now provide a self-contained everyday case; all existing explanation
paragraphs remain in the lesson. Each Foundations lesson also has an original
transfer prompt. Earlier Foundations and Introduction commentary is linked on
separate optional pages, rather than restarting teaching below the checks.
Sources and existing study identities remain available under Go Deeper.

## Visual and navigation changes

Six original bilingual teaching maps cover threefold versus fourfold human
constitution, soul development, spiritual transformation, the death/rebirth
cycle, Imagination/Inspiration/Intuition, and the overall course landscape.
Their text alternatives, source references and qualifications distinguish the
account being studied from evidence for its supersensible claims. These maps
are course paraphrases, not quotations or diagrams attributed to Steiner.

The revision keeps the existing academy palette, typography, course artwork and
reading controls. Shared interface fixes prevent the authored catalog from
being flattened and beginner guidance from being hidden. Course endings lead
to the pathways. Language links retain commentary context. Cache versions are
refreshed across existing course pages to deliver the shared interface changes.

The three established homepage entries are now source-owned templates, keeping
the Portuguese default and explicit English entry reproducible. Windows path
handling, Waldorf frontmatter parsing and the English homepage's platform-area
recognition were repaired during build verification.

An explicit preservation ledger retains published deep links through fresh
builds. It also preserves the two existing Introduction 27 source annotations:
the older owner JSON describes an earlier Markdown-only capture, while the
published annotation identifies a subsequently reviewed 14-capture PDF. The
ledger records that prior review without claiming a fresh PDF audit.

## Verification and limits

The complete published tree is checked for links, anchors, unique DOM IDs,
single page titles, language partners, all nine ordered steps, next/previous
chains, optional depth, source preservation and study controls. Reports record
the baseline revision and exact results. The browser review covers six key
pages at 375, 768 and 1280 pixels, with no horizontal page overflow, plus chapter
navigation, optional source panels, unsaved note entry and course endings.

The Anthroposophist plugin informed terminology, attribution and bilingual
explanation. Existing source banks and original course data were preserved.
This revision does not claim a new audit of private source books.

Other specialist courses retain their course-owned lesson formats and teaching.
Older checks still encode previous page layouts, route inventories and stale
source hashes; the new coherent-learning checker validates this revision's
visible structure and source evidence explicitly against the recorded baseline.
